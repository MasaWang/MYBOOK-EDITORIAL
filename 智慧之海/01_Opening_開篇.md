# Opening｜開篇

## The Voyage Begins｜航程開始

Humanity lived with wisdom long before it learned how to build intelligence.

We recognized it in memory and judgment, in the instincts of living things, in the rhythms of nature, and in the sailor who learned to read the wind before the storm arrived. We called some of it knowledge, some instinct, some experience—and, at its highest human expression, wisdom.

Then humanity began to make intelligence.

At first, it lived inside machines. Then inside networks. Then inside models that could see, listen, predict, generate language, and reason with us.

For a long time, we approached intelligence through interfaces. We learned how to enter the machine's world—how to ask, command, search, and wait for a response.

Now something is changing.

Artificial intelligence is beginning to enter our world.

It is moving beyond the interface and into the physical spaces where human life unfolds. There, intelligence encounters a world that does not wait for a prompt.

A room changes even when no one is looking at a screen. Light shifts. Air changes. People arrive and leave. Energy flows. Conditions change from one moment to the next.

The physical world continues.

And once intelligence enters this world, it encounters something no interface can fully contain:

**life in continuity.**

A space does not begin when an interface opens. Life does not end when an interaction ends.

Sunlight moves.  
Air changes.  
People arrive and leave.  
Energy flows.  
Time passes.

**Life continues.**

Intelligence that enters this world must learn to continue with it.

This is where our voyage begins.

---

**Why an ocean?**

Because intelligence is ceasing to resemble a single machine. A machine has a boundary. An interface has an edge.

An ocean does not.

It has depth and movement, visible currents and hidden ones. It connects distant shores without making them identical. Every part of it is local, yet no part exists entirely alone.

Every wave is local.  
No wave exists apart from the sea.

Perhaps intelligence is beginning to acquire something of this character—not because machines are becoming an ocean, but because intelligence is no longer confined to a single machine, interface, or place.

It can move across machines and networks, enter spaces, respond to changing conditions, and participate in the environments in which human life unfolds.

This is the ocean we are entering.

Between wisdom and intelligence lies a question of what humanity can create—and what it should create.

Between intelligence and human life lies a question of how technology should belong in the world.

Between where we stand and the horizon lies an ocean.

This book is an attempt to navigate it.

Not to conquer it. Not to reduce it to a single model or architecture. But to understand its depths and currents, the forms that may emerge within it, and the shores toward which it may carry human life.

OceanAI began as a name for this voyage.

The name came before the map was complete.

The questions came before the architecture.

And the voyage continues because the most important question remains open:

**How can artificial intelligence enter our physical spaces without making us live inside complex AI systems?**

The answer will not be found in intelligence alone. It will require us to ask what intelligence is, how it should be organized, what must continue when conditions change, where responsibility remains, and ultimately what kind of human life technology should make possible.

Those questions lie beyond the shore.

The horizon is before us.

Let us begin.

**The Ocean Comes into View.**

# 開篇

## 航程開始

在人類學會建造智能之前，智慧早已存在於人的生活之中。

我們在記憶與判斷中看見它，在生命的本能中看見它，在自然的節律中看見它，也在水手於風暴到來之前讀懂風向的經驗中看見它。我們把其中一些稱為知識，一些稱為本能，一些稱為經驗——而在人最高層次的表達中，我們稱之為智慧。

後來，人類開始創造智能。

最初，它存在於機器之中。之後進入網路。再後來，它進入能夠看、聽、預測、生成語言，並與我們共同推理的模型之中。

很長一段時間，我們通過介面接近智能。我們學會如何進入機器的世界——如何詢問、命令、搜尋，以及等待回應。

現在，有些事情正在改變。

人工智慧開始進入我們的世界。

它正在越過介面，進入人的生活得以展開的物理空間。在那裡，智能面對的是一個不會等待提示才開始變化的世界。

即使沒有人注視螢幕，一個房間仍在變化。光線移動。空氣改變。人們到來，又離開。能源持續流動。條件隨著每一個時刻而改變。

物理世界持續運行。

當智能進入這個世界，它也開始面對一件任何介面都無法完整容納的事情：

**持續展開的生活。**

空間不會因為介面被打開才開始存在。生活也不會因為一次互動結束就停止。

陽光移動。  
空氣變化。  
人們到來，又離開。  
能源流動。  
時間向前。

**生活持續。**

進入這個世界的智能，必須學會與它一起持續。

我們的航程由此開始。

---

**為什麼是海？**

因為智能正在不再像一台孤立的機器。機器有邊界，介面有邊緣。

海沒有。

海有深度，也有流動；有看得見的洋流，也有隱藏在深處的流動。它連接遙遠的海岸，卻不讓它們變得相同。海的每一處都是局部的，卻沒有哪一處能完全孤立存在。

每一道浪都是局部的。  
卻沒有一道浪能離開海而存在。

也許智能正在呈現某些相似的特徵——不是因為機器正在變成海，而是因為智能已不再被限制在單一的機器、介面或位置之中。

它可以跨越機器與網路，進入空間，回應不斷變化的條件，並參與塑造人的生活得以展開的環境。

這就是我們正在進入的海。

在智慧與智能之間，是人類能夠創造什麼，以及應該創造什麼的問題。

在智能與人的生活之間，是技術應當如何存在於世界之中的問題。

在我們站立之處與地平線之間，是一片海。

這本書試圖航行其中。

不是為了征服它，也不是為了把它簡化成單一模型或架構，而是為了理解它的深度與洋流，理解其中可能出現的形態，以及它可能把人的生活帶向怎樣的彼岸。

OceanAI 最初就是這段航程的名字。

名字出現在完整的地圖之前。

問題出現在架構之前。

而航程之所以仍在繼續，是因為最重要的問題仍然敞開：

**人工智慧怎樣進入人的物理空間，而不讓人生活在複雜的 AI 系統之中？**

答案不會只存在於智能本身。我們還必須追問：智能究竟是什麼，它應當如何被組織，當條件改變時什麼必須繼續，責任應當留在哪裡，以及最終，技術應當為人的生活開啟怎樣的可能。

這些問題就在海岸之外。

地平線就在前方。

讓我們開始。

**看見海。**
