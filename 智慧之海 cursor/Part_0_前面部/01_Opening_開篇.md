# Opening

## I. The Voyage Begins

Long before humanity learned how to build intelligence, wisdom already lived in human life.

Wisdom grew through memory, judgment, knowledge, and experience. People learned from living things, from the rhythms of nature, and from generations of human experience. A sailor could read the wind before the storm arrived.

Then humanity began to build intelligence.

At first, it existed inside machines. Then it entered networks. For a long time, intelligence remained behind the interface. People had to enter the machine’s world—learning how to ask, command, search, and wait for a reply.

The interaction could be discrete. The world could not.

Now something is changing.

Artificial intelligence is beginning to enter our world.

It is moving beyond the interface and into the physical spaces where human life unfolds. There, intelligence faces a world that does not wait for a prompt to begin changing.

## II. Why an Ocean?

We see the ocean.

Tides return. Waves reach the shore. The sun rises and sets above waters that are never exactly the same. The ocean continues through change.

On a clear night, the stars appear upon its surface. They belong to a sky far beyond the sea, yet their light reaches the water. Each passing wave changes the reflection, while the stars remain where they are.

We see the ocean.

Its power does not lie only in its size, its depth, or its motion. The power of the ocean lies in what it makes possible.

Life takes form within what the ocean makes possible. Through life, intelligence emerged. Living things learned to sense their surroundings, respond to change, and find ways to survive.

We see the ocean.

Today, we are beginning to understand more clearly how intelligence can exist, learn, reason, and act. We are also learning how to build some of these capabilities into artificial systems.

But what we create enters a world that already exists. It must act within conditions it did not create, among lives and relationships that have histories of their own.

Then how should intelligence participate in our world?

This is why this book is called *The Ocean of Wisdom*.

The Ocean of Wisdom is not merely a metaphor. It is the sea of thought this book sets out to enter.

It does not call artificial intelligence wisdom. Intelligence expands what can be done. Wisdom asks what is worth doing, what should be preserved, and what deserves to continue.

Greater capability brings new possibilities. Those possibilities create choices, and choices carry consequences. To understand what we can do is not yet to know what we should do.

The question of wisdom begins there.

The ocean gives us a way to see these relations. Its waters sustain life, carry movement, and reflect a world extending far beyond its shores. What appears upon its surface is not always what lies beneath it, nor does the sea become everything it reflects.

The ocean remains within the life that emerged from it.

Return is not repetition.

## III. The Dawn of Continuity

**Dawn returns, but the world does not return to where it began.**

The light returns, but it falls upon a world already changed.

The sun rises. Light moves. Air changes. People arrive and leave. Flowers bloom and fall. Time moves forward.

None of this begins when an interface opens.

And none of it ends when an interaction ends.

What continues is not the same moment repeated, but a world carrying itself forward through change.

**The world continues through change.**

Thought has boundaries. A question may receive an answer, yet understanding does not have to end there.

**A single response has boundaries. Continuous Thought seeks to move beyond them.**

When intelligence enters this world, it enters something already in motion. It encounters what has already begun and what continues to change.

Intelligence should not end with a single response. It should continue—through understanding, action, and responsibility.

To continue in this world, intelligence must remain connected to what has already begun, understand what is changing, act within what continues, and remain responsible for what follows.

**Return without repetition.**  
**Change without rupture.**  
**Beginning within continuation.**

# 開篇

## I. The Voyage Begins｜航程開始

Long before humanity learned how to build intelligence, wisdom already existed in human life.

在人類學會建造智能之前，智慧早已存在於人的生活之中。

We saw it in memory and judgment, in the instincts of living things, in the rhythms of nature, and in the experience of a sailor who could read the wind before the storm arrived. We called some of it knowledge, some instinct, some experience—and at its highest human expression, we called it wisdom.

我們在記憶與判斷中看見它，在生命的本能中看見它，在自然的節律中看見它，也在水手於風暴到來之前讀懂風向的經驗中看見它。我們把其中一些稱為知識，一些稱為本能，一些稱為經驗——而在人最高層次的表達中，我們稱之為智慧。

Wisdom is knowing how to live well within a world that continues through change. It is not merely the accumulation of information, but knowing what to keep, what to alter, and how to act with measure.

智慧，是懂得如何在持續變化的世界中妥善生活。它不只是資訊的累積，也在於懂得該保留什麼、改變什麼，以及如何有分寸地行動。

Machines first extended human power, and later our capacity to compute and process information.

機器最初延伸人的力量，後來又延伸我們計算與處理資訊的能力。

Then humanity began to build intelligence.

後來，人類開始創造智能。

For a long time, we approached this intelligence through interfaces. We entered the machine’s world to ask, command, search, and wait for a response.

很長一段時間，我們透過介面接近這種智能。我們進入機器的世界，詢問、命令、搜尋，然後等待回應。

Now something is changing.

現在，有些事情正在改變。a

Artificial intelligence is beginning to move beyond the interface and into the spaces where human life unfolds.

人工智慧開始越過介面，進入人類生活展開的空間。


## II. 為什麼是海？

我們看見海。

潮汐往復，海浪抵達岸邊。太陽在海面上升起，又落下，而眼前的水從未完全相同。海在變化中持續。

晴朗的夜晚，群星映現在海面上。它們屬於遠方的天空，星光卻抵達了海水。每一道經過的波浪都改變了倒影，而群星依然留在自己的位置。

我們看見海。

海的力量，不只在於它的廣闊、深度或流動。海的力量，在於它使什麼成為可能。

生命在海所提供的條件中形成。經由生命，智能出現了。生命逐漸發展出感知周圍環境、回應變化，以及尋求生存之道的能力。

我們看見海。

今天，我們開始更清楚地理解智能如何存在、學習、推理與行動。我們也開始把其中一些能力建構進人工系統。

然而，我們所創造的事物，進入的是一個早已存在的世界。它必須在並非由它創造的條件中行動，並參與那些各自承載歷史的生命與關係。

那麼，智能應當如何參與我們的世界？

這正是本書名為《智慧之海》的原因。

「智慧之海」不只是一個隱喻。它就是本書試圖進入的那片思想之海。

它並不是把人工智能稱作智慧。智能擴展我們能做什麼；智慧追問什麼值得去做、什麼應當被保留，以及什麼值得持續。

更強的能力帶來新的可能。這些可能帶來選擇，而選擇產生後果。理解我們能做什麼，還不等於知道我們應當做什麼。

智慧的問題，由此開始。

海讓我們得以看見這些關係。海水孕育生命、承載運動，也映照著遠遠超出海岸的世界。出現在海面上的事物，並不總是海面之下的事物；海也不會因為映照萬物，就成為它所映照的一切。

海仍存在於從中誕生的生命之中。

回歸，不是重複。

## III. 持續的黎明

## III. The Dawn of Continuity｜持續的黎明

**Dawn returns, but the world does not return to where it began.**

**黎明再次到來，但世界並沒有回到最初的地方。**

Dawn is both an ending and a beginning. The light returns, but the world has changed. Night has passed through it. Air has moved. Water has circulated. Life has rested, awakened, grown, and changed.

黎明既是終點，也是起點。晨光再次到來，但世界已經改變。黑夜曾經經過，空氣已經流動，水體已經循環，生命休息、甦醒、生長，也發生了變化。

What continues is not the repetition of the same moment. The world carries what has happened forward into what happens next.

持續的並不是同一時刻的重複。世界將已經發生的一切帶向前方，進入接下來發生的一切。

**The world continues through cycles.**

**世界在循環中持續。**

**Space continues through change.**

**空間在變化中持續。**

**Life continues through experience.**

**生活在經歷中持續。**

**Intelligence continues through understanding.**

**智能在理解中持續。**

**Thought continues beyond its boundaries.**

**思想越過自身的邊界而持續。**

Continuity is therefore more than persistence. It is the capacity to carry what matters forward through change.

因此，連續性不只是延續存在。它是在變化之中，將重要之事帶向前方的能力。

**Return without repetition.**

**回歸而不重複。**

**Change without rupture.**

**變化而不中斷。**

**Beginning within continuation.**

**在持續之中開始。**

This is why dawn matters.

這就是黎明之所以重要的原因。

It does not mark the moment when the world begins again.

它所標誌的，並不是世界重新開始的時刻。

**It marks the moment when what has continued becomes visible again.**

**它標誌的是：一直持續著的一切，再次變得可見。**
