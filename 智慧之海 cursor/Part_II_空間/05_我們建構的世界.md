# 第五章｜智能進入空間

**核心問題：** 人的創造如何塑造我們生活與行動的空間？

**獨有思想內容：**

- 人透過建築、道路、設施和技術安排改變空間。
- 建構不只是增加物體，也使新的活動方式成為可能。
- 空間的設計會影響人的行動、相遇、合作與選擇。
- 建構空間既承載人的目的，也反過來限制或擴展人的可能性。
- 技術逐漸成為建構空間的一部分，而不只是放置其中的工具。

**本章思想成果：** 建立 Built Space｜建構空間作為人的創造與人的活動相遇的場所。

**概念邊界：** 不重複第一章的創造動機，不深入研究架構中的能力組織。

---

### 英文版

**Intelligence enters places where life is already unfolding.**

A classroom holds more than desks and equipment. It is a place where people listen, question, work together, and sometimes pause without doing anything that a system can easily recognize. A home likewise accommodates activities whose purposes change throughout the day. Light, temperature, sound, and access can support these activities or make them more difficult. When artificial intelligence enters such places through systems and devices, its capabilities meet lives already shaped by habits, relationships, and choices. The place has a life before the system begins to act.

What, then, would make intelligence useful within a space? Connected devices can exchange information, and automated controls can carry out familiar responses. Further possibilities emerge when a system can interpret relevant conditions and coordinate support for an activity. Yet sensing a change does not establish what it means, and choosing an action does not guarantee that it will help. Understanding intelligent spaces therefore requires attention to how information becomes judgment, how judgment produces effects, and whether those effects serve the people whose activities give the place its purpose.

### 繁體中文版

**智能進入的，是生活早已展開的場所。**

教室裡不只有桌椅與設備。人們在其中聆聽、提問、共同工作，有時也只是稍作停留，沒有進行任何系統能夠輕易辨識的活動。家也同樣容納著一天之中目的不斷變化的活動。光線、溫度、聲音與通行條件，可以支持這些活動，也可能使它們更難進行。當人工智能透過系統與設備進入這些場所，它的能力所接觸的，是早已由習慣、關係與選擇塑造的生活。在系統開始行動之前，生活已在場所中展開。

那麼，智能如何才能在空間中發揮有用的作用？聯網設備可以交換資訊，自動化控制可以執行既定的回應。當系統能夠解讀相關條件，並協調對一項活動的支持，更多可能性便由此展開。然而，感測到變化，尚不足以確定它的意義；選定一個動作，也不能保證它會帶來幫助。因此，理解智能空間，需要關注資訊如何形成判斷、判斷如何產生作用，以及這些作用是否支持了那些透過活動賦予場所用途的人。

## 5.1 From Tools to Intelligent Spaces｜從工具到智能空間

### 英文版

We often encounter technology through something we choose to use. We turn on a lamp, adjust a thermostat, or open a program to complete a task. Each action connects an intention with a particular capability. The surrounding place brings these capabilities together in everyday life: light helps us read, ventilation supports comfort, and doors allow passage while providing separation. Yet the work of arranging them can remain with us. We decide when each is needed and how their effects should fit the activity at hand.

Some of this work can be assigned to automatic systems. A timer can switch a light on at a specified hour, and a controller can adjust equipment when a measured condition crosses a threshold. These arrangements make useful responses possible without repeated instructions. Their suitability, however, depends on how well the chosen conditions correspond to what people need. A scheduled action may remain appropriate for months, then become inconvenient when a room serves a different purpose. The setting persists while the activity has changed.

An intelligent space introduces the possibility of relating these responses more closely to the situation unfolding within it. Artificial intelligence can contribute ways of interpreting information, recognizing relevant changes, and selecting among possible responses. Artificial systems organize these capabilities alongside schedules, control rules, and safeguards. Physical devices provide measurements and carry out actions. What matters at the level of the place is whether these elements work together to support an activity. Installing them in the same room does not, by itself, establish that relationship.

Consider a classroom moving from a presentation to a group discussion. During the presentation, visibility of a projected image may matter; during the discussion, students may need to see one another and read materials across their tables. A system could use a teacher’s instruction, together with available information about the room, to coordinate an appropriate change. The teacher would express the intended activity without having to translate it into every individual device setting. Intelligence would contribute by connecting the activity with the conditions that help it proceed.

Such support also extends the reach of a system’s decisions. An adjustment can affect everyone sharing the room, including people who did not request it. The system may misunderstand the activity or overlook a need that its measurements cannot reveal. Continuous assistance therefore requires boundaries: people need workable ways to correct an interpretation, change an arrangement, or retain direct control. In some situations, a familiar switch or a simple schedule may provide all the support required. Greater involvement is valuable only when it improves the activity.

The movement from tools to intelligent spaces changes the scope of what we ask technology to support. We begin to consider the conditions of an activity as a connected whole, rather than leaving every adjustment as a separate task. A space becomes more helpful when its systems can sustain these conditions while allowing people to pursue their own purposes. Its intelligence must therefore be understood through the relationship between its capabilities and the life taking place within it.

### 繁體中文版

我們經常透過主動選擇使用的事物接觸技術。我們打開燈、調整恆溫器，或開啟程式完成一項工作。每個動作都將一個意圖與某種能力連接起來。在日常生活中，這些能力共同存在於我們所處的場所：光線幫助閱讀，通風支持舒適，門讓人通行，也提供分隔。然而，安排這些能力的工作，仍可能由我們承擔。我們決定何時需要它們，以及它們的作用應如何配合眼前的活動。

其中一些工作可以交由自動化系統處理。定時器可以在指定時刻開燈，控制器也可以在量測條件越過設定值時調整設備。這些安排使有用的回應無須依靠反覆指示便能發生。然而，它們是否合適，取決於所選條件與人的需要有多相符。一項排定的動作可能連續幾個月都很適當，卻在房間改作其他用途時變得不便。設定仍然持續，活動卻已經改變。

智能空間帶來一種可能：讓這些回應更貼近場所中正在展開的情境。人工智能可以提供解讀資訊、辨識相關變化，以及在可能的回應之間作出選擇的方法。人工系統則將這些能力與排程、控制規則及安全防護機制組織起來。實體設備提供量測，並執行行動。從場所的層次來看，重要的是這些要素是否能共同支持一項活動。把它們安裝在同一個房間裡，本身並不會建立這種關係。

試想一間教室從簡報轉入分組討論。簡報時，投影畫面是否清晰可能較為重要；討論時，學生則可能需要看清彼此，並閱讀桌上的資料。系統可以根據教師的指示，結合可取得的房間資訊，協調適當的調整。教師表達的是接下來要進行的活動，無須再把它逐一轉譯成每台設備的設定。智能在此發揮的作用，是將活動與有助於活動進行的條件連接起來。

這種支持也擴大了系統決定的影響範圍。一次調整可能影響共享房間的每個人，包括並未提出要求的人。系統可能誤解活動，也可能忽略量測無法揭示的需要。因此，持續協助需要有邊界：人需要實際可用的方法，來糾正判斷、更改安排，或保留直接控制。在某些情況下，熟悉的開關或簡單的排程，便足以提供所需支持。只有當更多介入確實改善活動時，它才具有價值。

從工具走向智能空間，改變了我們期待技術支持的範圍。我們開始將一項活動所需的條件視為相互聯繫的整體，不再讓每一次調整都成為各自獨立的工作。當場所中的系統能夠維持這些條件，同時讓人追求自己的目的，場所便能提供更好的支持。因此，理解空間的智能，必須考察它所具備的能力，與其中正在展開的生活之間的關係。

## 5.2 What Makes a Space Intelligent?｜什麼使空間具有智能？

### 英文版

A building can be filled with devices while leaving people to arrange every condition of their activities. Lights, doors, and ventilation may each work well, yet someone must still decide how they should work together. Adding equipment expands what can be controlled, but the significance of that control depends on what happens within the building. An office becomes more supportive of work when its capabilities help people concentrate, meet, or rest. The question of intelligence begins with this relationship between what a place can do and what people are doing there.

A garden timer can open a valve at precisely the appointed hour. It may repeat this action without error, even after rain has made watering unnecessary. Its reliability is real; so is the distance between completing an operation and doing something appropriate. That distance matters because an action takes its meaning from circumstances beyond its execution. Watering serves a purpose through its relation to plants, soil, and available water. The successful movement of a valve cannot, by itself, tell us whether that purpose has been served.

Appropriateness therefore belongs to a relationship. A brighter room may help someone read while making a presentation harder to see. The same physical change can support one activity and interrupt another. A system making judgments about a place must attend to these differences, drawing on information that connects conditions with purposes. Those purposes are not simply waiting inside sensor readings to be discovered. They also come from people’s intentions, shared arrangements, and the needs of living things. Understanding a place requires recognizing what its measurements leave unsaid.

Consider a farm where the soil in one field appears dry. Whether to irrigate may depend on the crop’s stage of growth, expected rain, water availability, and work planned for that field. A system could bring some of these considerations together, but its account would remain partial. A farmer may know that planting plans have changed or that a measurement no longer represents the ground. A useful judgment must remain open to this knowledge. The reasons supporting an action may change even when the measurement stays the same.

This does not make elaborate reasoning necessary for every task. Where a condition and an appropriate response are well understood, a simple rule may provide dependable support. Artificial intelligence may help where such arrangements leave important needs unmet. Its value depends on what it contributes; complexity alone gives no reason to prefer it. Intelligence includes the ability to work within these limits. Sometimes the most appropriate response is to wait, ask, or leave a familiar arrangement unchanged.

A space is intelligent insofar as its systems can judge which responses fit the circumstances and revise those judgments as relevant conditions change. This claim must remain answerable to experience. We should be able to examine whether judgments fit the situation, whether actions help, and whether mistakes can be corrected. Intelligence acquires meaning here through the support it provides: a building more responsive to its occupants, a farm more attentive to the conditions of cultivation, and people better able to pursue activities they have reason to value.

### 繁體中文版

一棟大樓可以裝滿設備，卻仍讓人自行安排活動所需的各種條件。照明、門與通風設備或許都運作良好，但仍需要有人決定它們應如何配合。增加設備擴大了可控制的範圍，而控制的意義，取決於大樓裡正在發生什麼。當辦公場所的能力有助於人專注、會面或休息，它才更能支持其中的工作。關於智能的問題，便從場所能夠做什麼與人在其中做什麼的關係開始。

花園的定時器可以在指定時刻準確開啟閥門。即使雨後已無須澆水，它仍可能毫無差錯地重複這個動作。它的可靠性是真實的；完成操作與採取適當行動之間的距離，也同樣真實。這段距離之所以重要，是因為行動的意義來自執行本身以外的情境。澆灌透過它與植物、土壤及可用水資源的關係，才具有目的。閥門順利開啟這件事，本身無法告訴我們那個目的是否已經實現。

因此，行動是否適當，取決於它所處的關係。更明亮的房間可能有助於閱讀，卻使簡報畫面更難看清。同一種物理變化，可以支持一項活動，也可以打斷另一項活動。對場所作出判斷的系統，必須留意這些差異，運用能將條件與目的聯繫起來的資訊。這些目的並不是早已藏在感測讀數裡，只等著被發現。它們也來自人的意圖、共同安排，以及生命的需要。理解一個場所，需要認識量測尚未說明的事情。

試想一座農場，其中一塊田地的土壤顯得偏乾。是否灌溉，可能取決於作物的生長階段、預期降雨、可用水量，以及田間預定的工作。系統可以將其中一些考量結合起來，但它所掌握的情況仍然有限。農人可能知道種植計畫已經改變，也可能知道某項量測已無法反映田地的實際狀態。有用的判斷必須持續向這些知識開放。即使量測結果沒有改變，支持某項行動的理由仍可能改變。

這並不意味著每項任務都需要複雜的推理。當某種條件及其適當回應已被充分理解，簡單規則便可能提供可靠的支持。當這些安排仍無法充分滿足重要需要時，人工智能可能提供幫助。它的價值取決於實際貢獻；複雜程度本身，不構成優先採用它的理由。智能也包括在這些界限內行動的能力。有時，最適當的回應是等待、詢問，或讓熟悉的安排維持原樣。

一個空間之所以具有智能，在於其中的系統能判斷哪些回應適合當前情境，並在相關條件改變時修正判斷。這項主張必須持續接受實際經驗的檢驗。我們應能考察判斷是否符合情況、行動是否提供幫助，以及錯誤能否得到修正。智能在此透過它所提供的支持取得意義：大樓更能回應使用者的需要，農場的運作更能顧及耕作條件，而人也更有能力投入那些有理由珍視的活動。

## 5.3 From Sensing to Understanding｜從感知到理解

### 英文版

A quiet classroom may be empty, or it may be occupied by students absorbed in reading. A sound measurement can be accurate in either situation without telling us which one is occurring. The difficulty begins when we move from what has been detected to what it means. An observation gives us something to consider, but its significance depends on circumstances that may not be contained within it. The passage from sensing to understanding requires us to examine how that significance is established.

Every act of sensing is selective. A sensor responds to certain physical conditions, within a particular range, from a particular position. What becomes available to a system has already been shaped by these limits. Selection is necessary: no useful account of a place can preserve every detail of everything happening there. The question is whether the selected information is adequate for the judgment being made. A precise measurement may still omit what matters most, because accuracy within a limited view does not make that view complete.

Understanding develops through relations among what is observed, what is already known, and what people are trying to do. Information becomes relevant when it helps answer a question arising within an activity. Purposes guide which differences deserve attention; surrounding conditions help determine how those differences should be interpreted. A system’s account of a place therefore depends on more than incoming data. It also depends on the distinctions built into its design and the purposes communicated by those who use it.

Moving from these relations to a judgment requires inference. The system treats some observations as evidence for a conclusion, relying on assumptions about how conditions and activities are connected. Those assumptions may be reasonable without being certain. Their adequacy depends on whether they still hold in the situation at hand. A responsible account must preserve the difference between an observation and the conclusion drawn from it. Otherwise, an interpretation can acquire the appearance of fact, concealing the very assumptions that would allow it to be questioned.

Additional data can strengthen an interpretation, but it cannot guarantee that the right question has been asked or that the relevant purpose has been understood. People may need to explain an intention, correct an assumption, or change what they want. Such contributions belong within the conditions of understanding. They need not be treated as interruptions to an otherwise complete process. Recognizing a limit can itself improve a judgment, allowing a system to seek clarification or refrain from acting where its grounds are insufficient.

For intelligent spaces, understanding is therefore a situated achievement: an account adequate to a particular activity, under particular conditions, and open to revision. Its worth becomes apparent through the judgments it supports and their consequences for the people involved. A system does not need a complete description of life to assist it. It needs to distinguish what it has grounds to conclude from what remains unresolved, and to let that distinction shape how it acts.

### 繁體中文版

安靜的教室可能空無一人，也可能坐滿專注閱讀的學生。在這兩種情況下，聲音量測都可以準確，卻無法單憑自身告訴我們正在發生哪一種情況。困難始於我們從偵測到什麼，走向它意味著什麼。觀測提供了可供思考的依據，但其意義取決於未必包含在觀測之中的情境。從感知走向理解，需要考察這些意義如何成立。

每一次感測都有所選擇。感測器從特定位置，在一定範圍內，回應某些物理條件。系統能夠取得的資訊，已受到這些界限的影響。選擇是必要的：任何對場所的有用描述，都無法保留其中一切事物的所有細節。問題在於，被選取的資訊是否足以支持正在作出的判斷。精確的量測仍可能遺漏最重要的事情，因為有限視野中的準確，並不使這個視野成為完整。

理解在觀測、既有知識與人正在追求的活動之間形成。當資訊有助於回答活動中出現的問題，它才取得相關意義。目的引導我們留意哪些差異，周圍條件則幫助判斷應如何解讀這些差異。因此，系統對場所的理解，依靠的不只是持續輸入的資料，也依靠設計中採用的概念區分，以及使用者向它表達的目的。

從這些關係走向判斷，需要經過推論。系統將某些觀測視為支持結論的證據，並依靠一些關於條件與活動如何相連的假設。這些假設可能合理，卻未必確定；它們是否充分，取決於當前情境中是否仍然成立。對情況作出負責任的說明，必須保留觀測與由此推得的結論之間的區別。否則，一種解釋便可能披上事實的外觀，掩蓋那些原本能讓人質疑它的假設。

更多資料可以加強一種解釋，卻不能保證問題問得正確，也不能保證相關目的已被理解。人可能需要說明意圖、糾正假設，或改變自己的願望。這些參與本就是理解得以成立的條件，不必被視為對某個原已完整的過程所造成的打斷。認識界限，本身也能改善判斷，使系統在依據不足時尋求澄清，或暫不採取行動。

因此，對智能空間而言，理解是在具體情境中形成的成果：對某項活動、在特定條件下足夠適用，並持續容許修正的認識。它的價值，透過所支持的判斷，以及這些判斷對相關人們造成的後果而顯現。系統無須完整描述生活，才能協助生活。它需要分辨哪些結論已有依據、哪些問題仍未解決，並讓這項區別影響自身的行動。

## 5.4 From Judgment to Action｜從判斷到行動

### 英文版

A judgment can remain open to reconsideration without changing the conditions it describes. Action crosses that boundary. It gives a particular interpretation a consequence in a world shared with others. When an intelligent system acts, its understanding of a situation begins to shape the situation itself. This is why the passage from judgment to action requires a further justification: having grounds to believe that a change would help does not yet establish the authority to make it, or determine whose purposes it should serve.

Judgment identifies a possible response; action commits to one. Between them lie questions of purpose, authority, and consequence. Several responses may address the same condition while affecting people differently. Choosing among them requires more than predicting which will produce a desired change. It also requires considering whose activity is being supported, what else may be disturbed, and whether the system has been entrusted with that choice. The ability to act and the grounds for acting must therefore be considered together.

An intention must also acquire a form that the physical world can receive. Artificial intelligence may contribute a recommendation, but an artificial system must organize the means of carrying it out. Devices then act through materials, energy, and mechanisms whose behavior cannot be settled by information alone. Each passage introduces conditions that the original judgment may not fully contain. What appears straightforward as a decision can become difficult in execution. A system must preserve the connection between the purpose of an action and what its equipment can actually accomplish.

For this reason, issuing a command cannot be the final evidence of success. A command may be received without being completed, and a completed operation may fail to produce the intended benefit. These are different moments in an action’s unfolding. Observing the result allows the system to discover whether its expectation was justified. It also makes room for effects that were overlooked. Feedback matters because the world can answer a decision with something other than the outcome its maker anticipated.

The possibility of correction, however, should not make intervention careless. Some effects persist, and even a reversible change can interrupt an activity at a moment that cannot be recovered. Appropriate action therefore involves proportion: the extent of intervention should bear a defensible relation to its purpose, the available evidence, and the consequences of error. Where uncertainty remains, a limited adjustment or a request for clarification may be more reasonable than a comprehensive response. Restraint can be an active expression of judgment.

Intelligence enters the life of a place through this relationship between understanding, authorized action, and experienced consequence. Its contribution becomes real when an intervention supports the activity for which it was undertaken. That support must remain open to examination by the people affected, whose experience may reveal what the system’s measurements miss. The passage from judgment to action is complete only in a limited sense: an operation can end, while the responsibility to attend to what it has changed continues.

### 繁體中文版

一個判斷可以持續接受重新考慮，而不改變它所描述的條件。行動則跨過了這道邊界，使某種特定解釋在與他人共享的世界中產生後果。當智能系統採取行動，它對情境的理解便開始塑造情境本身。因此，從判斷走向行動，需要進一步的正當理由：有依據認為某項改變會有幫助，尚不足以確立作出改變的權限，也不能決定它應服務於誰的目的。

判斷辨識可能的回應，行動則將其中一種付諸實行。兩者之間，存在著目的、權限與後果的問題。數種回應可能處理同一條件，卻以不同方式影響人。要在它們之間作出選擇，除了預測哪一種能產生期望的變化，也需要考慮正在支持誰的活動、還可能干擾什麼，以及系統是否受託作出這項選擇。因此，行動的能力與行動的依據，必須一同被考察。

意圖也必須取得物理世界能夠承接的形式。人工智能可以提出建議，但人工系統仍須組織實現建議所需的方法。設備再透過材料、能源與機構產生作用，而這些作用無法單憑資訊決定。每一步轉換，都會引入原先判斷未必充分包含的條件。在決策層面看似直接的事情，到了執行時可能變得困難。系統必須維持行動目的與設備實際能力之間的聯繫。

因此，發出指令不能成為成功的最終證據。指令可能已被接收，卻尚未完成；操作也可能已經完成，卻未帶來預期的幫助。這些是行動展開過程中的不同時刻。觀察結果，使系統能夠發現原先的預期是否有根據，也使先前被忽略的影響有機會被看見。回饋之所以重要，是因為世界對一個決定的回應，可能不同於作出決定者所預期的結果。

然而，可以修正，不應使介入變得輕率。有些影響會持續存在；即使變化可以逆轉，也可能在無法重來的時刻打斷一項活動。因此，適當行動需要分寸：介入的程度，應與其目的、現有證據及錯誤後果之間具有可說明的合理關係。當不確定性仍然存在，有限的調整或尋求澄清，可能比全面回應更合理。克制也可以是判斷的一種積極表現。

智能透過理解、獲得授權的行動，以及人實際經歷的後果之間的關係，進入場所的生活。當介入支持了它原本要協助的活動，智能的貢獻才得到實現。這種支持必須持續接受受影響者的檢視，因為人的經驗可能揭示系統量測所遺漏的事情。從判斷到行動的過程，只能在有限的意義上被視為完成：操作可以結束，對其所造成改變的關注與責任仍然持續。

## 5.5 Intelligence in Everyday Life｜日常生活中的智能

### 英文版

The usefulness of intelligence becomes clearer when we consider the activity that continues after a system has done its work. A successful adjustment may establish favorable conditions, yet those conditions acquire their value through what people can do within them. Comfort can help sustain concentration; dependable access can make a place easier to use. The contribution belongs partly to what follows the intervention. To understand that contribution, we must look beyond the completed operation to the activity it enables, preserves, or changes.

This requires a longer view than the moment of assistance. Daily life brings activities together, often without a clear boundary between them. Work gives way to conversation, concentration to rest, and plans to unexpected needs. Support that fits one moment may become an obstacle in the next. Its value depends on whether people can carry their activities forward without repeatedly rebuilding the conditions around them. Intelligence becomes useful within this continuity when its assistance remains available while the purposes it serves are allowed to change.

Availability, however, must become something people can actually rely on. A capability that requires repeated checking may leave the practical burden of its use largely unchanged. People still have to remember what should happen, discover what failed, and arrange an alternative. When support is dependable, they can make plans on the assumption that certain conditions will hold. This dependence is not necessarily a loss of freedom. It can make further activities possible, provided that failures can be recognized and the arrangements remain open to adjustment.

The same support can also enter different lives in different ways. A shared room may accommodate people working, learning, or resting at the same time. Improving one condition for one person can make another person’s activity harder. There may be no single arrangement that serves everyone equally well. An intelligent space must therefore belong to a practice of living together, in which preferences can be expressed and differences negotiated. A system can help carry out an agreement; its capacity to coordinate equipment does not give it the authority to settle every disagreement.

Nor does assistance acquire value only by making activity faster. Learning, caring, and cultivating a garden can involve effort that belongs to the purpose of the activity itself. Removing that effort indiscriminately may remove something a person wanted to do. The relevant question is which demands prevent participation and which forms of involvement people wish to retain. Intelligence can support everyday life by widening that choice, allowing people to accept help with some aspects of an activity while continuing to shape and undertake others themselves.

An intelligent space becomes part of everyday life when its capabilities can be taken up within purposes that remain people’s own. Its success is visible in activities people can sustain, in difficulties they no longer have to organize their lives around, and in possibilities they can genuinely use. These outcomes require attention to experience over time. They cannot be inferred from a list of functions. The place provides conditions for living; the people who inhabit it continue to give those conditions direction.

### 繁體中文版

當我們考察系統完成工作之後仍在繼續的活動，智能的用處便更為清楚。一次成功的調整可以建立有利條件，而這些條件的價值，透過人能在其中進行什麼活動而顯現。舒適可以幫助人保持專注，可靠的通行安排可以使場所更容易使用。智能的貢獻，部分存在於介入之後發生的事情中。要理解這份貢獻，我們必須從已完成的操作，進一步看見它所促成、維持或改變的活動。

這需要我們將目光延伸到協助發生的瞬間之外。日常生活將不同活動連在一起，它們之間往往沒有明確邊界。工作轉入交談，專注轉為休息，計畫也會因突如其來的需要而改變。適合某一時刻的支持，到了下一刻可能成為障礙。它的價值，取決於人能否繼續展開活動，而無須反覆重新安排周圍的條件。當協助持續可用，而它所服務的目的仍能改變，智能便能在這種生活的延續中發揮作用。

然而，可用的能力還需要成為人實際可以依靠的支持。如果一項能力需要反覆檢查，使用它的實際負擔便可能沒有減少多少。人仍須記住哪些事情應當發生、找出哪裡出了問題，並安排替代方法。當支持值得依靠，人便能以某些條件將會持續成立為前提，安排自己的活動。這種依賴未必意味著自由的減少。只要失效能被辨認、安排仍可調整，它也能使更多活動成為可能。

同一種支持，也會以不同方式進入不同人的生活。共享的房間可能同時容納工作、學習或休息的人。為一個人改善某項條件，可能使另一個人的活動更加困難。未必存在一種安排，能同樣妥善地服務所有人。因此，智能空間需要融入共同生活的實踐，讓人能夠表達偏好，並就差異進行協商。系統可以協助實現共同的約定；它協調設備的能力，並不賦予它裁決所有分歧的權限。

協助的價值，也不只在於使活動更快完成。學習、照顧與園藝所包含的某些投入，本就屬於活動目的的一部分。不加區分地消除這些投入，可能也消除了人原本想親自做的事情。真正需要辨明的是：哪些要求妨礙了參與，哪些參與方式則是人希望保留的。智能可以透過擴大這種選擇來支持日常生活，使人在活動的某些方面接受協助，同時繼續親自安排與承擔其他部分。

當智能空間的能力能被人運用於自己仍能作主的生活目的，它便成為日常生活的一部分。它是否成功，可以從人能持續進行的活動、不再需要圍繞其安排生活的困難，以及真正能夠運用的可能性中看見。理解這些結果，需要關注一段時間中的實際經驗，無法僅憑功能清單推得。場所提供生活的條件，而生活其中的人，持續賦予這些條件方向。
