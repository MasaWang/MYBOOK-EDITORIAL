# 第六章｜空間是關係性的

### English

Artificial intelligence enters a world of relationships that existed before it and will continue beyond it.

Across the physical world, life unfolds through countless activities. People work, travel, gather, and explore. Other living things move, grow, and respond to their surroundings. Water flows, materials exchange heat, and machines operate among people and other things. These activities take place within physical space, where positions, structures, material properties, and surrounding conditions influence what can happen. Each activity encounters relationships that already exist, while also becoming part of the relationships through which the world continues to change.

The physical space that artificial intelligence enters contains positions and movement, but also structures, matter, physical interactions, and relationships that continue to change. This world is far richer than a map for navigation. Space and environment are closely connected, for spatial relationships develop within the conditions of the surrounding world. Our concern here is with how things exist, act, and affect one another within physical space. As artificial intelligence begins to perceive, interpret, and participate in these activities, understanding spatial relationships becomes essential to understanding what its presence and actions may make possible.

### 繁體中文

人工智能進入的是一個早已存在各種關係，而且不會因人工智能的出現或離開而停止的世界。

在物理世界中，生命透過無數活動展開。人們工作、旅行、聚集與探索。其他生命移動、生長，並回應周圍的條件。水持續流動，物質之間傳遞熱量，機器在人與其他事物之間運作。這些活動發生於物理空間之中，而位置、結構、物質性質及周圍條件，影響著哪些事情能夠發生。每一項活動都會遇到早已存在的關係，同時也成為世界持續變化的關係的一部分。

人工智能進入的物理空間，既有位置與運動，也有結構、物質、作用與持續變化的關係。這個世界遠比一張可供導航的地圖豐富。空間與環境密切相連，因為空間關係總是在周圍世界的條件中發展。本章關注事物如何在物理空間中存在、活動並相互影響。當人工智能開始感知、解讀並參與這些活動，理解空間關係便成為理解其存在與行動可能帶來什麼的重要基礎。

### English

Every activity unfolds within physical space. Walking, building, learning, and working with materials all depend on the positions and properties of the things involved. A person may reach for an object, move around an obstacle, or work alongside others. Physical boundaries, dimensions, and arrangements influence how these activities can proceed. Yet describing the objects and their positions does not fully explain what is taking place. To understand an activity, we must also consider how the things involved relate to one another.

Physical space provides the conditions in which activities become possible. A surface can bear weight, a structure can maintain its form under load, and materials can conduct or resist the passage of heat. These properties remain real regardless of how people use them. When someone prepares food, builds an instrument, or operates a machine, the activity depends on relationships among materials, energy, physical structures, and the surrounding world. Some relationships are immediately visible; others become apparent through their effects. The possibilities of an activity arise from these conditions together, extending beyond the dimensions and boundaries of the space in which it occurs.

Relationships are present even when no deliberate activity is taking place. A book resting on a shelf is supported by a physical structure. Sunlight entering a room illuminates some surfaces while leaving others in shadow. A plant receives light and water through relationships with its surroundings. These conditions may appear ordinary because they are familiar, yet each involves physical properties and connections that influence what can happen. The arrangement of things matters through the relations it establishes, including relations that cannot be understood simply by naming the objects involved.

Activities bring these existing relationships into further contact. When someone uses a tool, the result depends on the tool's properties, the material being worked, and the action applied. When people collaborate, their activities also depend on the physical resources and conditions they share. Other living things participate in the world through their own capacities, responding to conditions that may be influenced by human activity without being created for human purposes. Physical space contains all these relationships together. Understanding an activity therefore requires attention to the wider conditions in which it unfolds, even when only a small part of those conditions is directly involved.

Artificial intelligence encounters this complexity through the information it receives about physical space. A camera may identify objects, a sensor may register a change in temperature, and a model may recognize patterns in the available observations. Yet identifying an object or measuring a condition does not necessarily reveal the relationships responsible for what is observed. A change in temperature may reflect sunlight, the operation of equipment, or the movement of air. Different conditions can produce similar observations, while important relationships may remain unseen. Understanding spatial activity requires interpreting information within this wider physical context, with an awareness of what remains unknown.

Space can therefore be understood through both physical presence and the relations among what is present. Its dimensions and boundaries are real, but they do not exhaust the conditions of activity. The physical world supports countless interactions through structures, materials, living beings, and artificial things, each contributing to what can happen. For intelligence entering this world, knowing what exists within space is one capability; understanding how things are related is another. To understand physical space, intelligence must recognize not only where things are, but how they are connected, how they affect one another, and what their relationships make possible.

### 繁體中文

每一項活動都發生於物理空間之中。行走、建造、學習及運用材料進行工作，都與相關事物的位置及性質有關。人可能伸手取得物件、繞過障礙，或與其他人共同工作。物理邊界、尺度與安排，影響這些活動如何進行。然而，僅僅描述物件及其位置，仍不足以說明其中正在發生什麼。要理解一項活動，還需要考察相關事物如何彼此關聯。

物理空間提供活動得以發生的條件。表面可以承受重量，結構可以在負載下維持形態，材料則可以傳導熱量，或阻礙熱量傳遞。無論人們如何使用，這些性質都真實存在。當人們準備食物、製作儀器或操作機器，活動便依賴材料、能量、物理結構及周圍世界之間的關係。有些關係可以直接觀察，另一些則透過其作用顯現。活動的可能性產生於這些條件的共同作用，超出了活動所在空間的尺度與邊界所能表達的範圍。

即使沒有刻意進行的活動，事物之間仍然存在關係。放在書架上的書，由物理結構承托。進入房間的陽光照亮部分表面，也使另一些地方留在陰影之中。植物透過與周圍世界的關係取得光線與水分。這些條件因為熟悉而顯得平常，卻都涉及物理性質及連接，並影響可能發生的事情。事物的安排透過其中建立的關係而具有作用，而這些關係無法僅憑列出相關物件的名稱得到理解。

活動使既有的關係產生進一步的相互作用。當一個人使用工具，結果取決於工具的性質、正在處理的材料，以及施加的動作。當人們共同工作，活動也依賴彼此共享的物理資源與條件。其他生命透過自身的能力參與世界，回應那些可能受到人類活動影響、卻並非為人類目的而存在的條件。物理空間同時包含這些關係。因此，要理解一項活動，還需要留意它所處的更廣泛條件，即使其中只有一小部分直接參與當下的活動。

人工智能透過取得物理空間的資訊，開始面對這種複雜性。攝影機可以辨識物件，感測器可以記錄溫度變化，模型則可能從觀測資訊中辨識某些模式。然而，辨識一個物件或量測某項條件，未必能揭示形成觀測結果的關係。溫度變化可能來自日光、設備運作或空氣流動。不同條件可能產生相似的觀測結果，而重要的關係也可能尚未被看見。理解空間中的活動，需要把資訊放在更廣泛的物理情境中加以解讀，同時認識仍有哪些事情尚未得知。

因此，理解空間需要同時考察物理存在，以及存在者之間的關係。尺度與邊界是真實的，卻不足以涵蓋活動的全部條件。物理世界透過結構、材料、生命與人工造物，支持無數相互作用，而它們都參與形成可能發生的事情。對進入這個世界的智能而言，知道空間中存在什麼是一種能力，理解事物如何彼此關聯則是另一種能力。要理解物理空間，智能需要辨識事物位於何處、如何彼此連接、如何相互影響，以及這些關係使什麼成為可能。

### English

Things exist at different positions within physical space, with distances, directions, relative positions, and connections among them. These relationships influence activities long before anyone attempts to describe them. The position of a window affects where daylight reaches; the distance between a person and an object influences whether it can be reached directly; the arrangement of equipment determines which resources can be used together. Each relationship connects the location of something to the possibilities available around it. For intelligence interpreting physical space, position becomes meaningful through the things with which it is related.

Distance is a measurable feature of the physical world, but its significance depends on what lies between the things being considered. Two objects separated by a short distance may be divided by a solid barrier, while a greater distance may be crossed through an accessible opening. The shape and size of the objects involved also matter. A passage sufficient for one object may be too narrow for another. Proximity therefore describes more than a number recorded between positions. Its practical significance emerges through the physical arrangements and properties that influence how things can approach, reach, or affect one another.

Direction contributes another dimension to these relationships. Sunlight enters a building differently as its direction changes, even when the building remains in the same location. A sensor facing one part of a space may observe conditions that another sensor, positioned differently, cannot detect. A surface may be exposed to light, sound, or airflow from one direction while another surface remains sheltered. Orientation shapes which interactions are possible and which conditions become observable. To understand position fully, we must therefore consider how things face, extend toward, or remain separated from one another.

Physical proximity and connectivity are related, but they describe different conditions. Objects may be close without being physically connected, and distant objects may exchange information through a communication network. Some connections carry energy or material; others allow signals or information to pass between locations. A connection may influence activities without changing the positions of the things involved. Accessibility introduces a further question: whether the available connection allows a particular person, object, or activity to reach what it requires. Being close, being connected, and being able to make use of a connection are distinct possibilities within physical space.

Activities also reveal that spatial relationships cannot always be understood from position alone. A tool may be near the person who needs it but oriented in a way that makes it difficult to use. Equipment may be installed close to another device while remaining separated by a structure that prevents direct interaction. A person walking through a shared space may encounter different possibilities according to the direction of movement and the positions of others. Artificial intelligence can use information about these relationships to interpret which interactions are possible, although the observations available to it may remain incomplete.

Position and proximity give physical activities their particular spatial conditions. Distance, direction, orientation, physical connections, and accessibility influence what can be reached, observed, exchanged, or accomplished. They matter to people, other living things, and artificial systems in different ways, according to their properties and capabilities. For intelligence entering the physical world, understanding these relationships means recognizing how the location of one thing affects the possibilities of another. Spatial relations make interaction possible, while the activities of different participants give those relations further significance.

### 繁體中文

事物存在於不同位置，彼此具有距離、方向、相對位置及連接關係。早在人們試圖描述這些關係之前，它們便已經影響各種活動。窗戶的位置影響日光能夠照射何處；人與物件之間的距離，影響能否直接伸手取得；設備的安排則影響哪些資源可以共同使用。每一種關係，都使事物所在的位置與周圍的可能性產生聯繫。對解讀物理空間的智能而言，位置透過它與其他事物的關係而具有意義。

距離是物理世界中可以量測的特徵，但它的重要性，取決於相關事物之間存在什麼。兩個相距很近的物件，可能被一道堅固的屏障分隔；更遠的距離，反而可能透過可通行的開口跨越。相關物件的形狀與大小也具有作用。足以讓某個物件通過的通道，對另一個物件可能過於狹窄。因此，接近所表達的意義，超出了位置之間的數值距離。它的實際意義，透過影響事物如何接近、抵達或彼此作用的物理安排與性質而呈現。

方向使這些關係具有另一個面向。即使建築始終位於同一位置，當日光的方向改變，光線進入建築的方式也會不同。朝向某個區域的感測器，可能觀測到另一個位置或朝向不同的感測器無法取得的條件。一個表面可能承受來自某個方向的光線、聲音或氣流，另一個表面卻受到遮蔽。朝向影響哪些相互作用能夠發生，以及哪些條件可以被觀測。因此，要充分理解位置，還需要考察事物朝向何處、向哪些方向延伸，以及如何彼此分隔。

物理接近與連接性彼此相關，卻描述不同的條件。物件可以相距很近而沒有物理連接，相隔遙遠的物件也可以透過通訊網路交換資訊。有些連接傳遞能量或物質，另一些則讓訊號與資訊在不同位置之間傳送。連接可以影響活動，而不必改變相關事物的位置。可達性又提出進一步的問題：既有的連接，是否能使特定的人、物件或活動取得所需的事物？彼此接近、建立連接，以及能夠實際利用連接，是物理空間中不同的可能性。

活動也顯示，空間關係未必能僅憑位置得到理解。一件工具可能就在需要它的人身旁，卻因擺放方向而難以使用。兩台設備可能安裝在相近的位置，卻被阻礙直接作用的結構分隔。當人們穿越共享空間，移動方向及其他參與者的位置，也會影響他們所面對的可能性。人工智能可以利用這些關係的資訊，解讀哪些相互作用能夠發生，即使它所取得的觀測仍可能不完整。

位置與接近，為物理活動建立具體的空間條件。距離、方向、朝向、物理連接與可達性，影響哪些事物能夠被抵達、觀測、交換或加以作用。根據各自的物理性質與能力，這些關係對人、其他生命與人工系統具有不同意義。對進入物理世界的智能而言，理解這些關係，意味著認識一個事物的位置如何影響另一個事物的可能性。空間關係使相互作用成為可能，而不同參與者的活動，又賦予這些關係進一步的意義。

### English

Things in the physical world affect one another. A person pressing a key causes a mechanism to move; a vessel placed over heat changes the temperature of what it contains; a tree provides shade to living things beneath it. These interactions occur through different physical processes, yet each reveals how the activity or properties of one thing can influence another. People, other living beings, materials, machines, and artificial systems participate in such relationships in different ways. Their presence matters through what they can do, what can be done to them, and the effects they produce within their surroundings.

The properties of physical things become significant through these encounters. The strength of a material influences what it can support, while its flexibility may allow it to bend without breaking. Water changes the behavior of soil, and heat can change the form or condition of a substance. People learn to work with these properties through observation, experience, and action. A craftsperson shaping wood responds to its grain and resistance; a gardener working with soil responds to moisture, texture, and growth. Their activities depend on relationships with a world that has its own physical characteristics, independent of the purposes people bring to it.

Human activities also involve relationships among people and the things they share. In a public library, books, tables, light, equipment, and digital resources support reading, research, and exchange. One person may examine a physical collection while another uses a computer; a discussion may begin between people who arrived for different reasons. The arrangement of the space influences how these activities coexist, but what occurs depends on the participants and the resources they encounter. The library illustrates how physical space can support many activities at once without reducing them to a single function.

Artificial intelligence can enter such activities through systems that observe, interpret, and act within their available capabilities. In a library, an artificial system might help locate a resource or coordinate equipment. In another setting, it might respond to changes in light or temperature, support a process involving materials, or help people interpret information about their surroundings. Its participation may occur through a fixed device, a networked service, or machinery capable of physical action. Yet an observation of occupied space does not explain every activity taking place there, and a measurable change does not always reveal the purposes or physical causes behind it.

Participation does not make every participant equivalent. People can form purposes, reconsider decisions, and take responsibility for their actions. Other living things possess their own forms of perception, activity, and need. Materials and physical objects influence events through their properties, without having purposes of their own. Artificial systems can process information and perform actions within the capabilities and authority available to them. These differences shape the relationships among participants. The influence of a heavy object, the response of a living organism, and the action of an artificial system may all matter to the same activity, but each must be understood according to its nature.

An interaction may also extend beyond those who initiate it. Equipment that generates heat can affect nearby materials and the comfort of people using the space. A change in lighting can support one activity while making another more difficult. A resource used by one person may become temporarily unavailable to others. Such consequences reveal the breadth of relationships involved in even familiar activities. Artificial intelligence can contribute to these interactions, but its actions enter the same physical world as the actions of every other participant. The relationships through which activities occur can, in turn, be changed by those activities themselves.

### 繁體中文

物理世界中的事物會相互影響。人按下一個按鍵，可以使機構運動；容器置於熱源之上，會改變其中物質的溫度；樹木則為下方的生命提供陰影。這些相互作用透過不同的物理過程發生，卻都顯示一個事物的活動或性質如何影響另一個事物。人、其他生命、材料、機器與人工系統，以不同方式參與這些關係。它們的存在，透過自身能夠做什麼、可能受到什麼作用，以及對周圍產生什麼影響而具有意義。

物理事物的性質，在這些相互作用中呈現出重要性。材料的強度影響它能夠支撐什麼，柔韌性則可能使它在彎曲時不致斷裂。水會改變土壤的狀態，熱量也可能改變物質的形態或性質。人們透過觀察、經驗與行動，學習如何與這些性質相處。木匠加工木材時，需要回應木材的紋理與阻力；園丁照料土壤時，則需要留意水分、質地與生長。這些活動依賴人與物理世界之間的關係，而這個世界具有自身的物理性質，不取決於人們賦予它的目的。

人的活動也包含人與人，以及人與共享事物之間的關係。在公共圖書館裡，書籍、桌椅、光線、設備與數位資源，支持閱讀、研究與交流。一個人可能正在查閱實體館藏，另一個人使用電腦；原本為不同目的來到這裡的人，也可能展開討論。空間的安排影響這些活動如何共同存在，而實際發生的事情，則取決於參與者及他們遇到的資源。圖書館讓我們看到，物理空間可以同時支持許多活動，而不必將它們歸於單一功能。

人工智能可以透過具備觀測、解讀與行動能力的系統，進入這些活動。在圖書館裡，人工系統可能協助尋找資源或協調設備；在其他場所，它可能回應光照或溫度的變化，支持涉及材料的工作過程，或協助人們理解周圍環境的資訊。這種參與可以透過固定設備、網路服務，或具有物理行動能力的機器實現。然而，觀測到一個空間有人使用，並不能解釋其中正在進行的全部活動；量測到某項變化，也未必能揭示背後的目的或物理原因。

共同參與並不意味著所有參與者都具有相同的性質。人可以形成目的、重新考慮決定，並為自己的行動承擔責任。其他生命具有各自的感知、活動與需要。材料及物理事物透過自身性質影響事件，而無須具有自己的目的。人工系統則在可用的能力與權限範圍內處理資訊並執行行動。這些差異塑造了參與者之間的關係。沉重物件的作用、生命的回應，以及人工系統的行動，都可能對同一項活動產生影響，但理解它們時，仍需尊重各自不同的性質。

相互作用的影響，也可能超出直接發起活動的參與者。產生熱量的設備可能影響附近的材料，以及使用空間的人所感受到的舒適程度。光照的改變可能支持某項活動，同時使另一項活動變得困難。一個人正在使用的資源，也可能暫時無法供其他人使用。這些後果揭示，即使是熟悉的活動，也包含廣泛的關係。人工智能可以參與這些相互作用，但它的行動與其他參與者的行動一樣，都進入同一個物理世界。而活動所依循的關係，也可能因活動本身而改變。

### English

Every action takes place within existing relationships and may change them. A person moving a table changes how an area can be used. Opening a window alters the passage of air and sound between adjoining spaces. Turning on a machine can introduce heat, vibration, or movement into its surroundings. Some of these changes involve the position of physical things; others alter the conditions through which things affect one another. The activity unfolds within relationships already present, while its consequences become part of the conditions encountered by whatever happens next.

These changes make time essential to understanding space. A room may retain the same walls and furniture while its illumination changes throughout the day. A surface can gradually warm under sunlight and cool when the light disappears. Soil may hold water after rain and become dry over the following days. The location of a thing alone cannot describe these developments. To understand what is taking place, we need some account of how its condition has changed, what relationships produced that change, and what may continue after the immediate activity ends.

Some actions leave consequences that remain long after the action itself. A structure assembled from separate materials can continue to support weight after the work of construction is finished. A door left open changes the exchange of air and the accessibility of adjoining areas. Equipment placed in a new position may alter how other resources can be used. These changes become part of the physical conditions of later activities. What is possible now depends partly on what occurred before, even when the earlier action is no longer visible.

Other relationships change without a single participant deliberately altering them. Plants grow toward available light, moisture moves through materials, and temperatures shift as energy passes between things. These processes may proceed slowly or rapidly, predictably or unexpectedly. They can also interact with human and artificial activities. A machine operating under one set of conditions may behave differently when its surroundings change. Understanding the space in which it operates therefore requires attention to processes beyond its immediate task, including changes that may not be directly observed.

Artificial intelligence encounters this continuing change through observations that are necessarily made at particular times. A sensor may report the temperature of a surface, but that reading does not establish whether the surface is warming or cooling. An image may show the state of an area, while leaving earlier events and hidden processes unknown. Information can also become outdated while the world continues to change. A system acting on the basis of its observations must therefore consider how much of the relevant relationship it understands and how the conditions may have changed since they were observed.

Spatial relationships differ in how long they endure. The position of a supporting column may remain stable for decades, while a shadow crosses a surface in minutes. A temporary change can have lasting effects, and a stable structure can participate in activities that vary from day to day. Physical space contains these different rhythms together. As actions and natural processes alter existing conditions, new possibilities and limitations arise for those who occupy it. Artificial intelligence enters this ongoing development, participating in relationships shaped by earlier events and capable of being changed by what happens next.

### 繁體中文

每項行動都發生在既有關係之中，並可能改變這些關係。人移動一張桌子，會改變某個區域可以如何使用；打開窗戶，會改變相鄰空間之間空氣與聲音的流動；啟動機器，則可能向周圍帶來熱量、振動或運動。有些變化涉及物理事物的位置，另一些則改變事物彼此作用的條件。活動在早已存在的關係之中展開，而它所造成的後果，也會成為接下來發生的事情所面對的條件。

這些變化使時間成為理解空間不可缺少的部分。一個房間可能保持相同的牆壁與家具，但其中的光照會隨一天的時間而改變。表面可能在日光照射下逐漸升溫，又在光線消失後冷卻。土壤可能在雨後保有水分，並於接下來幾天逐漸乾燥。僅憑事物的位置，無法描述這些發展。要理解正在發生什麼，還需要考察其狀態如何改變、哪些關係造成了變化，以及直接活動結束後可能持續發生什麼。

有些行動留下的後果，會在行動結束很久之後繼續存在。由不同材料組成的結構，可以在建造工作完成後持續承受重量。一扇保持開啟的門，改變相鄰區域之間的空氣交換與可達性。設備放置於新的位置，也可能改變其他資源的使用方式。這些變化成為後續活動所面對的物理條件。現在能夠發生什麼，部分取決於先前發生過的事情，即使早先的行動已經無法直接看見。

另一些關係的改變，並不需要某個參與者刻意促成。植物朝向可取得的光線生長，水分在材料之間移動，能量在事物之間傳遞時也改變溫度。這些過程有些緩慢，有些迅速；有些容易預測，有些則未必如此。它們還可能與人類及人工系統的活動相互作用。在某種條件下運作的機器，當周圍條件改變時，可能呈現不同的運行狀態。因此，理解機器所在的空間，也需要留意超出其當下任務的過程，包括那些未必能被直接觀測的變化。

人工智能透過特定時刻取得的觀測，面對這個持續變化的世界。感測器可以回報某個表面的溫度，但單次讀數無法說明表面正在升溫還是降溫。影像可以顯示某個區域當下的狀態，卻可能無法揭示此前的事件與隱藏的過程。當世界持續改變，資訊也可能逐漸失去時效。因此，系統依據觀測採取行動時，需要考慮自己對相關關係的理解達到什麼程度，以及自觀測以來，物理條件可能發生了哪些改變。

不同空間關係具有不同的持續時間。支撐柱的位置可能數十年保持不變，一道陰影卻可能在幾分鐘內越過某個表面。暫時的改變可能留下長久的影響，穩定的結構也可能參與每天不同的活動。物理空間同時包含這些不同的變化節奏。當行動與自然過程改變既有條件，也會為其中的參與者帶來新的可能與限制。人工智能進入的正是這種持續發展的現實：其中的關係由過去的事件塑造，也可能因接下來發生的事情而改變。

### English

Physical space is shared by things that differ in their nature, capacities, and forms of activity. People work, rest, learn, and create; other living beings grow, move, and respond to their surroundings; machines perform operations through the physical capabilities built into them. These activities do not always serve a common purpose, and they need not be coordinated by one participant. Yet they unfold within a world of shared physical conditions. What happens in one part of a space may influence activities elsewhere, sometimes through relationships that none of those involved has deliberately established.

Coexistence does not require direct interaction. A tree growing beside a building can alter the shade and temperature experienced by people inside it. A machine operating in one area may generate vibrations or sound that travel beyond the place where its task is performed. Water passing through soil can affect the growth of plants whose roots never touch the original source. The participants in these relationships may never encounter one another directly. Their activities nevertheless become connected through matter, energy, structures, and other conditions of the physical world.

The meaning of a shared space also differs among those who inhabit it. A sheltered corner may provide a place for a person to read, a resting place for an animal, or a favorable condition for a plant to grow. The same source of light may support one activity while creating difficulty for another. An artificial system may register some of these differences through sensors and observations, but what it measures is only a part of the relationships present. People, other living things, and artificial systems perceive and respond according to different capacities. No single participant experiences the entirety of a shared space.

New relationships may form through activities whose outcomes were never anticipated. People working on separate problems may discover that their knowledge can be brought together. A tool created for one purpose may enable an unfamiliar form of work when it is used alongside other materials. A change in the physical conditions of a place may allow a living thing to occupy an area where it could not previously survive. Such developments arise from encounters among existing things and conditions. Space provides possibilities for these encounters, while what emerges from them remains open to the actions and properties of those involved.

Artificial intelligence enters this shared space as a further participant. It may help people recognize connections, coordinate the use of resources, or respond to physical changes that would otherwise escape immediate attention. Its actions may also produce effects beyond their intended purpose. A system adjusting a physical condition for one activity can influence other activities occurring nearby. New relationships may arise as people and other systems respond to what it does. The intelligence available to an artificial system therefore participates within a world that exceeds the purposes, observations, and models through which it was created to operate.

Understanding intelligent space requires attention to the relationships that make activity possible and to the limits of each participant's knowledge of them. Physical things retain their properties, living beings pursue their own activities, and artificial systems act through capabilities that remain partial. Their interactions may preserve existing conditions, alter them, or bring new relationships into being. Intelligence can act within space only to the extent that it can engage with the relationships that make action possible. Artificial intelligence enters a world whose relationships existed before its arrival, are changed in part through its participation, and continue beyond what it can know or foresee.

### 繁體中文

物理空間由性質、能力與活動方式各不相同的事物共同佔有。人們工作、休息、學習與創造；其他生命生長、移動並回應周圍的條件；機器則透過自身具備的物理能力執行操作。這些活動未必具有共同目的，也無須由某個參與者統一協調。然而，它們都在共享物理條件的世界中展開。空間某處發生的事情，可能影響其他地方的活動，有時甚至透過參與者從未刻意建立的關係產生作用。

共同存在無須以直接互動為前提。生長在建築旁的樹木，可以改變建築內的人所感受到的陰影與溫度。某個區域運作的機器，可能產生傳到工作場所之外的振動或聲音。流經土壤的水，也可能影響根部並未接觸原來水源的植物生長。這些關係中的參與者，可能從未直接相遇。它們的活動仍然透過物質、能量、結構，以及物理世界的其他條件彼此相連。

共享空間對其中不同的存在者，也可能具有不同意義。一處受到遮蔽的角落，可能讓人安靜閱讀，成為動物休息的場所，或為植物生長提供適合的條件。同一個光源可能支持某項活動，卻為另一項活動帶來困難。人工系統可以透過感測器與觀測辨識其中部分差異，但它所量測的，始終只是實際存在關係的一部分。人、其他生命與人工系統，依照各自不同的能力感知並回應世界。沒有任何單一參與者能夠經驗共同空間中的一切。

新的關係，也可能從結果未曾被預先設想的活動中形成。研究不同問題的人，可能發現彼此的知識可以結合。為某個目的製作的工具，在與其他材料共同使用時，可能開啟新的工作方式。一個地方的物理條件改變，也可能使某種生命得以進入原本無法生存的區域。這些發展產生於既有事物與條件之間的相遇。空間為這些相遇提供可能，而相遇之後會形成什麼，仍取決於相關參與者的行動與性質。

人工智能作為進一步的參與者，進入這個共同的空間。它可能協助人們辨識關聯、協調資源的使用，或回應那些未必能被人立即注意的物理變化。它的行動也可能產生超出原先目的的影響。為某項活動調整物理條件的系統，可能同時影響附近正在進行的其他活動。當人與其他系統回應它的行動，新的關係也可能隨之形成。因此，人工系統所具備的智能，始終參與在一個超出其設計目的、觀測資訊與模型所能完整掌握的世界之中。

理解智能空間，需要留意使活動成為可能的各種關係，也需要認識每個參與者對這些關係的理解都具有界限。物理事物保有自身的性質，其他生命進行各自的活動，人工系統則透過始終有限的能力參與其中。它們的相互作用可能維持既有條件、改變這些條件，或使新的關係形成。智能能夠在空間中參與行動的程度，取決於它能在多大程度上理解並回應使行動成為可能的關係。人工智能進入的是一個在它到來之前便已存在各種關係的世界；這些關係可能因它的參與而改變，也將持續存在於它所能認識與預見的範圍之外。

# 第六章——空間是關係性的

**核心問題：** 人、事物與活動如何透過彼此的關係構成空間？

**獨有思想內容：**

- 空間具有物理範圍，但其意義不能只由尺寸和位置決定。
- 距離、接近、分隔、連接與可達性影響人和事物的關係。
- 相同的物理場所，可以因活動與關係不同而具有不同意義。
- 人不只是佔據空間，也透過活動賦予空間秩序和用途。
- 智能系統進入空間時，面對的是既有的關係，而不是空白容器。
- 空間關乎關係，環境關乎條件；兩者相關但不能混同。

**本章思想成果：** 建立 Relational Space｜關係性空間的概念。

**概念邊界：** 不把空間等同於人際關係；不提前研究有目的的系統組織及架構設計。
