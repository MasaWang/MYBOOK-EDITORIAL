# 第二章——人的尺度

**核心問題：** 我們應以什麼尺度判斷自身創造的價值？

**獨有思想內容：**

- 能夠創造，不代表創造一定值得追求。
- Capability｜能力、Possibility｜可能性與 Value｜價值並不相同。
- Intelligence｜智能擴展可能性，Wisdom｜智慧參與方向與價值判斷。
- 人的尺度不只是效能、效率或能力大小。
- 人的尺度也不能簡化為單一的生活方式或幸福標準。
- 對創造價值的判斷需要考慮人與其他生命、自然及共同世界的關係。

**本章思想成果：** 建立人的尺度作為理解、衡量和判斷創造價值的哲學依據。

**概念邊界：** 不把尺度等同於人的能力限制；不詳細分析非預期後果或責任分配。

# Chapter 2 — The Human Measure

# 第二章——人的尺度

Capability is easy to enlarge. Judgment is not.

能力容易擴大。判斷不容易。

We can make systems see farther, answer faster, act with less waiting. That kind of growth shows up on charts. What does not show up as cleanly is the quieter rearrangement that follows: what a person must now watch, explain, accept, or stop being able to refuse. If nothing stands outside capability to measure it, capability begins to measure us.

我們可以讓系統看得更遠、答得更快、少一些等待再行動。這種增長會出現在圖表上。較不清楚顯現的，是隨之而來的安靜重組：人現在必須監看什麼、解釋什麼、接受什麼，或者再也難以拒絕什麼。若沒有什麼站在能力之外去衡量它，能力就開始衡量我們。

> **Human life is the measure by which intelligence, technology, systems, and artifacts must ultimately be judged.**  
> **人的生活，是衡量智能、技術、系統與人工造物的最終尺度。**

## I. The Starting Point Is Human Life

## 一、起點是人的生活

Technology can begin almost anywhere—in a model, a laboratory, a market, a sudden feasibility. Judgment cannot. Judgment has to begin somewhere firm enough that later success does not rewrite the question.

技術幾乎可以從任何地方開始——模型、實驗室、市場，或一次突然變得可行的可能。判斷不行。判斷必須從某處足夠穩固的地方開始，使後來的成功無法改寫問題本身。

If we start from what machines can do, “better” soon means they can do more. If we start from what markets can grow, “better” soon means larger reach. Neither answer is empty. Both leave something unpaid: whether the people living with the result are freer, clearer, safer in a way that still feels like living—or only more efficiently arranged.

若從機器所能之事出發，「更好」很快就變成它們更能做更多。若從市場所能擴大之事出發，「更好」很快就變成觸及更廣。兩種答案都不空洞。兩者都留下未付清的帳：與結果共處的人，是否更自由、更清楚、更安全，且仍像在生活——還是僅僅被安排得更有效率。

This is why the starting point is human life. Not because life is softer than technology, but because life is where the unpaid part appears.

這就是為什麼起點是人的生活。不是因為生活比技術更柔軟，而是因為未付清的那一部分，會在生活裡顯現。

Life is already running before any system arrives. People are already mid-sentence, mid-errand, mid-worry, mid-care. A system does not open the day; it steps into a day that has begun without it. That fact is easy to forget when we are building. Building loves clean beginnings. Life rarely offers them.

在任何系統到來以前，生活已經在運行。人已經在話沒說完、事沒做完、擔心沒放下、照顧沒結束的半途之中。系統並不打開這一天；它走進一個沒有它也已開始的日子。建造的時候，這一點很容易被忘掉。建造喜歡乾淨的起點。生活很少提供。

So the measure has to travel with us from the first decision: not “does it work,” first, but “into whose living does this working enter, and what does it ask them to become.”

因此尺度必須從第一個決定就跟著我們走：不是先問「它是否有效」，而是問「這份有效進入誰的生活，又要求他們變成什麼」。

## II. A Person Is More Than a User

## 二、人不只是使用者

“User” is a sharp tool. It cuts a person down to what a system can take in.

「使用者」是一把利器。它把人削到系統吞得下的部分。

That cut is useful. Without it, design cannot even begin. Buttons need someone to press them; permissions need someone to grant them; logs need someone to leave a mark. The trouble starts when the cut is mistaken for the whole.

這一刀有用。沒有它，設計幾乎無法開始。按鈕需要有人去按；權限需要有人去給；紀錄需要有人留下痕跡。麻煩始於：把這一刀誤認成全部。

A person continues where the log ends. They withhold. They change their mind without filing an update. They carry debts and loves that never become input. They depend on a system and still cannot see through it; or they see through it and still cannot step out. None of that fits neatly into “user,” and yet all of it is what lives with whatever we build.

人在紀錄結束之處仍繼續。他們保留。他們改變主意，卻不提交更新。他們背負從不成為輸入的虧欠與愛。他們依賴一個系統，卻仍看不透它；或者看透了，卻仍走不出去。這些都不整齊裝進「使用者」，卻正是與我們所建造之物共處的全部。

If we design only for the cut, we will improve the surface and starve the rest. Interaction can become smoother while a life becomes thinner—because the system has learned to recognize everything except the person who must still live after recognition is over.

若我們只為這一刀而設計，我們會改善表面，並讓其餘挨餓。互動可以更順，生活卻可以更薄——因為系統學會了辨認一切，除了那個在辨認結束之後仍必須活下去的人。

The person is not an enriched user. The user is a narrowed person. That order matters. Reverse it, and every success starts training us to forget what we cut away.

人不是被豐富過的使用者。使用者是被收窄過的人。這個次序很重要。顛倒它，每一次成功都會訓練我們，忘掉被削去的東西。

## III. Need, Vulnerability, and Dignity

## 三、需要、脆弱性與尊嚴

We build because something does not hold by itself.

我們建造，是因為有些東西無法自己撐住。

Night falls. Bodies tire. Distance remains. Attention breaks. People ask for help—sometimes out loud, sometimes only by failing in places where failure hurts. Need is not an insult to inventiveness. It is the reason inventiveness has somewhere to go.

夜會來。身體會倦。距離仍在。注意力會斷。人會求援——有時說出口，有時只是在失敗會傷人的地方失敗。需要不是對創造力的侮辱。它是創造力有處可去的理由。

But the same opening that lets help in also lets overreach in. To meet a need, a system must come close. Coming close, it can see more than it was asked to see. It can decide faster than a person is ready for. It can protect so tightly that the protected life has nowhere left to turn. What we call support and what we call capture often share a doorway; we only learn which one we built after someone has to live inside it.

可是讓幫助進來的同一個開口，也讓越界進來。要回應需要，系統必須靠近。一靠近，它就可能看見超出被請求的東西。它可能比人準備好的速度更快做決定。它可能保護得太緊，使被保護的生活再也無處轉身。我們所稱的支持與所稱的捕捉，往往共用一扇門；究竟建成哪一種，常常要等到有人必須住進去，才知道。

Vulnerability is this exposure—not a mood, not a slogan. It is what happens when life must lean on what it does not fully control. That lean is how care becomes possible. It is also how domination becomes convenient.

脆弱就是這種暴露——不是情緒，也不是口號。它是生活不得不倚靠自己無法完全掌控之物時所發生的事。這份倚靠使照料成為可能。它也使支配變得方便。

Dignity is what we refuse to trade away in that lean. A person may accept help and still remain the one who understands what is happening, who may refuse the next step, who is not rewritten into a case to be processed. Lose that, and the need may still be met. The one who needed may already be gone from the meeting.

尊嚴是我們在這份倚靠裡拒絕拿去交換的東西。人可以接受幫助，卻仍然是那個明白正在發生什麼的人，仍然可以拒絕下一步，仍然不被改寫成待處理的個案。失去這個，需要也許仍被滿足。那個有需要的人，卻可能已從這場滿足裡消失。

## IV. What Counts as Improvement?

## 四、什麼才算改善？

I distrust a clean celebration of improvement.

我不大信任對改善的乾淨慶祝。

Not because speed or accuracy are false. They can save hours, prevent harm, keep a night from tipping into worse. The distrust is simpler: those gains can be real while something else is being spent that the celebration never names.

不是因為速度或準確是假的。它們可以省下時間、擋住傷害、讓一夜不滑向更糟。不信任更簡單：這些收益可以是真的，同時另一些東西正在被花掉，而慶祝從不說出它們的名字。

A task finishes sooner, and a person spends the saved time watching the system that finished it. A recommendation arrives early, and the chance to form one’s own sense of the situation arrives late—or not at all. Failure becomes rarer in one place and more opaque in another. The project can still be called a success. The day around it may feel more supervised than supported.

一件事更快做完，人卻把省下的時間拿去監看那個做完它的系統。建議來得更早，人自己形成對情境的感覺卻來得更晚——或者根本不來。失敗在一處變少，在另一處變得更不透明。項目仍可被稱為成功。它周圍的一天，卻可能更像被監管，而不是被支持。

So improvement is not a number looking for applause. It is a claim that must say for whom life became more possible, and what was taken to buy that possibility. If we cannot answer, we are not measuring improvement. We are advertising capability.

因此改善不是一個等待掌聲的數字。它是一項主張，必須說出：對誰而言，生活變得更可能；又花掉了什麼，才買到這份可能。若答不出，我們就不是在衡量改善。我們是在為能力做廣告。

## V. Life as the Measure

## 五、以生活為尺度

To take life as the measure is not to decorate engineering with kindness. It is to keep the unpaid costs from disappearing into the success of the machine.

以生活為尺度，不是給工程披上善意的裝飾。它是不讓未付清的代價，消失進機器的成功裡。

Life does not offer one correct pattern. People gather time differently, owe themselves to different others, change in ways no deployment plan schedules. The measure is not a template of the good life. It is a demand that whatever we enlarge remain answerable to the living that has to absorb the enlargement.

生活不提供一種正確樣板。人聚合時間的方式不同，對不同的人負有不同的虧欠，改變的方式也從不按部署計劃發生。尺度不是好生活的範本。它是一項要求：凡我們所擴大之物，都必須向那不得不吸收這場擴大的生活交代。

Technology may still grow sharper, closer, more continuous. It may carry what people should not have to carry alone. None of that is forbidden by the measure. What the measure forbids is quieter and harder: that we let “it works” finish the sentence when the sentence still belongs to someone’s day.

技術仍可以變得更利、更近、更持續。它仍可以承擔人不應獨自承擔的東西。尺度並不禁止這些。尺度禁止的，更安靜，也更難：在句子仍屬於某個人的一天時，就讓「它有效」把句子說完。

**This is the human measure.**

**這就是人的尺度。**

Once life stands as the measure, another pressure appears. We will need to say what we mean by intelligence when it keeps expanding—and how that expansion differs from knowing what is worth doing.

一旦生活立為尺度，另一種壓力就出現了。我們將需要說清：當智能不斷擴大時，我們究竟在說什麼——以及這種擴大，與知道什麼值得去做，如何不是同一件事。
