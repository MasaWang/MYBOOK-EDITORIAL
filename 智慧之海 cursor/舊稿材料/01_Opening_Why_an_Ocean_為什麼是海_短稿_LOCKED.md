We see the ocean.  
我們看見海。

Tides return. Waves reach the shore again and again. The sun rises and sets. The power of the ocean lies in what it makes possible.  
潮汐往復，海浪一次次抵達岸邊。太陽升起，又落下。海的力量，在於它所開啟的可能。

We see the ocean.  
我們看見海。

Life takes form within what the ocean makes possible. Through life, intelligence emerged.  
生命在海所開啟的可能之中成形。經由生命，智能隨之出現。

We see the ocean.  
我們看見海。

We begin to understand how intelligence can exist.  
我們開始理解智能可以如何存在。

Then how should it participate in our world?  
那麼，它應當如何參與我們的世界？

Return is not repetition.  
回歸並非重複。
