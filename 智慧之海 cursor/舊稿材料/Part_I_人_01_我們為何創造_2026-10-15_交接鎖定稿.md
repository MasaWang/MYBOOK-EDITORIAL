# 第一章——我們為何創造

**核心問題：** 人為什麼創造能夠擴展自身能力並改變世界的事物？

**獨有思想內容：**

- 人不只是適應世界，也透過創造改變自身與世界的關係。
- 創造源於需求，也源於好奇、想像、探索、表達與合作。
- 工具、機器與技術將人的能力延伸到身體之外。
- 創造使原本不存在的可能性進入現實。
- 人的創造不僅改變能做什麼，也可能改變人的活動方式。

**本章思想成果：** 建立 Creation｜創造作為人類擴展可能性、改變世界的基本活動。

**概念邊界：** 不在本章完整判斷創造的價值，不研究具體裝置的物理存在，也不提前展開責任理論。

## 1.1 The Need to Create｜創造的需要

> Approved & Locked v1.1｜已批准並鎖定。日期｜2026-10-14。作者解鎖並以 `CH01_1.1_The_Need_to_Create_v1.1_LOCKED.md` 覆蓋正文。自 v1.0 改兩處：第三段刪去宣布語；第五段末句改為可能性浮現，且早於任何新事物形成。其餘段落不變。English words｜513（僅計七段英文正文；不含標題、中文及編輯註；縮約詞計一詞）。
> **Source｜來源：** 作者鎖定稿，2026-10-14。v1.0 鎖定於 2026-10-12。

### 英文版

Human beings enter a world that already exists. Long before we build anything, there are rivers to cross, seasons to endure, distances to travel, and conditions we cannot choose. Our bodies allow us to move, carry, reach, and work, but they also have limits. We learn to live within these conditions, sometimes by adapting our actions to them, and sometimes by finding ways to change what those conditions allow us to do.

Consider a river separating two communities. For generations, people may have crossed it by boat or traveled a longer route to reach the other side. The river is not necessarily a problem to be eliminated. It is part of the landscape and part of the lives built around it. When people construct a bridge, they do not remove the river. They create another way of living and acting in relation to it.

The need to create often appears when people recognize a difference between what they wish to accomplish and what their present circumstances make possible. A journey takes too long. A task requires more effort than one person can provide. An existing arrangement prevents people from working together as they would like. Such difficulties may lead to a new tool, a different arrangement, or a shared method that did not exist before. People are not limited to repeating the ways they have already learned.

Yet the need itself is not always obvious. People may follow a familiar routine for years without thinking of it as difficult. They know how to perform the work, and the effort it requires has become part of what they expect. Then someone notices another way. The task has not suddenly become harder, nor has the world necessarily changed. What has changed is the person's understanding of what might be possible.

We do not always begin with a clearly defined problem and then search for an invention. Sometimes a new possibility allows us to recognize a need that we had never named. What once seemed natural or unavoidable becomes something we can reconsider. The familiar world has not disappeared, but it can now be understood differently. The possibility of creating something new may first come into view at this moment, before anything new has taken shape.

Still, recognizing a need does not mean that something new must be made. People can respond by changing a habit, sharing a responsibility, or using what they already have in a different way. Not every adjustment is a creation, and not every invention is necessary. Creation involves developing a new arrangement, method, or thing. It is one way of responding to the world, not the only way.

We create within a world that both supports and limits our actions. Its materials, forces, and conditions are not simply obstacles standing in our way; they are also what make creation possible. We cannot choose every condition of our existence, but neither are we confined to the ways of acting we already know. Even within the world as it is, there are ways of living and acting that we have yet to discover.

### 繁體中文版

人來到一個早已存在的世界。遠在人類開始建造任何事物之前，河流便需要跨越，季節需要經歷，路途需要跋涉，還有許多我們無法選擇的條件。身體使我們能夠移動、搬運、觸及事物與工作，但身體也有其限制。我們學習在這些條件中生活，有時調整自己的行動以適應它們，有時則尋找方法，改變這些條件所允許的行動。

想像一條河流分隔著兩個社區。世世代代，人們可能乘船渡河，也可能繞行更遠的道路抵達對岸。河流本身不一定是需要消除的問題。它是地貌的一部分，也是周圍生活的一部分。當人們建造橋樑，他們並沒有消除河流，而是創造了另一種與河流相處和行動的方式。

創造的需要，往往在人們察覺到想要完成的事情與現有條件所允許的可能之間存在差距時出現。一段旅程耗時過長，一項工作需要超出個人所能提供的力量，或既有安排妨礙人們以期望的方式合作。這些困難可能促使人創造新的工具、不同的安排，或過去尚不存在的共同方法。人並非只能重複已經學會的行動方式。

然而，需要本身並不總是顯而易見。人們可能多年來都遵循熟悉的做法，從未認為它有什麼困難。他們知道如何完成工作，也早已習慣其中需要付出的勞動。直到有人注意到另一種方法。工作並沒有突然變得更困難，世界也未必發生了變化。真正改變的，是人對可能性的認識。

我們並不總是先有一個明確的問題，再去尋找某項發明。有時，是新的可能性使我們認識到一種過去從未說明的需要。原本看似自然或不可避免的事情，開始變得可以重新思考。熟悉的世界並沒有消失，但我們已能以不同的方式理解它。創造新事物的可能性，或許就在這一刻開始浮現，甚至早於任何新事物形成之時。

然而，察覺一種需要，並不意味著一定要製造新的事物。人們可以改變習慣、共同分擔工作，或以不同方式使用已有的東西。並非每一項調整都是創造，也並非每一項發明都有其必要。創造涉及新的安排、方法或事物。創造是人回應世界的一種方式，卻不是唯一的方式。

我們在一個既支持也限制自身行動的世界中創造。世界的材料、力量與條件，不只是阻擋我們的障礙，也是使創造成為可能的基礎。我們無法選擇自身存在的每一項條件，卻也不必局限於早已熟悉的行動方式。即使世界有其既定的條件，仍有許多生活與行動的方式，等待我們去發現。

## 1.2 Beyond Necessity｜超越生存需要

> Approved & Locked v1.0｜已批准並鎖定。日期｜2026-10-15。作者鎖定稿覆蓋 Candidate v0.1，未改句子。English words｜694（僅計八段英文正文；不含標題、中文及編輯註；縮約詞計一詞）。
> **Source｜來源：** 作者鎖定稿 `CH01_1.2_Beyond_Necessity_v1.0_LOCKED.md`。稿內註明由 Candidate v0.1 依指定段落修訂。

### 英文版

Solving a problem does not bring human creation to an end. A machine may complete a difficult task, yet its success can prompt another question: what else might it do? The ability to accomplish something once considered difficult often changes what people are willing to attempt next. We do not stop creating simply because our immediate needs have been met. We begin to consider capabilities that our present circumstances do not require, and possibilities that no familiar task has yet given us reason to pursue.

This pursuit does not always begin with something that has gone wrong. People may wish to observe what lies beyond their sight, understand phenomena too small or distant to experience directly, or discover patterns that ordinary attention cannot recognize. They may not know what these discoveries will make possible, or whether they will lead to any practical application. Exploration can begin before its purpose is fully understood. When familiar ways of observing and understanding cannot reach what people wish to explore, inquiry can become a reason to create new capabilities.

There is a difference between solving a problem with capabilities we already possess and seeking capabilities that do not yet exist. In the first case, the goal may be clear even when the solution is uncertain. In the second, both the capability and its future uses may remain unknown. People begin to imagine not only new ways of performing familiar tasks, but also actions they have never been able to undertake. The question gradually changes from how to accomplish a known task to what new kinds of action might become possible.

Yet people do not seek new capabilities only to exercise them personally. We also create things that can carry out actions without requiring our direct involvement at every moment. A machine may continue performing a task while a person turns attention elsewhere. The person may still establish its purpose, determine when it should operate, and decide when intervention is necessary. What changes is the need to perform every action directly. Creation thus opens another possibility: some actions can be entrusted to the things we build.

Many machines perform work according to mechanisms or instructions established in advance. Some can also detect changes and adjust their operation through predetermined rules or feedback; such responses do not in themselves require artificial intelligence. A further possibility is to build systems that can learn from experience or respond to situations not exhaustively specified beforehand. This raises a different question: what capabilities can we create when the conditions of a system’s operation cannot be fully anticipated?

Artificial intelligence gives these questions new significance. We can build systems that, within particular limits, interpret information, learn from data or experience, and produce judgments or decisions without having every possible response individually specified in advance. These capabilities remain dependent on human design, training, available information, and the conditions in which the systems operate. Yet they allow us to explore something beyond the execution of a fully predetermined sequence: artificial systems that can select or adjust some of their actions in response to changing circumstances.

The ability to act without continuous human direction is not the same as independence from human purposes, nor does it make every system intelligent. Automatic execution alone does not establish intelligence; neither a response to changing conditions nor the ability to learn, taken alone, provides a complete definition of it. Artificial intelligence introduces different possibilities, but not a universal destination for human creation. Whether a particular capability is useful, appropriate, or worth creating remains a separate question. What matters here is that the relationship between people and their creations can extend beyond direct operation.

We create not only in response to what the world requires of us, but also in pursuit of capabilities that have not yet been realized. Some extend what we can do ourselves. Others allow the things we build to carry out actions that once required our continuous involvement. As we explore systems capable of perceiving, learning, judging, and acting under changing conditions, the question becomes broader. We are no longer asking only what our creations can help us do, but also what they may become capable of doing.

### 繁體中文版

解決一個問題，並不意味著人的創造就此結束。機器或許已經完成一項困難的工作，但它的成功又可能引發另一個問題：它還能做些什麼？當原本困難的事情變得可以完成，人也常會重新思考接下來願意嘗試什麼。我們不會僅僅因為眼前的需要得到滿足，就停止創造。我們開始思考現有處境尚未要求我們具備的能力，以及熟悉的工作尚未使我們有理由追尋的可能性。

這種追求不一定始於某件事情出了差錯。人可能希望觀察視線之外的事物，理解那些過於微小或遙遠、無法直接經驗的現象，或發現一般注意力難以辨認的規律。人未必知道這些發現將帶來什麼可能，也未必知道它們最終是否具有實際用途。探索可以早於明確目的的形成。當既有的觀察與理解方式不足以支持人想要進行的探索，探究本身便可能成為創造新能力的理由。

運用已經具備的能力解決問題，與追求尚未存在的能力，兩者有所不同。在前一種情況中，即使解決方法尚不確定，目標仍可能十分清楚；在後一種情況中，新的能力及其未來用途都可能尚未可知。人開始想像的，不只是完成熟悉工作的新方法，也包括過去從未能夠採取的行動。問題逐漸從如何完成已知的工作，轉向哪些新的行動可能成為現實。

然而，人追求新的能力，不只是為了親自行使它們。我們也創造能夠執行某些行動，而不必時刻由人直接參與的事物。機器可以在持續執行工作的同時，讓人將注意力轉向其他事情。人仍可能確定它的目的、決定何時運行，以及何時需要介入。改變的是，人不再必須親自完成每一個動作。創造因此開啟了另一種可能：某些行動可以交由我們創造的事物承擔。

許多機器依照預先設定的運作機制或指令完成工作。有些機器也能偵測變化，並透過既定規則或回饋調整運作；這類回應本身並不需要人工智能。進一步的可能，是創造能夠從經驗中學習，或回應事先未能窮盡指定之情況的系統。這引出另一個問題：當系統的運行條件無法被完全預見時，我們能夠創造什麼樣的能力？

人工智能使這些問題獲得了新的意義。我們能夠建構一些系統，使它們在特定限制下解讀資訊、從資料或經驗中學習，並在不必事先逐一指定所有可能回應的情況下形成判斷或決策。這些能力仍取決於人的設計、訓練、可用資訊，以及系統實際運行的條件。然而，它們使我們得以探索超越完全預定之行動順序的可能：人工系統可以隨情境變化，選擇或調整自身的部分行動。

無須人持續指揮就能執行行動，不等於脫離人的目的而獨立存在，也不意味著每個這樣的系統都具有智能。單憑自動執行並不足以證明智能的存在；對變化作出回應或具備學習能力，也都不能單獨構成智能的完整定義。人工智能開啟了不同的可能，但它並不是人類創造的唯一終點。某項能力是否有用、是否適當、是否值得創造，仍是另一個需要判斷的問題。此處重要的是，人與創造物的關係可以超越直接操作。

我們創造，不只是為了回應世界對我們提出的要求，也為了追求尚未實現的能力。有些創造延伸了我們親自能做的事，另一些則使我們創造的事物能夠承擔過去需要人持續參與的行動。當我們探索能夠在變化的條件下感知、學習、判斷與行動的系統時，問題也隨之擴展。我們不再只是追問創造物能幫助人做什麼，也開始追問它們本身可能具備什麼樣的能力。

## 1.3 Extending Human Capability｜延伸人的能力

> Approved & Locked v1.0｜已批准並鎖定。日期｜2026-10-15。正文與 Candidate v0.4 逐字相同，只更新版本與鎖定狀態。English words｜576（僅計七段英文正文；不含標題、中文及編輯註；縮約詞計一詞）。
> **Source｜來源：** 作者鎖定稿 `CH01_1.3_Extending_Human_Capability_v1.0_LOCKED.md`。v0.3 → v0.4 刪除第五段一組重複英中句子；v0.4 → v1.0 作者批准，正文不變。

### 英文版

The human body allows us to act within the world, but every bodily ability has a range. Our hands can grasp and shape objects, yet they cannot bear every weight. Our eyes can distinguish forms and movements, yet much of the physical world remains beyond their reach. We can sustain effort for hours, but not without rest. These limits are part of being human. They also help explain why so much of what we accomplish depends on things we have created.

Technology does more than increase the strength or reach of the body. A remotely operated underwater vehicle allows researchers to observe and collect samples at depths they cannot safely reach themselves. Instruments aboard a spacecraft gather measurements from places no human has visited and transmit the results across great distances. In neither case does the human body acquire the ability to withstand deep-sea pressure or travel through space. Instead, observation, measurement, and, where the equipment permits, physical action become possible beyond the body's direct reach.

What matters in these examples is not simply that machines can operate where people cannot. An instrument may collect measurements beyond the reach of human senses, but those measurements become part of human inquiry only when they can be interpreted and used. A remotely operated vehicle may perform an action beyond the reach of the human body, yet the person directing it does not acquire the machine's physical capabilities. The machine has its own capabilities; the person brings purpose, knowledge, and judgment. What extends human capability is the relationship through which these different contributions make an action possible.

For this relationship to support action, people need ways to direct a machine and understand its responses. Equipment may perform its intended functions, yet remain difficult to use when its controls provide no clear indication of what has happened or how to proceed. Even when the technical operation succeeds, people may be unable to direct it effectively without adequate feedback. Controls and feedback allow a machine’s performance to become part of purposeful human action.

Technology also allows human activities to extend across distance and time. An engineer can send instructions to equipment operating far away. Measurements taken in one place can guide decisions made in another. Such arrangements do not erase the need for human knowledge or judgment. They allow people to act without being physically present and to use observations long after they were made. Human intentions and actions can thus have effects beyond the immediate presence of an individual.

Acting at a distance also changes the information and timing on which an action depends. A command may arrive after circumstances have changed; a measurement may describe an earlier state rather than what is happening now. These differences matter when people direct equipment beyond their immediate reach. Extending action across distance therefore requires attention not only to what a machine can do, but also to when information becomes available and when an action can take effect.

Human action no longer has to end where the body can reach. We can observe places we cannot visit, obtain information beyond our senses, and bring about physical actions without performing every movement ourselves. These possibilities do not make the human body unlimited, nor do they free our creations from the conditions of the physical world. They change where and how human action can take place. What extends beyond the body is not the body itself, but the range of what people can accomplish.

### 繁體中文版

人的身體使我們能夠在世界中行動，但每一種身體能力都有其範圍。雙手能夠抓取與塑造事物，卻無法承受所有重量。眼睛能夠辨認形狀與運動，但物理世界中仍有許多事物超出視覺所及。我們可以持續工作數小時，卻不能不休息。這些限制是人的存在條件之一，也使我們理解，為什麼我們所完成的許多事情，都依賴自己創造的事物。

技術所做的，不只是增強身體的力量或延伸其觸及範圍。遠端操作的水下探測器，使研究人員能夠在自身無法安全抵達的深度進行觀察與採樣。太空探測器上的儀器，能夠從人類尚未到達的地方取得測量資料，並跨越遙遠距離將結果傳回。這些技術都沒有使人的身體獲得承受深海壓力或穿越太空的能力。相反地，觀察、測量，以及在設備具備相應功能時的物理行動，得以在身體無法直接觸及的地方實現。

這些例子的重要之處，不只是機器能夠在人無法到達的地方運行。儀器可以取得人的感官無法直接獲得的測量資料，但這些資料只有在能夠被解讀與運用時，才成為人類探索的一部分。遠端操作設備可以在人體無法觸及的地方執行行動，但指揮它的人並不因此獲得機器自身的物理能力。機器具有自身的能力；人則帶來目的、知識與判斷。真正延伸人的能力的，是這些不同因素得以共同促成行動的關係。

要使這種關係真正支持人的行動，人需要能夠指揮機器並理解其回應的方式。設備或許能夠完成預定功能，但如果控制介面無法清楚顯示已經發生了什麼，以及接下來可以如何操作，人仍可能難以使用它。即使技術操作本身成功，缺乏充分的回饋仍可能使人無法有效控制。操作方式與回饋，使機器的功能表現得以成為人有目的之行動的一部分。

技術也使人的活動得以跨越距離與時間。工程師可以向遠方運行的設備發送指令。一個地方取得的測量資料，可以成為另一個地方作出決策的依據。這些安排並沒有消除對人的知識或判斷的需要。它們使人無須親臨現場也能採取行動，並能在觀察發生很久之後繼續運用所取得的資訊。人的意圖與行動，因此能夠在個人身體未能直接到達的地方產生作用。

在遠方行動，也會改變行動所依賴的資訊與時間條件。指令抵達時，當地情況可能已經改變；測量資料描述的可能是較早的狀態，而不是當下正在發生的事情。當人指揮身體無法直接觸及的設備時，這些差異便具有實際意義。因此，使行動跨越距離，不只需要考慮機器能完成什麼，也必須留意資訊何時可以取得，以及行動何時能夠產生效果。

人的行動不再必須止於身體所能觸及的地方。我們可以觀察無法親自到達的場所，取得超出自身感官範圍的資訊，也能在不必親自完成每個動作的情況下，使物理行動得以發生。這些可能性並不使人的身體變得無所不能，也不使我們的創造物脫離物理世界的條件。它們改變的是人的行動可以在何處、以何種方式發生。延伸到身體之外的，不是身體本身，而是人所能完成之事的範圍。

## 1.4 From Possibility to Reality｜從可能到現實

### 英文版

Human beings can imagine things that do not yet exist. A new device, a software application, or a system capable of responding to changing conditions may first appear as an idea. We can describe what it might accomplish without knowing how to make it work. Yet being able to imagine something does not establish that it can be realized. Between an idea and its existence in the world lies a question that creation must answer: under what conditions can this possibility become real?

An idea often leaves its most important conditions unspecified. A physical device requires components and mechanisms through which it can perform its intended functions. Software requires a way to represent information, define behavior, and execute instructions. An artificial system may require both, connecting information processing with actions in the physical world. These creations take different forms, but each requires more than a description of its intended purpose. What has been imagined must be expressed in a form whose behavior can be examined.

Consider software intended to help people coordinate activities within a shared space. The initial idea may seem straightforward: recognize relevant changes, determine what needs to happen, and provide an appropriate response. But implementation raises questions the idea alone cannot settle. What information is available? How should conflicting instructions be handled? What happens when a message arrives late or a device does not respond? A design that appears complete in description may prove incomplete when different events occur together.

The same challenge appears in physical creations, though its details differ. A sensor may behave differently under changing temperatures. A mechanism may succeed in one trial but fail when repeated. Software may produce an unexpected result when inputs arrive in an unfamiliar order. An intelligent system may perform well with familiar data yet respond poorly to conditions outside its tested range. These outcomes reveal what the original idea did not account for. They provide reasons to examine the assumptions on which the creation was based.

As these assumptions are tested, the creation itself may change. A function once considered essential may be simplified, or an approach may be replaced when experience reveals its limitations. The resulting creation may no longer resemble the first proposal in every detail. Such changes do not necessarily mean that its original purpose has been lost. Sometimes that purpose becomes clearer as the initial idea is tested and revised through experience.

Yet a successful demonstration does not mean a creation is ready for its intended use. Software that produces the expected result in one test may fail under different inputs. A connected device may respond correctly once but behave differently when communication is interrupted. Testing under different conditions helps establish which behaviors can be expected and where the limits of dependable operation lie. A single success demonstrates what may be possible; reliable use requires evidence that the expected behavior can be sustained under the conditions for which the creation is intended.

Creation therefore involves more than carrying an idea unchanged into the world. An imagined possibility must acquire a form in which it can be examined, challenged, and revised. What becomes real is shaped not only by what people intended, but also by what they discover while attempting to realize it. The movement from possibility to reality is not simply the completion of an original thought. It is a process through which thought encounters evidence, and what can actually exist becomes clearer.

### 繁體中文版

人能夠想像尚未存在的事物。一台新的裝置、一套軟體應用程式，或一個能夠回應變化條件的系統，都可能首先以構想的形式出現。我們可以描述它可能完成什麼，卻未必知道如何使它實際運作。然而，能夠想像某件事物，並不代表它已具備實現的可能。從構想到它在世界中的實際存在，還有一個創造必須回答的問題：需要具備什麼條件，這種可能性才能成為現實？

構想往往尚未說明實現它所需的關鍵條件。一台實體裝置需要相應的元件與機構，才能執行預定功能。軟體則需要表示資訊、定義行為及執行指令的方式；人工系統可能同時需要兩者，使資訊處理與物理世界中的行動建立聯繫。這些創造具有不同形式，但都不能只依靠對預期用途的描述。想像中的事物必須取得一種能夠檢驗其實際行為的形式。

以一套協助人們協調共同空間活動的軟體為例。最初的構想可能相當直接：辨認相關變化、判斷需要發生什麼，並作出適當回應。但實際實現時，便會出現單憑構想無法解決的問題。哪些資訊可以取得？相互衝突的指令應如何處理？訊息延遲或設備沒有回應時，又會發生什麼？一項在描述中看似完整的設計，可能在不同事件同時發生時，才顯露出尚未考慮的部分。

實體創造也面臨相同的挑戰，只是具體情況有所不同。感測器可能在溫度變化時呈現不同的表現；機構可能在一次試驗中成功，重複運作時卻失敗；軟體可能因輸入資料以未曾預期的順序到達而產生異常結果；智能系統可能在熟悉的資料上表現良好，面對測試範圍之外的條件時卻無法妥善回應。這些結果揭示了原始構想未曾考慮的情況，也使人有理由重新檢視創造所依據的假設。

隨著這些假設接受檢驗，創造本身也可能改變。原本認為不可缺少的功能可能得到簡化；當實際經驗揭示某種方法的限制時，人也可能改用另一種方法。最終形成的創造，不一定在每個細節上都與原始構想相同。這些改變不一定意味著原本的目的已經失去。有時，正是在最初構想接受實際檢驗並不斷修正的過程中，創造的目的才逐漸變得清晰。

然而，一次成功的展示，並不意味著某項創造已經能夠用於預定用途。軟體在一次測試中產生預期結果，面對不同輸入時仍可能失敗。連網裝置可能正確回應一次指令，卻在通訊中斷時呈現不同的行為。在不同條件下進行測試，有助於確認哪些行為可以預期，以及可靠運行的界限何在。一次成功只能展示某種可能；要達到可靠使用，還需要證據證明創造物能在預定條件下持續呈現預期行為。

因此，創造不只是將一個構想原封不動地帶入世界。想像中的可能性必須取得可以接受檢驗、質疑與修正的形式。最終成為現實的事物，不只受到人原本意圖的影響，也受到實現過程中新發現的事實所塑造。從可能走向現實，不只是完成最初的想法，更是一個思想不斷面對實際證據，並逐漸認清什麼能夠真實成立的過程。

## 1.5 Creation Changes Us｜創造也改變我們

> **Source｜來源：** 作者鎖定稿 `CH01_1.5_Creation_Changes_Us_v1.0_LOCKED.md`。稿內註明來自 Candidate v0.4：八段正文；僅第四段中文修正、第六段英中末句修正。

### 英文版

When people create something new, they usually begin with what they hope it will make possible. A tool may reduce effort, a machine may perform an unfamiliar task, and software may help people organize information or coordinate their activities. Yet the effects of creation do not end when the intended function has been achieved. Once something becomes part of daily life, it can change the activities for which it was made, along with the habits and abilities of the people who use it.

Consider how navigation has changed with digital maps. Travelers once relied more heavily on printed maps, familiar landmarks, directions from others, and their own memory of a route. Digital navigation makes it possible to find unfamiliar destinations, compare routes, and respond to changing traffic conditions with less preparation. But the change reaches beyond finding the way. People may begin a journey with less knowledge of the surrounding area, follow instructions more closely, or pay attention to different features of the road. The journey remains a human activity, but the way it is undertaken has changed.

Changes in action can gradually become changes in habit. At first, a new tool or system may require people to learn unfamiliar procedures and consciously adjust what they do. With repeated use, those procedures can become familiar, and what once required deliberate attention may become an ordinary part of daily activity. People may begin to expect information, assistance, or results that were previously unavailable. Over time, the new way of acting may no longer feel new. It becomes one of the ways people ordinarily approach the world.

The capabilities people develop may change as well. A task that once required a particular skill may become easier with technological assistance, while new skills become important for using that assistance effectively. Someone working with analytical software may spend less time performing calculations and more time deciding which questions to ask or how to interpret the results. Yet completing a task with assistance is not the same as developing the ability to perform or understand every part of it independently. Technology may expand what people can accomplish while changing which abilities they continue to develop, practice, or rely upon.

Creation can also change how people work together. A shared digital document allows people in different places to contribute to the same work without gathering around a single physical copy. Changes can be recorded, compared, and discussed as the work develops. This does more than accelerate the exchange of information. It can alter how contributions are organized, how unfinished work is shared, and how people coordinate their efforts. A new capability becomes part of a relationship among people, and that relationship may develop new practices of its own.

Such changes rarely follow a single direction. A creation may make an activity more accessible while introducing new difficulties. It may save time in one part of a task while requiring attention elsewhere. People may become accustomed to a capability that previous generations did not possess, until its presence seems ordinary and its absence becomes noticeable. What was once an invention can become part of the conditions under which people plan their activities. Over time, what people once regarded as a new possibility may become part of the ordinary circumstances of life.

This does not mean that every creation transforms human life, or that people must accept every change it makes possible. Some creations remain limited in use. Others are adapted, abandoned, or replaced as experience reveals what they offer and what they demand. Their influence depends not only on their functions, but also on the circumstances in which people encounter them and the ways they choose to use them. Human creation is therefore not a completed relationship between a maker and an object. Its consequences continue to develop through use.

We create to change what is possible. Yet as our creations become part of life, they also change the ways we act, the abilities we develop, and the practices we come to regard as ordinary. These changes may extend beyond what their makers originally imagined, and they remain open to further human choices. Creation is therefore more than the making of something new. Through what we create, we also take part in shaping the lives we will go on to live.

### 繁體中文版

當人創造新的事物時，通常首先想到的是它能帶來什麼可能。一件工具可以減輕勞動，一台機器可以執行原本難以完成的工作，一套軟體可以協助人們整理資訊或協調活動。然而，創造的影響並不在預期功能實現時便告結束。當某項創造進入日常生活，它不只可能改變原本為之設計的活動，也可能改變使用者的習慣與能力。

以數位地圖帶來的變化為例。過去，旅行者更多依靠紙本地圖、熟悉的地標、他人的指引，以及自己對路線的記憶。數位導航讓人能以較少的事前準備找到陌生目的地、比較路線，並因應交通情況的變化。但改變不只發生在尋路這件事上。人們可能在不熟悉周圍環境的情況下開始旅程，更加依循導航指示，或將注意力放在道路的不同特徵上。旅程仍是人的活動，但人完成旅程的方式已經改變。

行動方式的改變，可能逐漸成為習慣的改變。起初，新的工具或系統可能要求人們學習不熟悉的程序，並有意識地調整自己的行動。隨著反覆使用，這些程序逐漸變得熟悉，過去需要刻意留意的事情，也可能成為日常活動的一部分。人們開始期待以往無法取得的資訊、協助或結果。隨著時間推移，新的行動方式可能不再令人感到新奇，而成為人們日常面對世界時自然採取的方式。

人所發展的能力也可能隨之改變。原本需要特定技能才能完成的工作，可能因技術協助而變得容易；同時，有效運用技術協助所需的新能力，也可能變得重要。使用分析軟體的人，可能花較少時間進行計算，轉而花更多時間判斷應該提出哪些問題，或如何解讀結果。然而，借助技術完成一項工作，並不等於人也因此發展出獨立完成或理解其中每個部分的能力。技術可能擴展人所能完成的事情，同時改變他們持續發展、經常運用或需要依靠的能力。

創造也可能改變人們合作的方式。共享數位文件讓身處不同地方的人，不必圍繞同一份實體文件，也能共同參與一項工作。隨著工作推進，修改可以被記錄、比較與討論。這不只是加快資訊交換，也可能改變貢獻如何組織、尚未完成的工作如何分享，以及人們如何協調彼此的努力。一種新的能力進入人與人的關係，而這些關係也可能逐漸形成新的共同做法。

這些改變很少只朝單一方向發展。一項創造可能使某種活動更容易參與，同時帶來新的困難；它可能節省某部分工作的時間，卻要求人在其他地方投入注意力。人們可能逐漸習慣前人未曾擁有的能力，直到它的存在變得平常，反而在失去它時才有所察覺。原本的一項發明，可能成為人們規劃活動時所依據的條件。隨著時間推移，人們曾經視為新可能性的事物，可能逐漸成為日常生活條件的一部分。

這並不意味著每一項創造都會改變人的生活，也不意味著人必須接受創造所帶來的每一種變化。有些創造的用途始終有限；另一些則隨著人們逐漸認識其作用與要求，而被調整、放棄或取代。創造的影響不只取決於它具有什麼功能，也取決於人們在什麼情況下接觸它，以及選擇如何使用它。因此，人的創造並不是創造者與造物之間一種已經完成的關係。它的後續影響，仍會在使用過程中持續發展。

我們創造，是為了改變什麼事情能夠成為可能。然而，當創造進入生活，它也改變我們的行動方式、所發展的能力，以及逐漸視為日常的做法。這些變化可能超出創造者最初的想像，也仍然受到人後續選擇的影響。因此，創造不只是製造新的事物。透過我們所創造的一切，我們也參與塑造自己將要繼續過的生活。
