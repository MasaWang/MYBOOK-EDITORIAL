# 第三章——超越我們的意圖

**核心問題：** 為什麼我們的創造所產生的後果可能超越原始意圖？

**獨有思想內容：**

- 創造意圖與實際結果並不必然一致。
- 創造物進入世界後，可能被他人重新使用或賦予新用途。
- 不同事物、系統與社會活動的結合可能產生新的效果。
- 創造可能帶來未曾預見的利益，也可能形成風險。
- 一項創造的影響可能超越創造者的直接控制。
- 技術結果應在具體使用和世界關係中理解，而不只依據設計意圖。

**本章思想成果：** 建立 Intention–Consequence Distinction｜意圖與後果的區分。

**概念邊界：** 研究意圖與結果為何分離，而不是全面討論誰應承擔責任。

## 3.1 The Intention to Create｜創造的意圖

### 英文版

Human intentions give creation a direction. They connect what people hope to accomplish with the decisions they make about what to bring into the world. An intention may express a need, a purpose, or an expectation, but it becomes part of creation only through the choices and actions that follow. Design is one way these intentions enter the work of making. It is where what people mean to achieve begins to take a form that can be examined, developed, and eventually realized.

To turn an intention into action, people must work within the conditions of the physical world. Materials have their properties, tools have their limits, and different requirements cannot always be satisfied together. What people wish to accomplish must therefore be pursued through choices about how to act and what means to use. These choices are not merely steps toward a predetermined result. Each opens a possible path while setting others aside. What can be realized depends not only on what people intend, but also on what the world allows them to do.

A purpose gives people a reason to create, but realizing that purpose requires more than completing a set of tasks. People establish goals to define what should be achieved and to judge whether their efforts have succeeded. Such goals may be clear, measurable, and entirely reasonable. Yet reaching them does not necessarily mean that the purpose behind them has been fulfilled. A creation may perform exactly as specified without bringing about what people originally hoped to accomplish. The distinction matters because success in what we have chosen to measure is not always success in what we meant to achieve.

People create according to their understanding of the world. They anticipate how things will be used, what others may know, how people are likely to act, and which conditions can reasonably be expected. Some of these expectations are stated openly; others remain assumptions that are rarely questioned. Such assumptions allow people to make decisions without having to examine every possibility. Yet they are formed within particular experiences, circumstances, and ways of life. What seems ordinary to one person may be unfamiliar to another, and conditions taken for granted in one setting may be absent in another. Every act of creation therefore draws upon an understanding of the world that is useful, but necessarily situated.

Nor is the intention behind a creation necessarily the intention of a single person. What is made through cooperation often brings together different forms of knowledge, practical concerns, and expectations. People may work toward a shared purpose while understanding its meaning differently. Some decisions reflect deliberate agreement; others emerge from compromises or from the work of bringing separate contributions together. The resulting creation may therefore embody more than any one participant set out to express. Its design can be coherent without being the expression of a single, complete intention. To understand why something was made, we may need to consider how several intentions came to take form within one creation.

An intention is thus both a beginning and a boundary of understanding. It helps explain why a creation takes the form it does, which purposes guided its development, and what its makers expected of it. Design gives intention form, but what takes form is more than intention. A creation acquires properties, capacities, and a place among other things and people. To understand what has been created, we must look beyond why it was made and consider what now exists in the world.

### 繁體中文版

人的意圖為創造提供方向。它將人們希望實現的事情，與他們決定把什麼帶入世界聯繫起來。意圖可以表達需要、目的或期待，但只有透過隨後的選擇與行動，才能真正參與創造。設計是意圖進入創造過程的一種方式，使人們希望實現的事情開始取得可以被研究、發展並最終實現的形式。

要將意圖付諸行動，人必須面對物理世界的現實條件。材料具有自身的性質，工具有其限制，不同的要求也未必能同時得到滿足。因此，人們必須透過具體選擇，決定如何行動，以及採用哪些手段來實現目的。這些選擇不只是通往預定結果的步驟。每一項選擇都開啟一條可能的路徑，同時排除其他路徑。能夠實現什麼，不只取決於人的意圖，也取決於現實世界允許人做什麼。

目的使人有理由進行創造，但要實現這個目的，並不只是完成一系列工作。人們設定目標，以明確界定應當達成什麼，並判斷自己的努力是否成功。這些目標可以清晰、可衡量，也完全合理。然而，達成目標並不必然意味著背後的目的已經實現。一項創造可能完全符合既定要求，卻沒有帶來人們最初希望實現的結果。這項區分之所以重要，是因為我們在所選擇的衡量標準上取得成功，並不總是意味著實現了真正希望達成的事情。

人依據自己對世界的理解進行創造。他們預想事物將如何被使用、他人可能知道什麼、人們通常會如何行動，以及哪些條件可以合理地預期。其中一些期待會被明確表達，另一些則成為很少受到質疑的預設。這些預設使人們不必檢查每一種可能性，也能作出決定。然而，它們總是在特定的經驗、處境與生活方式中形成。對一個人而言理所當然的事，對另一個人可能十分陌生；在某種情境下被視為既定的條件，在另一種情境中也可能並不存在。因此，每一項創造都依賴著某種對世界的理解。這種理解具有實際作用，卻始終植根於特定的處境之中。

而且，一項創造背後的意圖，也未必只屬於某一個人。透過合作形成的事物，往往結合了不同的知識、實際考量與期待。人們可能朝向共同目的努力，卻對其意義有著不同的理解。有些決定來自有意識的共識，另一些則源於妥協，或在整合不同參與者的工作時逐漸形成。因此，最後的創造物可能承載了超出任何單一參與者原先所欲表達的內容。它的設計可以形成一致的整體，卻不必然是一個單一而完整的意圖的表現。要理解某件事物為何被創造，我們可能需要考察多種意圖如何共同在其中形成具體的樣貌。

因此，意圖既是理解創造的起點，也是這種理解的一道邊界。它幫助我們解釋一項創造為何具有現在的形式、哪些目的引導了它的發展，以及創造者原本對它抱有什麼期待。設計使意圖取得形式，但取得形式的事物，已不只是意圖。一項創造已具備自身的特性與能力，並在其他事物與人之間取得了某種位置。要理解一項創造，我們不能只看它為何被創造，還必須看它如今如何存在於世界之中。

## 3.2 A Life Beyond the Creator｜創造物自己的歷程

### 英文版

The completion of a creation marks an end to the work of making it, but not to its history. The moment something is finished, it can begin to serve purposes, enter daily routines, and become part of arrangements that continue over time. Its existence is no longer defined solely by the process that brought it into being. What follows may unfold over years, through circumstances and relationships that are not part of the original act of creation.

People come to understand created things through experience. Knowing what something is meant to do is not the same as knowing how it works in everyday life. Through repeated use, people discover its strengths and limitations, the attention it requires, and the conditions under which it can be relied upon. Some of this knowledge can be explained in advance; much of it develops through direct experience. A creation therefore becomes known not only through what its makers say about it, but also through what people learn from living with it.

Materials wear, parts fail, and the conditions required for continued use must be maintained. Care, repair, and adjustment become part of what allows a creation to remain useful. This work may be less visible than the work of making something new, but it is no less important to its continued presence. Making something is one achievement; keeping it useful in a changing world is another.

Time also changes the world around what has been made. The people who depend upon a creation may grow older, their needs may shift, and the circumstances in which they live may no longer be the same. Other things are introduced, older arrangements disappear, and what once fitted naturally into daily life may require adjustment. The creation itself need not change for its place in the world to change. Its significance depends partly on the relationships and conditions that surround it, and these do not remain still.

The people who create something may not remain part of its history for as long as the creation itself endures. They may leave their work, pass it to others, or belong to a time that has already passed. Yet what they made can remain, entering the lives of people who never knew them. Those who come afterward inherit more than a finished object. They encounter decisions made before their time and must decide how what remains will belong to their own. In this way, the history of a creation can pass from one generation to another without remaining bound to the lives of its makers.

The history of a creation is not contained in the thing alone. It also takes shape through the experience of those who live with it, the work required to sustain it, and the changing circumstances in which it remains present. To understand its history is therefore to understand not only what has endured, but also the relationships through which its presence has continued.

### 繁體中文版

一項創造的完成，標誌著製作工作的結束，卻不是它自身歷程的終點。當一件事物完成，它便可以開始發揮作用、進入日常生活，並成為隨時間持續存在的各種安排的一部分。它的存在不再僅由使它誕生的製作過程所界定。接下來的歷程可能跨越多年，在不同的處境與關係中展開，而這些並不屬於最初的創造活動。

人們透過經驗認識創造物。知道一件事物原本被用來做什麼，並不等於知道它在日常生活中如何發揮作用。透過反覆使用，人們逐漸了解它的長處與限制、需要留意的地方，以及在什麼條件下可以依賴它。其中一些知識可以事先說明，另一些則需要透過親身經驗才能獲得。因此，人們對一項創造的認識，不只來自創造者對它的說明，也來自與它共同生活的經驗。

材料會磨損，部件可能失效，持續使用所需要的條件也必須得到維持。照料、修復與調整，成為創造物得以繼續發揮作用的必要工作。這些工作或許不像創造新事物那樣引人注目，卻同樣關係到創造物能否持續存在。創造出一件事物是一種成就；使它在變化的世界中持續發揮作用，則是另一回事。

時間也會改變創造物周圍的世界。依賴它的人可能逐漸年長，需求可能轉變，生活條件也可能不再相同。新的事物不斷出現，舊有的安排逐漸消失，原本自然融入日常生活的事物，也可能需要重新調整。即使創造物本身沒有改變，它在世界中的位置仍可能發生變化。它的意義有一部分來自周圍的關係與條件，而這些關係與條件並不會永遠保持原樣。

創造者未必能始終參與一項創造的全部歷程。他們可能離開原有的工作，將它交給其他人，也可能早已屬於過去的時代。然而，他們所創造的事物仍可能留在世界中，進入從未認識過創造者的人們的生活。後來的人所承繼的，不只是一件已經完成的事物。他們也會面對前人留下的決定，並必須思考這些留存下來的事物，應如何成為自己所處時代的一部分。如此，一項創造的歷程便可能從一代人延續到另一代人，而不再受限於創造者自身的生命歷程。

一項創造的歷程，並不只存在於事物本身。它也透過與之共同生活的人們所累積的經驗、維持其作用所需要的工作，以及它持續存在其中的變化條件而形成。因此，理解它的歷程，不只要看什麼得以延續，也要理解它透過哪些關係持續存在於世界之中。

## 3.3 Unexpected Possibilities｜未曾預見的可能性

### 英文版

We often encounter familiar things without questioning what else they might make possible. Their established purposes guide our expectations, shaping what we notice and what we leave unexplored. Yet a different question can open another way of seeing what is already before us. Instead of asking only how something is ordinarily used, we may begin to ask what else can be done with it. The possibility has not necessarily appeared for the first time. It may be our attention that has found a new direction.

A new use often begins with a different human need. People encounter things while pursuing purposes that their makers did not share or could not have anticipated. They may recognize that an existing capability can serve a new task. The thing itself may remain unchanged, while the purpose for which it is used becomes different.

New possibilities often begin when people recognize relationships that had previously gone unnoticed. Things created for different purposes may share qualities, complement one another, or become useful together under new circumstances. Recognizing such connections allows people to explore combinations that were never part of the original plans. Yet bringing things together does not, by itself, produce something new. What matters is how their different qualities can contribute to a common activity. Through these relationships and combinations, people discover possibilities that might remain unnoticed when each creation is considered alone.

People do not always know the full range of what their creations can do. Certain properties may become apparent only under conditions that have not previously been examined. Experience, observation, and deliberate experimentation can reveal capabilities that were present but remained unrecognized. Such discoveries do not always change the creation itself. They change what people know about it, making new forms of action possible.

Yet recognizing a possibility is not the same as realizing it. Bringing a new use into practice may require exploration, making a combination work may call for adjustment, and a discovery may need further examination. Some possibilities remain ideas; others require changes in tools, knowledge, or the way people work together. Through these efforts, people may establish ways of acting that did not exist when the original creation was made. What was unexpected becomes something that can be understood, developed, and shared.

What people discover through creation can become available to others who face different questions. A new way of using something may provide knowledge for another undertaking, while a successful combination may make further work possible. The results of one effort can become the starting conditions of another, allowing people to build upon possibilities they did not have to discover for themselves. What has already been made can provide the means, knowledge, and opportunities from which further creation begins.

### 繁體中文版

我們經常接觸熟悉的事物，卻不一定追問它們還能帶來什麼可能。既定用途引導著我們的期待，也影響我們注意什麼，以及忽略什麼。然而，一個不同的問題，便可能使我們以另一種眼光看待眼前已有的事物。我們不再只問某件事物通常如何使用，也開始追問還能用它做些什麼。可能性未必是剛剛出現的；也許只是我們的注意力轉向了新的方向。

新的用途往往始於不同的人的需要。人們帶著創造者未必具有、也未必能夠預見的目的接觸既有事物。他們可能發現，某項已有的能力可以用來完成新的工作。事物本身可以保持不變，而人們使用它的目的卻已不同。

新的可能性，往往始於人們發現先前未曾注意到的關聯。為不同目的而創造的事物，可能具有某些共同特性、彼此互補，或在新的條件下共同發揮作用。認識這些聯繫，使人們得以探索最初計畫中未曾出現的組合。然而，僅僅將事物放在一起，並不會自然產生新的成果。重要的是，它們不同的特性如何共同參與某項活動。透過這些關聯與組合，人們得以發現單獨考察各項創造時可能未曾注意到的可能性。

人並不總能完整了解自己所創造的事物具有哪些能力。某些特性可能只有在先前未曾檢驗的條件下才顯現出來。經驗、觀察與有意識的實驗，能夠揭示原本已經存在、卻尚未被認識的能力。這類發現未必改變創造物本身，卻改變了人對它的認識，使新的行動方式成為可能。

然而，認識到一種可能性，並不等於已經實現它。將新的用途付諸實踐，可能需要探索；使新的組合發揮作用，可能需要調整；而新的發現，也可能需要進一步檢驗。有些可能性停留在想法之中，另一些則需要改變工具、知識，或人們相互合作的方式。透過這些努力，人們可能建立起創造物最初形成時尚不存在的行動方式。原本未曾預見的事情，於是成為可以被理解、發展與分享的可能。

人們在創造中獲得的發現，可以為面對不同問題的其他人所運用。一種新的使用方式，可能為另一項工作提供知識；一種成功的組合，也可能使後續工作成為可能。先前努力取得的成果，可以成為下一次創造的起始條件，使人們得以運用不必由自己重新發現的可能性。已經存在的創造，可以提供手段、知識與機會，使新的創造由此開始。

## 3.4 Consequences Beyond Control｜超越控制的後果

> **Source｜來源：** `CH03_3.4_Consequences_Beyond_Control_v1.0_LOCKED.md`。SHA-256 `fb86ccdaebb7baf5632a737b6bd6d9d1d3e79d436042e6a936342fda72398fe6`。來源為 Candidate v0.7，作者批准後未改正文。段落基準：P1 v0.6、P2 v0.5、P3 v0.7、P4 v0.5、P5 v0.5、P6 v0.6。

### 英文版

When we create something, we seek to give it a form and function that can be relied upon. A machine should perform its work, a structure should serve its purpose, and the things we make should behave within conditions we understand. Much of design is devoted to making this possible. We establish how something operates, what it can do, and where its limits should lie. Yet the control we exercise over a creation chiefly concerns how it operates. It does not necessarily extend to everything that happens because it works.

A creation can work exactly as intended and still bring about changes its makers did not anticipate. A machine that makes work easier may change how work is organized. A tool that becomes dependable may alter the skills people practice and the assistance they expect. These changes do not necessarily arise from defects or failures. They may occur because the creation succeeds and becomes part of ordinary life. Its intended function explains what it was designed to do, but not every consequence of people coming to rely upon it.

As a creation becomes part of everyday activity, its effects are shaped by more than the purposes for which it was designed. People adjust their work, expectations, and relationships around what they have come to rely upon. Others begin to make decisions on the assumption that it will remain available, and their work may depend on arrangements that have grown around it. What began as something people used can become something their activities depend upon. Its consequences now arise not only from what it does, but from how life has been organized around its presence.

Over time, these consequences may become less visible even as their influence grows. Habits formed around a creation can persist until people scarcely notice how much their activities depend on it. Arrangements once made for convenience may become difficult to change, while effects that seemed unimportant at first may acquire greater significance under new conditions. People may recognize these consequences only after they have begun to shape later choices. By then, changing the creation itself may no longer be enough to reverse the changes that have taken place around it.

This reveals a distinction between controlling a creation and controlling what follows from its use. We may determine how a machine operates, restrict its functions, or stop it altogether. Such control is real and often essential. But the habits, dependencies, and arrangements that have grown around it cannot always be changed by changing the machine. Those consequences have become part of relationships extending beyond the creation itself. Control over what we make is therefore not the same as control over what our creations bring about.

Recognizing this distinction does not make the creator powerless. The ability to change a creation remains important, but it is not always sufficient. We may also need to understand how people have come to use it, what their activities now depend upon, and which consequences can still be addressed. As circumstances change, our responses must take account of the world that has developed around what we made. What we bring into the world may continue to call for our attention long after it begins to work as designed.

### 繁體中文版

當我們創造事物，總希望賦予它可以信賴的形式與功能。機器應能完成工作，建築應能發揮用途，而我們製造的事物，也應在可以理解的條件下運作。設計中的許多努力，正是為了實現這些目標。我們確立事物如何運作、能夠做什麼，以及它應有的界限。然而，我們對創造物施加的控制，主要關乎它如何運作，卻未必涵蓋因其運作而發生的一切。

創造物即使完全按照預期運作，也可能帶來創造者未曾預見的改變。使工作更輕鬆的機器，可能改變工作的組織方式；值得依賴的工具，可能改變人們經常運用的技能，以及他們對協助的期待。這些改變未必源於缺陷或故障，反而可能因為創造物成功發揮作用，並逐漸成為日常生活的一部分。原定功能可以解釋它被設計來做什麼，卻無法解釋人們開始依賴它之後所產生的全部後果。

當創造物逐漸進入日常活動，它所產生的影響便不再只由原定目的決定。人們圍繞逐漸依賴的事物，調整工作、期待與彼此的關係。人們開始以它會持續存在為前提作出決定，工作也可能依賴圍繞它逐漸形成的安排。原本供人使用的事物，可能逐漸成為人們展開活動時所依賴的條件。此時，後果不只來自創造物做了什麼，也來自人的生活如何圍繞它而組織。

隨著時間推移，這些後果可能變得不那麼顯眼，即使它們的影響仍在增長。圍繞創造物形成的習慣可能持續存在，直到人們幾乎不再察覺自己的活動已經多麼依賴它。最初為了便利而建立的安排，後來可能變得難以改變；起初看似不重要的影響，也可能在新的條件下具有更大的意義。人們有時要等到這些後果已經開始影響後續選擇，才逐漸認識它們。到了那時，即使改變創造物本身，也未必足以逆轉它周圍已經發生的變化。

這揭示了控制創造物與控制其使用後果之間的區別。我們可以決定機器如何運作、限制它的功能，甚至使它完全停止。這些控制真實存在，而且往往不可或缺。然而，圍繞機器形成的習慣、依賴關係與各種安排，未必能夠隨著機器的改變而改變。這些後果已經進入超出創造物本身的關係之中。因此，控制我們製造的事物，並不等同於控制這些創造所帶來的後果。

認識這項區別，並不意味著創造者無能為力。改變創造物的能力仍然重要，但未必總是足夠。我們還可能需要理解人們如何開始使用它、他們的活動如今依賴什麼，以及哪些後果仍然可以處理。隨著條件改變，我們的回應也必須考慮圍繞創造物逐漸形成的現實。我們帶入世界的事物，即使早已按照設計運作，仍可能長久地需要我們的關注。
