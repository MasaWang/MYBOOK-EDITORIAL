## 章首

Humans have long built machines to extend what they can do in the physical world. Cars, ships, and aircraft carry us across land, over oceans, and through the sky. These creations do more than lighten our labor. They open possibilities for movement, work, and exploration that the human body alone could never achieve.

人類長久以來創造機器，以延伸自身在物理世界中的能力。汽車、輪船與飛機，載著我們穿越陸地、跨越海洋、飛越天空。這些創造不只是減輕人的勞動，更開啟了單憑人的身體無法實現的移動、工作與探索的可能。

As human knowledge and creativity have grown, so have the capabilities of the machines we build. Sensors detect changes in their surroundings, computers process information, and control systems coordinate complex actions. Artificial intelligence extends these capabilities further, enabling some machines to interpret information, learn from experience, and adapt their behavior. Human capabilities, intentions, and ways of acting enter machines through design. As machines evolve, they in turn expand what humans can do.

隨著人類知識與創造力的增長，我們所創造的機器也獲得了更多能力。感測器偵測周遭環境的變化，電腦處理資訊，控制系統協調複雜的行動。人工智能進一步擴展這些能力，使部分機器能夠解讀資訊、從經驗中學習，並調整自身行為。人的能力、意圖與行動方式，透過設計進入機器。隨著機器不斷演進，它們也反過來擴展人所能做到的事。

Yet creation changes more than what a person is able to do. A machine may carry us farther, and intelligence may reveal possibilities we could not previously see. Once those possibilities enter the world, the ways people move, work, and explore change with them.

然而，創造所改變的，不只是人能夠做什麼。機器可以帶我們走得更遠，智能可以揭示過去無法看見的可能性。當這些可能性進入世界，人移動、工作與探索的方式也隨之改變。
