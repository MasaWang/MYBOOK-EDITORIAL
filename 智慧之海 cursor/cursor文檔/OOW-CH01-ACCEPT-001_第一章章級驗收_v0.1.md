## 3. Continuity of reasoning｜全章推理連續性

**Editorially coherent｜編輯層面成立：**

1.1 distinguishes recognition of need from the existence of a new possibility; 1.2 moves beyond present necessity toward capabilities, delegated action, and AI; 1.3 establishes how human action may extend through created things; 1.4 examines how imagined capabilities obtain a practically testable form; 1.5 shows how lived actions, habits, individual capabilities and cooperation may in turn change.

The progression gives the chapter an intelligible beginning, middle, and end. The final paragraph of 1.5 returns to *possibility* and closes on humans participating in shaping the lives they continue to live; it does not itself supply the value criteria reserved for Chapter 2.

**Editorial observation｜編輯觀察：** Chapter 1 is predominantly about technological creation, despite the wider philosophical scope of “creation.” This is consistent with many examples and the book's technological direction, but should be recognized as a deliberate scope choice rather than assumed to define all forms of creation.

## 4. Cross-section duplication and boundaries｜跨節去重及思想邊界

**Finding F-01 — Structural baseline variance｜結構基準差異，Author decision needed｜須作者決議：** The approved 120-section baseline says 1.2 should study curiosity, imagination, exploration, expression, and shared ideals as drivers of creation. The prose is not automatically wrong, and the locked structural baseline must not be silently changed. Decide whether this is an accepted author-approved narrowing of the section's scope or whether a separately approved structural update is necessary. No text change recommended within this review.

**Finding F-02 — Chapter 3 boundary watch｜第三章邊界提醒，Monitor｜留待跨章審查：** 1.5 P7 mentions creations being adapted, abandoned or replaced as experience reveals their effects, and consequences developing through use. This may be read as adjacent to Chapter 3 §3.2–3.3 (uses beyond the creator). In Chapter 1, keep its reading restricted to changes in the activities and practices of human participants. It does not yet analyze independent new uses beyond makers. No immediate prose amendment required; check when drafting Chapter 3.

**Finding F-03 — Chapter 2 boundary｜第二章邊界，Pass｜通過：** The closing of 1.5 describes mutual shaping but does not decide which changes are valuable. This reserves explicit value criteria for Chapter 2 — The Human Measure.
