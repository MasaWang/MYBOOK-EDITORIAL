**EN:** Focus on how intelligence participates in the activities of a place. ## Chapter Opening｜章首（Approved v0.3）

### English｜英文
**Intelligence enters places where life is already unfolding.**

Human life unfolds in physical space. We inhabit places, move between them, and establish relationships through the activities we undertake. A home, a city, a forest, and an ocean differ greatly in scale and character, yet each brings together physical forms, living beings, and activities within a world that is continually changing. Space is where these relationships take place, where things coexist, and where actions acquire meaning through their surroundings.

Every space also exists within an environment. The environment includes the natural and human-made conditions that sustain, influence, and sometimes constrain what happens there. Air, water, light, energy, climate, and the activities of living things all participate in these conditions. Space and environment are inseparable in the physical world, but they bring different questions into view. Space draws our attention to where things exist and interact; environment draws our attention to the conditions that shape their existence and the consequences of their interactions.

When artificial intelligence enters physical space through artificial systems and devices, it begins to participate in these existing relationships. Its ability to sense, interpret, judge, and act can influence the conditions under which people and other living things carry out their activities. Yet participating in a space also means encountering purposes, relationships, and circumstances that the system did not create and may never fully understand.

The question, then, is how intelligence can become a meaningful participant in physical space. Its contribution must be examined through the activities it supports, the judgments it makes, and the consequences of its actions. Understanding intelligent space begins with understanding these relationships—and recognizing that every intervention takes place within a world already in motion.

### 繁體中文
**智能進入的，是生活早已展開的場所。**

人的生活展開於物理空間之中。我們居住於不同場所，在場所之間往來，並透過各種活動建立關係。家庭、城市、森林與海洋，在尺度與性質上各不相同，卻都在持續變化的世界中，容納著物理形態、生命與活動。空間是這些關係發生的所在，事物在其中共存，行動也透過與周圍世界的聯繫而取得意義。

每一個空間也都存在於環境之中。環境包含維持、影響，有時也限制其中活動的自然與人造條件。空氣、水、光、能源、氣候，以及生命的活動，都參與構成這些條件。在物理世界中，空間與環境密不可分，但它們引導我們關注不同的問題。空間使我們關注事物在哪裡存在、如何相互作用；環境則使我們關注哪些條件塑造了它們的存在，以及相互作用所產生的後果。

當人工智能透過人工系統與實體設備進入物理空間，它便開始參與這些既有關係。感知、解讀、判斷與行動的能力，使它能夠影響人與其他生命展開活動的條件。然而，參與空間也意味著面對那些並非由系統創造、而且可能永遠無法被它充分理解的目的、關係與情境。

因此，問題在於智能如何成為物理空間中具有實際意義的參與者。我們需要透過它所支持的活動、作出的判斷，以及行動帶來的後果，考察它的貢獻。理解智能空間，始於理解這些關係，並認識到每一次介入，都發生在一個早已持續運動的世界之中。

## 5.1 From Tools to Intelligent Spaces｜從工具到智能空間

### English｜英文

A place brings together activities that depend on one another and on the physical conditions around them. People move, work, meet, rest, and pursue different purposes within surroundings they often share. Light, ventilation, access, and other capabilities help make these activities possible, but their usefulness changes with what people are doing. A setting suitable for one activity may need to change as another begins. To support life within a place, technology must become relevant to these changing relations between activities and the conditions in which they unfold.

Many arrangements can be maintained through direct controls, schedules, and automatic responses. A timer can provide lighting at an appointed hour, while a controller can adjust ventilation when a measured condition crosses a threshold. Such arrangements can be reliable and sufficient for familiar needs. Yet activities do not always follow the assumptions on which they were established. A meeting may end early, a room may be used for an unexpected purpose, or several activities may require different conditions at once. The challenge arises when the needs of a place change faster than its established arrangements can accommodate.

An intelligent space introduces the possibility of relating these responses more closely to the situation unfolding within it. Artificial intelligence can contribute ways of interpreting information, recognizing relevant changes, and selecting among possible responses. Artificial systems organize these capabilities alongside schedules, control rules, and safeguards. Physical devices provide measurements and carry out actions. What matters at the level of the place is whether these elements work together to support an activity. Installing them in the same room does not, by itself, establish that relationship.

Consider a classroom moving from a presentation to a group discussion. During the presentation, visibility of a projected image may matter; during the discussion, students may need to see one another and read materials across their tables. A system could use a teacher’s instruction, together with available information about the room, to coordinate an appropriate change. The teacher would express the intended activity without having to translate it into every individual device setting. Intelligence would contribute by connecting the activity with the conditions that help it proceed.

Such support also extends the reach of a system’s decisions. An adjustment can affect everyone sharing the room, including people who did not request it. The system may misunderstand the activity or overlook a need that its measurements cannot reveal. Continuous assistance therefore requires boundaries: people need workable ways to correct an interpretation, change an arrangement, or retain direct control. In some situations, a familiar switch or a simple schedule may provide all the support required. Greater involvement is valuable only when it improves the activity.

The movement from tools to intelligent spaces broadens the relationship between technology and the activities of a place. Capabilities that people once arranged individually can now contribute to shared conditions across changing situations. Artificial intelligence enters this relationship by helping connect information about a place with the activities taking place within it. Its participation becomes meaningful through that connection. A further question follows: how can we judge whether the support it provides is appropriate to the circumstances?

### 繁體中文

一個場所匯集了彼此相關、也依賴周圍物理條件的各種活動。人們在經常共享的環境中移動、工作、相聚、休息，並追求不同的目的。光線、通風、通行及其他能力，使這些活動得以進行，但它們是否有用，會隨人的活動而改變。適合一項活動的安排，在另一項活動開始時可能需要調整。技術若要支持場所中的生活，就需要回應活動與其展開條件之間不斷變化的關係。

許多安排可以透過直接控制、排程與自動回應來維持。定時器可以在指定時刻提供照明，控制器也可以在量測條件越過設定值時調整通風。對於熟悉的需要，這些安排可能可靠而且足夠。然而，活動未必總是依照原先設定所依據的假設進行。會議可能提前結束，房間可能臨時改作其他用途，也可能同時有幾項活動需要不同的條件。當場所中的需要變化得比既有安排所能配合的速度更快，新的問題便出現了。

智能空間帶來一種可能：讓這些回應更貼近場所中正在展開的情境。人工智能可以提供解讀資訊、辨識相關變化，以及在可能的回應之間作出選擇的方法。人工系統則將這些能力與排程、控制規則及安全防護機制組織起來。實體設備提供量測，並執行行動。從場所的層次來看，重要的是這些要素是否能共同支持一項活動。把它們安裝在同一個房間裡，本身並不會建立這種關係。

試想一間教室從簡報轉入分組討論。簡報時，投影畫面是否清晰可能較為重要；討論時，學生則可能需要看清彼此，並閱讀桌上的資料。系統可以根據教師的指示，結合可取得的房間資訊，協調適當的調整。教師表達的是接下來要進行的活動，無須再把它逐一轉譯成每台設備的設定。智能在此發揮的作用，是將活動與有助於活動進行的條件連接起來。

這種支持也擴大了系統決定的影響範圍。一次調整可能影響共享房間的每個人，包括並未提出要求的人。系統可能誤解活動，也可能忽略量測無法揭示的需要。因此，持續協助需要有邊界：人需要實際可用的方法，來糾正判斷、更改安排，或保留直接控制。在某些情況下，熟悉的開關或簡單的排程，便足以提供所需支持。只有當更多介入確實改善活動時，它才具有價值。

從工具走向智能空間，擴展了技術與場所中各種活動之間的關係。過去由人逐項安排的能力，如今可以在不斷變化的情境中，共同支持場所中的活動條件。人工智能透過協助連接場所的相關資訊與其中正在進行的活動，參與這種關係。這種聯繫使它的參與具有實際意義。接下來便產生另一個問題：我們如何判斷它所提供的支持，是否適合當前的情境？

## 5.2 What Makes a Space Intelligent?｜什麼使空間具有智能？

### English｜英文

Once a system can participate in arranging the conditions of a place, a new question arises: what makes its contribution appropriate? A building may coordinate lighting, ventilation, and access with considerable reliability, yet the same arrangement can serve one activity and hinder another. A response cannot be judged solely by whether the equipment performed as instructed. Its significance also depends on the activity underway, the people affected, and the circumstances in which the response occurs. The intelligence of a space therefore raises a question about the relation between what a system accomplishes and what the situation calls for.

A garden timer can open a valve at precisely the appointed hour. It may repeat this action without error, even after rain has made watering unnecessary. Its reliability is real; so is the distance between completing an operation and doing something appropriate. That distance matters because an action takes its meaning from circumstances beyond its execution. Watering serves a purpose through its relation to plants, soil, and available water. The successful movement of a valve cannot, by itself, tell us whether that purpose has been served.

Appropriateness therefore belongs to a relationship. A brighter room may help someone read while making a presentation harder to see. The same physical change can support one activity and interrupt another. A system making judgments about a place must attend to these differences, drawing on information that connects conditions with purposes. Those purposes are not simply waiting inside sensor readings to be discovered. They also come from people’s intentions, shared arrangements, and the needs of living things. Understanding a place requires recognizing what its measurements leave unsaid.

Consider a farm where the soil in one field appears dry. Whether to irrigate may depend on the crop’s stage of growth, expected rain, water availability, and work planned for that field. A system could bring some of these considerations together, but its account would remain partial. A farmer may know that planting plans have changed or that a measurement no longer represents the ground. A useful judgment must remain open to this knowledge. The reasons supporting an action may change even when the measurement stays the same.

This does not make elaborate reasoning necessary for every task. Where a condition and an appropriate response are well understood, a simple rule may provide dependable support. Artificial intelligence may help where such arrangements leave important needs unmet. Its value depends on what it contributes; complexity alone gives no reason to prefer it. A system also needs to recognize when the available information is insufficient to justify a change. Sometimes the most appropriate response is to wait, ask, or leave a familiar arrangement unchanged.

The intelligence of a space can therefore be examined through the appropriateness of its responses. A judgment must be considered in relation to the activity it serves, the circumstances on which it depends, and the possibility that those circumstances have been misunderstood. Reliability remains important, but reliable execution alone cannot establish whether an intervention was warranted. This leaves a further problem: before a system can judge what is appropriate, it must form some account of what is happening in the place. How can that understanding arise from information that is necessarily incomplete?

### 繁體中文

當系統能夠參與安排場所中的活動條件，一個新的問題便出現了：什麼使它的貢獻成為適當的支持？一棟建築可以相當可靠地協調照明、通風與通行，但同一種安排可能支持某項活動，也可能妨礙另一項活動。我們不能只根據設備是否依照指令完成操作，判斷一項回應的價值。它的意義也取決於正在進行的活動、受到影響的人，以及回應發生時的情境。因此，空間的智能使我們需要考察：系統所完成的事情，與當前情境真正需要的事情之間，存在什麼關係？

花園的定時器可以在指定時刻準確開啟閥門。即使雨後已無須澆水，它仍可能毫無差錯地重複這個動作。它的可靠性是真實的；完成操作與採取適當行動之間的距離，也同樣真實。這段距離之所以重要，是因為行動的意義來自執行本身以外的情境。澆灌透過它與植物、土壤及可用水資源的關係，才具有目的。閥門順利開啟這件事，本身無法告訴我們那個目的是否已經實現。

因此，行動是否適當，取決於它所處的關係。更明亮的房間可能有助於閱讀，卻使簡報畫面更難看清。同一種物理變化，可以支持一項活動，也可以打斷另一項活動。對場所作出判斷的系統，必須留意這些差異，運用能將條件與目的聯繫起來的資訊。這些目的並不是早已藏在感測讀數裡，只等著被發現。它們也來自人的意圖、共同安排，以及生命的需要。理解一個場所，需要認識量測尚未說明的事情。

試想一座農場，其中一塊田地的土壤顯得偏乾。是否灌溉，可能取決於作物的生長階段、預期降雨、可用水量，以及田間預定的工作。系統可以將其中一些考量結合起來，但它所掌握的情況仍然有限。農人可能知道種植計畫已經改變，也可能知道某項量測已無法反映田地的實際狀態。有用的判斷必須持續向這些知識開放。即使量測結果沒有改變，支持某項行動的理由仍可能改變。

這並不意味著每項任務都需要複雜的推理。當某種條件及其適當回應已被充分理解，簡單規則便可能提供可靠的支持。當這些安排仍無法充分滿足重要需要時，人工智能可能提供幫助。它的價值取決於實際貢獻；複雜程度本身，不構成優先採用它的理由。系統也需要辨認，現有資訊何時不足以支持一項改變。有時，最適當的回應是等待、詢問，或讓熟悉的安排維持原樣。

因此，我們可以透過回應是否適當，考察一個空間所具有的智能。判斷需要放在它所服務的活動、所依據的情境，以及可能誤解情境的風險之中加以檢驗。可靠性仍然重要，但可靠地完成操作，本身不足以說明一次介入是否具有充分理由。這也留下另一個問題：系統若要判斷什麼是適當的，便需要先對場所中正在發生的事情形成某種理解。然而，當資訊必然有所缺漏，這種理解如何可能？

## 5.3 From Sensing to Understanding｜從感知到理解

### English｜英文

A quiet classroom may be empty, or it may be occupied by students absorbed in reading. A sound measurement can be accurate in either situation without telling us which one is occurring. The difficulty begins when we move from what has been detected to what it means. An observation gives us something to consider, but its significance depends on circumstances that may not be contained within it. The passage from sensing to understanding requires us to examine how that significance is established.

Every act of sensing is selective. A sensor responds to certain physical conditions, within a particular range, from a particular position. What becomes available to a system has already been shaped by these limits. Selection is necessary: no useful account of a place can preserve every detail of everything happening there. The question is whether the selected information is adequate for the judgment being made. A precise measurement may still omit what matters most, because accuracy within a limited view does not make that view complete.

Within a place, understanding depends on relations among observations, prior knowledge, and the activities taking place. A change in sound, light, or movement acquires different significance according to who is present, what they are doing, and what the place is being used for. Several activities may also unfold together, making the same condition relevant in different ways. A system must therefore connect its observations with purposes and circumstances that measurements alone cannot supply. Some of these relations can be anticipated in its design; others become available through the people who inhabit and use the place.

A system must still infer what its observations suggest about the situation. Movement near an entrance may indicate someone arriving, leaving, passing through, or waiting for another person. Each interpretation relies on assumptions about how observed conditions relate to activities within the place. Those assumptions may be useful without being certain. A judgment becomes more defensible when the system can distinguish what it has observed from what it has inferred, and when competing interpretations remain possible where the evidence does not settle the matter. A place cannot be fully understood by treating every detected change as an unambiguous event.

Further observations may help distinguish among possible interpretations, but they cannot always reveal why people are using a place in a particular way. A room prepared for a scheduled meeting may instead be needed for quiet work; those present may share intentions that no sensor can establish. People can explain these purposes, correct an interpretation, or change an arrangement when their needs change. Their participation contributes knowledge that the system would otherwise lack. Where uncertainty remains significant, seeking clarification or leaving conditions unchanged may provide better support than acting on an unconfirmed assumption.

For the purposes of assistance, a system’s understanding of a place consists in an account adequate to a particular activity, under particular conditions, and open to revision. Its worth becomes apparent through the judgments it supports and their consequences for the people involved. A system does not need a complete description of life to assist it. It needs to distinguish what it has grounds to conclude from what remains unresolved, and to let that distinction shape how it acts.

### 繁體中文

安靜的教室可能空無一人，也可能坐滿專注閱讀的學生。在這兩種情況下，聲音量測都可以準確，卻無法單憑自身告訴我們正在發生哪一種情況。困難始於我們從偵測到什麼，走向它意味著什麼。觀測提供了可供思考的依據，但其意義取決於未必包含在觀測之中的情境。從感知走向理解，需要考察這些意義如何成立。

每一次感測都有所選擇。感測器從特定位置，在一定範圍內，回應某些物理條件。系統能夠取得的資訊，已受到這些界限的影響。選擇是必要的：任何對場所的有用描述，都無法保留其中一切事物的所有細節。問題在於，被選取的資訊是否足以支持正在作出的判斷。精確的量測仍可能遺漏最重要的事情，因為有限視野中的準確，並不使這個視野成為完整。

在一個場所中，理解取決於觀測、既有知識與正在進行的活動之間的關係。聲音、光線或移動的變化，會因誰在場、他們正在做什麼，以及場所當時的用途，而具有不同意義。幾種活動也可能同時展開，使同一條件以不同方式與各項活動相關。因此，系統必須將觀測與目的及情境聯繫起來，而這些目的與情境無法單憑量測取得。其中一些關係可以在設計時預先考慮，另一些則需要透過生活與活動於場所中的人們才能獲知。

系統仍需要透過推論，判斷觀測結果可能意味著什麼。入口附近出現移動，可能表示有人到達、離開、經過，或正在等待另一個人。每一種解釋，都依賴於觀測條件與場所中活動如何相連的假設。這些假設可能有用，卻未必確定。當系統能區分實際觀測與由此推得的結論，並在證據不足以判定時保留其他可能的解釋，它的判斷便更有依據。若將每一次偵測到的變化都視為意義明確的事件，便無法充分理解一個場所。

更多觀測或許有助於區分不同的可能解釋，卻未必能揭示人們為何以某種方式使用場所。一間原本安排開會的房間，可能臨時需要用於安靜工作；在場的人也可能具有任何感測器都無法確知的共同意圖。人們可以說明這些目的、糾正系統的解釋，或在需要改變時調整安排。他們的參與，為系統提供了原本無法取得的知識。當不確定性仍然重要，尋求澄清或維持現有條件，可能比依據未經確認的假設採取行動，更能提供適當的支持。

就協助活動而言，系統對場所的理解，是在特定條件下足以支持某項活動、並容許修正的認識。它的價值，透過所支持的判斷，以及這些判斷對相關人們造成的後果而顯現。系統無須完整描述生活，才能協助生活。它需要分辨哪些結論已有依據、哪些問題仍未解決，並讓這項區別影響自身的行動。

## 5.4 From Judgment to Action｜從判斷到行動

### English｜英文

A judgment can remain open to reconsideration without changing the conditions it describes. Action crosses that boundary. It gives a particular interpretation a consequence in a world shared with others. When an intelligent system acts, its understanding of a situation begins to shape the situation itself. This is why the passage from judgment to action requires a further justification: having grounds to believe that a change would help does not yet establish the authority to make it, or determine whose purposes it should serve.

Even within an authorized purpose, choosing among possible actions requires a further judgment. Several responses may address the same condition while affecting people differently. Choosing among them requires more than predicting which will produce a desired change. It also requires considering whose activity is being supported, what else may be disturbed, and whether the system has been entrusted with that choice. The ability to act and the grounds for acting must therefore be considered together.

For a judgment to affect a place, it must become an action within its physical conditions. Artificial intelligence may help determine what change is needed, while artificial systems and devices provide the means through which that change can occur. Opening a door, adjusting airflow, or changing illumination affects a setting already shared by people and other living things. The intended benefit depends on what can actually be changed and on how that change meets the activities underway. An action must therefore be considered in relation to both the purpose that guides it and the physical circumstances in which it takes effect.

The result of an action must be examined within the place where it occurs. A door may open as commanded while allowing an unwanted disturbance to enter; ventilation may change as intended without improving the conditions for those present. The equipment has performed an operation, but the activity it was meant to support may remain unaffected or become more difficult. Observing what follows allows the system and the people involved to compare the intended benefit with what actually happened. Feedback matters because the world can answer a decision with something other than the outcome its maker anticipated.

The possibility of correction, however, should not make intervention careless. Some effects persist, and even a reversible change can interrupt an activity at a moment that cannot be recovered. Appropriate action therefore involves proportion: the extent of intervention should bear a defensible relation to its purpose, the available evidence, and the consequences of error. Where uncertainty remains, a limited adjustment or a request for clarification may be more reasonable than a comprehensive response. Restraint can be an active expression of judgment.

Intelligence enters the life of a place through this relationship between understanding, authorized action, and experienced consequence. Its contribution becomes real when an intervention supports the activity for which it was undertaken. That support must remain open to examination by the people affected, whose experience may reveal what the system’s measurements miss. The passage from judgment to action is complete only in a limited sense: an operation can end, while the responsibility to attend to what it has changed continues.

### 繁體中文

一個判斷可以持續接受重新考慮，而不改變它所描述的條件。行動則跨過了這道邊界，使某種特定解釋在與他人共享的世界中產生後果。當智能系統採取行動，它對情境的理解便開始塑造情境本身。因此，從判斷走向行動，需要進一步的正當理由：有依據認為某項改變會有幫助，尚不足以確立作出改變的權限，也不能決定它應服務於誰的目的。

即使目的已獲授權，在可能的行動之間作出選擇，仍需要進一步判斷。數種回應可能處理同一條件，卻以不同方式影響人。要在它們之間作出選擇，除了預測哪一種能產生期望的變化，也需要考慮正在支持誰的活動、還可能干擾什麼，以及系統是否受託作出這項選擇。因此，行動的能力與行動的依據，必須一同被考察。

一項判斷若要對場所產生影響，就必須在實際物理條件中成為行動。人工智能可以協助判斷需要作出什麼改變，人工系統與實體設備則提供實現改變的方法。開啟一扇門、調整氣流或改變照明，都會影響一個已由人與其他生命共同使用的場所。預期的幫助能否實現，取決於哪些條件實際可以改變，以及這些改變如何作用於正在進行的活動。因此，考察一項行動，必須同時關注引導它的目的，以及它產生作用時的物理情境。

一項行動的結果，必須放在它發生的場所中加以考察。門可能依照指令開啟，卻讓不必要的干擾進入；通風可能按預期改變，卻沒有改善在場者所處的條件。設備完成了操作，但原本希望支持的活動可能毫無改善，甚至變得更加困難。透過觀察後續發生的事情，系統與相關的人便能比較預期的幫助與實際結果。回饋之所以重要，是因為世界對一個決定的回應，可能不同於作出決定者所預期的結果。

然而，可以修正，不應使介入變得輕率。有些影響會持續存在；即使變化可以逆轉，也可能在無法重來的時刻打斷一項活動。因此，適當行動需要分寸：介入的程度，應與其目的、現有證據及錯誤後果之間具有可說明的合理關係。當不確定性仍然存在，有限的調整或尋求澄清，可能比全面回應更合理。克制也可以是判斷的一種積極表現。

智能透過理解、獲得授權的行動，以及人實際經歷的後果之間的關係，進入場所的生活。當介入支持了它原本要協助的活動，智能的貢獻才得到實現。這種支持必須持續接受受影響者的檢視，因為人的經驗可能揭示系統量測所遺漏的事情。從判斷到行動的過程，只能在有限的意義上被視為完成：操作可以結束，對其所造成改變的關注與責任仍然持續。

## 5.5 Intelligence in Everyday Life｜日常生活中的智能

### English｜英文

The usefulness of intelligence becomes clearer when we consider the activity that continues after a system has done its work. A successful adjustment may establish favorable conditions, yet those conditions acquire their value through what people can do within them. Comfort can help sustain concentration; dependable access can make a place easier to use. The contribution belongs partly to what follows the intervention. To understand that contribution, we must look beyond the completed operation to the activity it enables, preserves, or changes.

This requires a longer view than the moment of assistance. Daily life brings activities together, often without a clear boundary between them. Work gives way to conversation, concentration to rest, and plans to unexpected needs. Support that fits one moment may become an obstacle in the next. Its value depends on whether people can carry their activities forward without repeatedly rebuilding the conditions around them. Intelligence becomes useful within this continuity when its assistance remains available while the purposes it serves are allowed to change.

Dependable support changes how people can use and rely on a place. When lighting, access, and other conditions remain suitable without constant attention, people can concentrate on the activities they came to undertake. Yet an arrangement that appears reliable may still fail when circumstances change. If people must repeatedly check what the system has done or reconstruct the conditions it was meant to maintain, the burden of managing the place remains with them. Support becomes more dependable when people can recognize a failure, request a change, and continue their activities through available alternatives.

A shared place seldom serves only one purpose. In a library, some people may be reading quietly while others need to discuss their work; in a public square, people may gather, pass through, or rest. Conditions that favor one activity can interfere with another, and the people affected may not have participated in requesting a change. Intelligent support must therefore account for the coexistence of different purposes within the same place. People need ways to express preferences, recognize competing needs, and reach workable arrangements. A system can assist in carrying out those arrangements, but its ability to coordinate physical conditions does not give it authority to settle every difference among the people who share them.

Some activities derive part of their value from the involvement they require. People may wish to tend a garden, prepare a room for a gathering, or arrange a place according to changing preferences. Assistance can remove difficulties that prevent these activities from taking place, but it can also displace forms of participation that people value. Within an intelligent space, the extent of assistance should therefore remain responsive to how people wish to take part. A place becomes more supportive when its capabilities can be used selectively, helping with what people choose to delegate while leaving room for what they wish to do themselves.

The value of an intelligent space becomes visible in the life that continues within it. People can undertake different activities, share places, adapt arrangements, and respond when circumstances change. Artificial intelligence contributes when its understanding and actions help sustain these possibilities without requiring people to organize their lives around the system itself. Such participation must be examined through experience, including the needs it meets, the difficulties it creates, and the changes it makes possible. The place remains part of a wider physical world, shaped by conditions beyond any system's control. Intelligence enters that world as a participant whose contribution is measured by what life within it can continue to become.

### 繁體中文

當我們考察系統完成工作之後仍在繼續的活動，智能的用處便更為清楚。一次成功的調整可以建立有利條件，而這些條件的價值，透過人能在其中進行什麼活動而顯現。舒適可以幫助人保持專注，可靠的通行安排可以使場所更容易使用。智能的貢獻，部分存在於介入之後發生的事情中。要理解這份貢獻，我們必須從已完成的操作，進一步看見它所促成、維持或改變的活動。

這需要我們將目光延伸到協助發生的瞬間之外。日常生活將不同活動連在一起，它們之間往往沒有明確邊界。工作轉入交談，專注轉為休息，計畫也會因突如其來的需要而改變。適合某一時刻的支持，到了下一刻可能成為障礙。它的價值，取決於人能否繼續展開活動，而無須反覆重新安排周圍的條件。當協助持續可用，而它所服務的目的仍能改變，智能便能在這種生活的延續中發揮作用。

可靠的支持會改變人們使用與依賴一個場所的方式。當照明、通行及其他條件能夠持續保持合適，而無須人們不斷留意，他們便能專注於原本要進行的活動。然而，看似可靠的安排，仍可能在情境改變時失效。如果人們需要反覆檢查系統做了什麼，或重新安排它原本應當維持的條件，管理場所的負擔便仍然落在他們身上。當人們能夠察覺失效、要求改變，並透過可用的替代方式繼續活動，這種支持才更值得依靠。

一個共享的場所，很少只服務於單一目的。在圖書館裡，有人可能正在安靜閱讀，另一些人則需要討論工作；在公共廣場上，人們可能聚集、經過或休息。有利於一項活動的條件，可能干擾另一項活動，而受到影響的人未必曾參與提出改變的要求。因此，智能的支持必須考慮不同目的如何在同一場所中共存。人們需要能夠表達偏好、認識彼此不同的需要，並形成可行的共同安排。系統可以協助實現這些安排，但協調物理條件的能力，並不賦予它裁決共享場所中所有分歧的權限。

有些活動的價值，部分來自人們親自投入的過程。人們可能希望照料花園、為聚會布置場所，或依照不斷改變的偏好安排周圍空間。協助可以消除妨礙這些活動進行的困難，但也可能取代人們原本珍視的參與方式。因此，在智能空間中，協助應當回應人們希望如何親自參與。當場所中的能力可以依需要選擇運用，協助人們完成願意交付的工作，同時為希望親自從事的活動保留空間，場所便能提供更好的支持。

智能空間的價值，會在其中持續展開的生活中顯現。人們可以從事不同活動、共享場所、調整安排，並在情境改變時作出回應。當人工智能的理解與行動有助於維持這些可能性，而無須人們圍繞系統本身安排生活，它便作出了有意義的貢獻。這種參與必須透過實際經驗檢驗，包括它滿足了哪些需要、造成了哪些困難，以及開啟了哪些新的可能。場所始終存在於更廣大的物理世界之中，並受到任何系統都無法完全控制的條件影響。智能作為這個世界的參與者，其貢獻應從其中的生活還能如何持續展開來衡量。
