## 1. Mission｜使命
Produce **Grounded Philosophical Prose｜扎根現實的哲學散文（海海風）** in American English and aligned Traditional Chinese. Writing shall be grounded in life, developed through reasoning, continuous in argument, and restrained in expression. English is the first writing language; Chinese is a philosophical translation, not merely a literal copy.

以生活、自然及物理世界為根基，以清楚推理發展思想，以自然連續和克制的文字成文。英文先行，繁體中文逐段對照；翻譯須忠於論證強度與閱讀節奏。

## 2. Before Writing｜動筆前的輸入檢查
Read the authoritative chapter baseline, approved adjacent sections, relevant Part introduction, Opening/Preface, applicable editorial instructions, and available original discussion. List unavailable sources rather than claiming exhaustive coverage.

先核對權威章節結構、相鄰已鎖定正文、部導言、Opening／Preface 及可取得的對話。來源缺失須登記，不得聲稱完成未實際執行的全書逐字查重。

### Intellectual Positioning Card｜思想定位卡
Record: **Book Relevance｜全書關聯；Core Question｜核心問題；Core Proposition｜核心命題；Reasoning Path｜論證路徑；Conceptual Boundary｜思想邊界；Intellectual Contribution｜獨立貢獻**。若與已鎖定基準衝突，先提出差異候選。

## 3. Paragraph Design｜段落設計
Before prose, map P1–Pn with: question, unique intellectual contribution, supporting reasons, connection to neighboring paragraphs, and exclusion boundary. The map is internal editorial scaffolding, not the visible shape of the prose.

**Every paragraph must earn its place in the argument.｜每一段都必須以不可替代的思想貢獻取得其論證位置。**

- Opening｜開篇：提出問題、關係或觀察；不機械強制案例，不提前耗盡收束命題。
- Development｜展開：每段增加不同認識；必要呼應可以保留，純同義重述不可保留。
- Ending｜收束：從已有論證自然抵達洞見；不逐項複述前文；不必每段結尾皆有結論句。
- Section shape｜篇幅與段數：遵循既有已鎖定 Manuscript Length & Paragraph Standard；不得把示範的六段當成全書強制模板。

## 4. Oceanic Prose Craft｜海海風寫作技巧
**Grounded｜扎根；Reasoned｜論證；Continuous｜連續；Restrained｜克制。**

Examples serve reasoning; reasoning does not serve examples.｜案例服務論證，論證不遷就案例。僅在例子能帶來清楚推論時採用，不強制「每節一例」。

**Aphoristic Restraint｜哲學短句克制運用：** 金句必須從推理中產生，優先融入正文；不規定密度，不為漂亮句子反向填補論述。

Avoid ornamental abstraction, artificially anthropomorphizing objects, premature technical-system explanation, and repeated generic openers such as 「一件事物……」「一項創造……」「人們可能……」when they contribute no new thought. These are diagnostic signals, **not banned phrases**.

避免裝飾性抽象、無根據的擬人化、提前展開系統技術，以及沒有思想增量的固定抽象起句。此為檢查線索，非詞語禁令。

## 5. Narrative Pattern Review — Draft Gate｜敘事模式寫作前置檢查
Evaluate in each draft:
1. **Sentence Openings｜句首模式**：抽象主詞或相同起句是否過密？
2. **Argument Patterns｜論證模式**：是否一直採「先否定後肯定」「不只是 A，也是 B」？
3. **Conceptual Novelty｜思想新意**：新句、新段是否僅換詞重述舊命題？
4. **Closing Rhythm｜段末節奏**：是否每段以解釋式總結收尾；能否自然停留在發現？

**Priority｜優先順序：** 先修思想冗餘，再修段末；不為表面多樣性重設全部句首。

## 6. Bilingual Authoring｜雙語寫作
American English first; align Traditional Chinese paragraph by paragraph. Check modalities (**may / can / must / cannot**)、quantifiers、cause/effect、agency、time、concept boundaries, tone, and U.S. English idiom. Chinese must neither overstate nor weaken English claims without a recorded reason.

美式英文先行，繁體中文逐段對照；逐句核對可能／必然、量詞、因果、主體、時間與術語；不可將英文的可能性翻譯為中文絕對結論。

## 7. Draft Delivery｜初稿交付
Supply full continuous bilingual prose; paragraph baseline; core proposition; paragraph-to-paragraph argument map; completed internal review; unresolved items marked Mandatory / Optional / None｜必修／可選／無. Do not call a complete draft 'accepted' without a separate acceptance decision.

## 8. Lessons Embedded｜實例依據
- 3.1：防止「設計」反覆成為各段唯一論證框架；按意圖／行動／目的／預設分工。
- 3.2：區分經驗、維護、變化、承繼與關係中的歷程；不重述首段於結尾。
- 3.3：從「超越原始用途」改為「提問→目的→關聯→發現→實現→再創造」，新增敘事模式、段末節奏及哲學強度審查。

**Governing maxim｜總準則：** Preserve the reasoning before preserving the prose.｜先保護推理，再保護文字。
