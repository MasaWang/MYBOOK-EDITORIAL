# Chapter 2 — The Human Measure｜第二章 人的尺度

### Core Question｜核心問題

What measure should we use to understand the value of what we create?｜我們應以什麼尺度理解自身創造的價值？

### Core Orientation｜核心方向

The human measure is a dynamic basis for understanding and judgment, not a fixed scoring formula or a claim of human supremacy. Human life takes place through relations with other people, living beings, and the natural world.

人的尺度是動態的理解與判斷依據，不是固定的評分公式，也不是人類至上主義。人的生活始終發生於與其他人、生命及自然世界的關係之中。

### Five Sections｜五節（名稱與順序保持結構基準）

1. **2.1 Capability Is Not Value｜能力不等於價值** — Distinguish measuring capability from understanding value in real circumstances.｜區分能力的衡量與實際情境中的價值理解。
2. **2.2 Intelligence and Possibility｜智能與可能性** — Examine how intelligence reveals, compares, narrows, and expands possibilities, without presenting those functions as an exhaustive definition of intelligence.｜研究智能如何揭示、比較、縮小及擴展可能性，不以此作為智能的完整定義。
3. **2.3 Wisdom and Judgment｜智慧與判斷** — Explain purpose, trade-offs, restraint, and direction in judging possible actions.｜研究目的、取捨、分寸與方向如何參與判斷。
4. **2.4 The Boundaries of Human Life｜人的生活邊界** — Examine finitude in time, attention, experience, and relation; do not describe technology as infinite or life's limits as defects.｜研究時間、注意力、經驗與關係上的有限性；不把技術描寫成無限，也不把生活邊界當作缺陷。
5. **2.5 What Makes Creation Worthwhile｜什麼使創造值得** — Integrate capability, wisdom, lived circumstances, and the shared world without reducing value to a fixed formula.｜整合能力、智慧、生活情境與共同世界，不把價值簡化為固定公式。

### Reasoning Progression｜推理路徑

Technical Achievement → Human Circumstances → Value → The Question of Measure (2.1); Possibility (2.2) → Judgment (2.3) → Finitude and Shared Time (2.4) → Worthwhile Creation in a Shared World (2.5).

技術成就 → 生活情境 → 價值 → 尺度問題（2.1）；可能性（2.2）→ 判斷（2.3）→ 有限性與共同時間（2.4）→ 共同世界中值得的創造（2.5）。
