## 章首

### 英文版

**Why do we create?**

Human beings have filled the world with creations that were once beyond imagination. We have built places to live, crossed oceans, learned to fly, composed music, and developed ways of understanding things that our senses cannot directly perceive. Today, artificial intelligence allows us to create systems with capabilities that once required immediate human direction. Yet the history of creation cannot be understood through growing capability alone. **Through creation, we bring new possibilities into the world. Yet the significance of what we create lies not only in its capabilities, but also in what it makes possible for life.**

The things we make become part of places and relationships that existed before them. They can extend human action, preserve knowledge, invite exploration, and alter the conditions of everyday life. Some demand our attention; others serve us best when they allow us to turn our attention elsewhere. We create as people who have hopes and abilities, but also as inhabitants of a world we did not make and cannot claim for ourselves alone. To ask why we create is therefore to ask what draws us beyond the present, what creation brings into existence, and how the lives unfolding around it may be changed.

### 繁體中文版

**我們為何創造？**

人類在世界中留下了無數曾經難以想像的創造。我們建造居所、跨越海洋、學會飛行、創作音樂，也發展出理解感官無法直接察覺之事物的方法。今天，人工智能使我們能夠創造具備某些過去需要人直接指揮才能實現之能力的系統。然而，創造的歷史不能只從能力的增長來理解。**透過創造，我們使新的可能性進入世界。然而，創造的意義不只在於造物具有什麼能力，也在於它使生命與生活獲得什麼可能性。**

我們的造物進入的是早已存在的場所與關係之中。它們可以延伸人的行動、保存知識、開啟探索，也能改變日常生活的條件。有些造物需要我們持續關注，另一些則在使我們能夠將注意力留給其他事情時，發揮了最好的作用。我們以擁有願望與能力的人之身分創造，卻也始終生活於一個並非由自己創造、也不能獨自佔有的世界。追問我們為何創造，因此也需要理解：是什麼使人超越眼前，創造究竟使什麼成為現實，以及周圍的生命與生活如何因此改變。
