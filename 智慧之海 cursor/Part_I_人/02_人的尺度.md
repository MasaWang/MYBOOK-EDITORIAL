# 第二章——人的尺度

**核心問題：** 我們應以什麼尺度判斷自身創造的價值？

**獨有思想內容：**

- 能夠創造，不代表創造一定值得追求。
- Capability｜能力、Possibility｜可能性與 Value｜價值並不相同。
- Intelligence｜智能擴展可能性，Wisdom｜智慧參與方向與價值判斷。
- 人的尺度不只是效能、效率或能力大小。
- 人的尺度也不能簡化為單一的生活方式或幸福標準。
- 對創造價值的判斷需要考慮人與其他生命、自然及共同世界的關係。

**本章思想成果：** 建立人的尺度作為理解、衡量和判斷創造價值的哲學依據。

**概念邊界：** 不把尺度等同於人的能力限制；不詳細分析非預期後果或責任分配。

## Opening｜章首

### 英文版

An intelligent system may carry out its work without asking us to notice it, yet still change the way we live. It can respond to a changing environment, arrange familiar activities, or make decisions that once required our attention. Its performance tells us something important about what has been achieved; our experience of living with it reveals other questions. We begin to consider which choices remain our own, what forms of dependence arise, and whether the changes are ones we can welcome. Human life gives us a measure by which to understand and judge the value of what we create.

People encounter the same creation through different circumstances, relationships, and ways of living. What gives one person greater freedom may place new demands on another; what improves one activity may alter conditions important to other forms of life. Such differences do not necessarily yield a single answer, but they cannot be set aside when we consider value. To judge what we create is therefore to consider not only what it enables, but how it enters the relationships and conditions through which life is lived. The human measure begins with our lives, without claiming that the world exists for us alone.

### 繁體中文版

一套智能系統可以在不要求人時時留意的情況下完成工作，卻仍然改變我們的生活方式。它能回應環境的變化、安排熟悉的日常活動，或作出過去需要人投入注意力的決定。系統的運行表現，讓我們認識它取得了什麼成就；與它共同生活的經驗，則使其他問題逐漸浮現。我們開始思考，哪些選擇仍由自己作出、形成了怎樣的依賴，以及這些改變是否值得接受。人的生活，為我們理解與判斷自身創造的價值提供了尺度。

人們在不同的處境、關係與生活方式中接觸同一項創造。使一個人獲得更多自由的事物，也可能對另一個人提出新的要求；改善某種活動的創造，也可能改變其他生命賴以存在的重要條件。這些差異未必能得到單一答案，但當我們考慮價值時，也不能將它們排除在外。因此，判斷自身的創造，不只需要理解它帶來哪些能力，也需要認識它如何進入生命與生活得以展開的關係與條件。人的尺度從我們的生活出發，卻不宣稱世界只為我們而存在。

## 2.1 The Value of Creation｜創造的價值

### 英文版

A dam can make a dependable supply of water possible where rainfall is uncertain. Stored water may sustain crops, households, and essential services during a dry season; controlled releases and electricity generation can provide further benefits. These are achievements with consequences for daily life, not merely specifications of a structure. To understand their value, we must ask what becomes more secure or possible for those who rely on them, while leaving room to examine the effects of the dam beyond its intended users.

These capabilities can be described and tested. Engineers can measure a reservoir's storage capacity, evaluate its generators, and determine whether its structure meets design requirements under specified conditions. Such evidence establishes what the dam can actually do and how reliably it can perform. A capability becomes a meaningful technical achievement when it can be demonstrated, tested, and relied upon. Recognizing that achievement is essential to evaluating the dam, though it does not by itself settle its value.

The water held behind a dam is also water that would otherwise move downstream. Changes in the timing and volume of its flow can affect floodplain soils, fish migration, and the people whose activities depend on seasonal rhythms. These effects are not simply another use of the dam to be added to its list of functions. They arise because controlling one part of a river changes conditions elsewhere along its course. The dam's value therefore calls for attention to consequences that its designed performance does not describe.

The scale of a reservoir is only one consideration in deciding whether to build it. A community facing recurrent shortages must also examine available water sources, the reliability of less disruptive measures, and the losses that a large project might impose elsewhere. Where alternatives exist, a greater storage capacity may contribute little that justifies its effects; where they do not, that capacity may protect essential needs. The choice is therefore not a ranking of engineering achievements but a comparison of what different courses of action would ask people and the river to bear. A capability gains its particular value through the circumstances in which it becomes part of life.

Nor does recognizing the river's existing conditions mean that leaving it unchanged is necessarily better. Human creations may sustain life, reduce hardship, and open possibilities that natural conditions alone would not provide. They may also alter relationships and conditions that already matter. Neither preserving a river exactly as it is nor changing it simply because we can is, by itself, a sufficient measure of value. The worth of change depends on what it brings into being and what existing conditions must change or be given up.

An engineering project can fulfill every design requirement and still leave the decision to build it open to question. The evidence that confirms a dam's performance cannot by itself justify choosing that dam over another way of meeting the need. This earlier choice calls for reasons that take account of those who benefit, those who bear its effects, and the possibilities left unexplored. Technical success deserves recognition; the decision to pursue it must stand on reasons of its own.

### 繁體中文版

在降雨不穩定的地方，水壩可以提供較為可靠的水源。儲存的河水可能在乾旱季節支持農作、家庭與必要的公共服務；調節放水與發電，也能帶來其他益處。這些成就不只是工程結構的規格，而是會對日常生活產生實際影響的能力。理解它們的價值，需要看見依賴這些能力的人因此獲得了什麼，同時保留對水壩如何影響其他生命與環境的追問。

這些能力可以描述，也可以檢驗。工程師可以測量水庫的蓄水容量、評估發電機組，並判斷結構在指定條件下是否符合設計要求。這些證據能確定水壩實際可以完成什麼，以及它能否可靠運作。一項能力能夠得到實際展示、接受檢驗並值得信賴，才成為具有實際意義的技術成就。承認這項成就，是評價水壩不可缺少的一步，卻還不足以決定它的全部價值。

水壩蓄起的河水，原本也會繼續流向下游。水量與流動時序的改變，可能影響河漫灘的土壤、魚類遷徙，以及依循季節水文節律安排活動的人們。這些影響並不是水壩功能清單中可以另添的一項用途，而是因為控制河流的一部分，也改變了沿途其他地方的條件。理解水壩的價值，因此還必須看見其設計性能無法直接說明的後果。

水庫的規模，只是判斷是否應當興建它時需要考慮的一項條件。面對反覆缺水的社區，還必須研究現有水源、干擾較小的方案是否可靠，以及大型工程可能使其他地方承受什麼損失。有替代方案時，更大的蓄水能力未必能帶來足以抵償其影響的益處；缺少替代方案時，同樣的能力卻可能保障基本需要。因此，選擇的關鍵不是為不同工程成就排名，而是比較各種行動將使人與河流分別承受什麼。一項能力在進入具體生活處境時，才逐漸呈現其特有的價值。

同樣地，承認河流原有的條件，也不意味著保持河流不變就必然更好。人類的創造可以維繫生命、減輕困難，並開啟單憑自然條件無法提供的可能性；它們也可能改變原本就具有重要意義的關係與條件。無論是讓河流完全維持原狀，還是僅僅因為有能力改變它便加以改變，都不足以單獨成為價值的尺度。改變是否值得，需要同時理解它使什麼得以實現，以及原有的哪些條件將因此改變或失去。

一項工程可以符合全部設計要求，卻仍不能讓當初的興建決定免於追問。確認水壩運行表現的證據，並不足以單獨證明選擇興建水壩，比採用其他方法滿足需要更為妥當。這項選擇需要自己的理由，也必須考慮受益的人、承受影響的人，以及未曾採用的其他可能。技術上的成功值得肯定；選擇追求這項成功，也必須有足以成立的理由。

## 2.2 The Measure of Human Life｜人的生活尺度

### 英文版

Human life unfolds within boundaries that are not all of the same kind. Some limitations can be overcome as knowledge and capability grow. Others arise from the conditions through which life is lived: we experience the world through a body, within time, and in relation to others. These conditions are not fixed in every detail, nor do they prescribe a single way to live. Yet they cannot be treated simply as deficiencies awaiting correction. They give human experience its particular character, even as the ways we live continue to change. Some human limits invite us to create; others help us understand what our creations are for.

Time is one of these conditions. We can measure its passage with increasing precision and accomplish more within a given period, but the time of a life is not experienced merely as a succession of equal intervals. An hour spent in anticipation differs from an hour spent in rest; time shared with another person carries a significance that cannot be established by duration alone. What has passed cannot be lived again in precisely the same way, and what lies ahead remains uncertain. The meaning of time emerges through the events, commitments, and relationships that give it shape. More time becomes a human gain through what we are able to live within it.

Attention gives our experience its focus. We cannot notice everything at once; attending to one thing means letting others recede for a time. This selectiveness allows a conversation to deepen and understanding to grow through sustained involvement. More abundant information does not give us more attention, and constant interruption can leave our experience fragmented. The direction of our attention shapes the life we actually experience.

Experience is inseparable from the body through which it takes place. Through movement, sensation, effort, and rest, we learn about the world and our place within it. A description may preserve knowledge of an experience without reproducing what it feels like to live through it. Technology can extend our reach and assist our senses, but information about an experience cannot simply replace the experience itself. We come to know the world not only through what we can describe, but through what we live.

No human life unfolds through individual experience alone. Our lives take shape among others, whose experiences we cannot possess and whose choices are not ours to determine. Relationships grow through the time people give one another, the ways they respond, and the freedom each retains to live as a person in their own right. Greater coordination can help people act together, but it cannot erase the difference between their lives. To live together is to share a world without possessing one another’s lives.

Time, attention, embodiment, and life with others do not form a checklist against which every invention can be scored. They reveal why achievements must be understood in the lives they touch. We measure our creations against human life because it is within life that their capabilities become consequences, their possibilities become choices, and their value becomes meaningful. Yet this human measure judges our own creations; it does not make human usefulness the sole source of value in the natural world or in other living things.

### 繁體中文版

人的生活在不同性質的邊界之中展開。有些限制可以隨著知識與能力的增長而克服；另一些則來自生活本身得以展開的條件：我們透過身體、在時間之中，並在與他人的關係裡經驗世界。這些條件並非每個細節都固定不變，也不規定唯一的生活方式。然而，它們不能簡單地被視為等待修正的缺陷。即使生活方式持續改變，這些條件仍賦予人的經驗特有的性質。人的某些限制促使我們創造，另一些限制則幫助我們理解創造究竟為了什麼。

時間是其中一項條件。我們可以越來越精確地測量時間的流逝，也能在一定時間內完成更多事情，但生命中的時間，並不只是由一連串等長的間隔所構成。一小時的等待與一小時的休息，具有不同的經驗；與他人共同度過的時間，其意義也不能單憑長短來確定。已經過去的時刻，無法以完全相同的方式重新經歷；尚未到來的時間，則始終帶有不確定性。時間的意義，透過其中發生的事情、所承擔的承諾與所形成的關係逐漸顯現。更多時間之所以成為人的收穫，在於我們能夠在其中經歷怎樣的生活。

注意力使我們的經驗具有焦點。我們無法同時留意一切；專注於某件事，也意味著讓其他事物暫時退到意識之外。這種選擇性，使交談得以深入，也使理解在持續投入中逐漸形成。資訊更豐富，不代表人就擁有更多注意力；不斷受到打斷，也可能使經驗變得破碎。我們將注意力投向何處，也在塑造我們真正經歷的生活。

經驗無法與承載它的身體分離。我們透過移動、感受、付出與休息，認識世界，也認識自己身處其中的位置。對一段經驗的描述，可以保存相關知識，卻無法重現親身經歷時的感受。技術能夠延伸我們的行動範圍、協助感官，但關於經驗的資訊，不能直接取代經驗本身。我們認識世界，不只透過能夠描述的事物，也透過自身親歷的生活。

人的生活從來不只透過個人的經驗展開。我們的生活在他人之間逐漸形成，而他人的經驗無法被我們占有，他們的選擇也不由我們決定。關係透過人們給予彼此的時間、相互回應的方式，以及每個人仍能作為獨立個體而生活的自由逐漸發展。更緊密的協調，可以幫助人們共同行動，卻無法抹去彼此生活的差異。共同生活，是共享同一個世界，而不彼此占有對方的人生。

時間、注意力、身體經驗與他人的共同生活，並不是一份可以替所有創造物評分的清單。它們讓我們理解，為什麼創造的成就必須放回受到影響的生活之中。我們以人的生活作為衡量自身創造的尺度，因為正是在生活之中，創造的能力產生後果，可能性成為選擇，而價值得以呈現其意義。然而，人的尺度用來判斷我們自己的創造，並不表示自然世界與其他生命的價值只能由它們對人的用途決定。

## 2.3 The Art of Judgment｜判斷的藝術

### 英文版

Judgment begins when we must decide what deserves our attention and action. We may have several reasons for taking a particular course, yet those reasons do not always point in the same direction. Some arise from immediate needs, others from commitments we have made or consequences we wish to avoid. To judge is not merely to select an option from those available. It is to consider the reasons for acting, examine their significance, and determine how they should guide a decision. Judgment turns what matters to us into reasons for how we choose to act.

Choosing a purpose is itself an act of judgment, not merely the beginning of action. We may pursue an aim because it is familiar, because others expect it of us, or because it promises a result we desire. Each offers a reason, though none necessarily settles the matter. Judgment requires us to ask what supports a purpose and whether those reasons remain convincing when examined alongside other concerns. This inquiry need not end in a single principle that governs every decision. It calls instead for an understanding of why a particular aim matters here, under these conditions, and in relation to what else we have reason to care about.

Reasons become more difficult to weigh when legitimate concerns compete. A choice that protects one interest may limit another. A benefit gained now may carry consequences later, while an action that serves one person may alter the circumstances of others. A difficult choice is sometimes difficult because more than one path leads toward something worth protecting; more information alone cannot decide how those concerns should be weighed. Judgment must distinguish between what can be compromised and what should not be surrendered, between a preference that may be reconsidered and a commitment that gives a decision its meaning. Such distinctions do not eliminate conflict. They make it possible to approach conflict without pretending that every important concern can be satisfied at once.

Wisdom gives judgment depth through experience and reflection. It is expressed not simply in holding firm to a principle, nor in adjusting to every circumstance, but in knowing why firmness or flexibility may be called for. Experience can reveal the limits of earlier decisions, while reflection helps us distinguish what was mistaken in our reasoning from what changed beyond our control. Over time, wisdom can give judgment a sense of proportion without pretending that all values share a single scale. Wisdom does not supply a formula for resolving competing reasons. It allows judgment to become more attentive to the particular situation without losing sight of the wider life in which that situation belongs.

Artificial intelligence can participate in this work in consequential ways. It can help identify relevant considerations, examine possible consequences, and make relationships visible that might otherwise escape attention. It can also make or recommend decisions according to learned patterns, stated objectives, and operational constraints. Yet decisions made through artificial systems depend on how questions are framed, what information is available, and which considerations are included or left aside. Intelligence can widen the grounds of judgment, but those grounds must themselves remain open to judgment: we must be able to examine and challenge how a recommendation was produced. Responsibility does not disappear when reasoning and decision-making are shared with the systems we create.

A decision rests on what we can understand at the time, even though its consequences continue beyond that understanding. Experience may confirm our reasons or expose what we failed to see. Good judgment does not require us to foresee everything; it requires us to remain answerable to what we learn. We can revise a decision without denying the reasons that once justified it, just as we can accept responsibility without claiming to have controlled every outcome.

### 繁體中文版

當我們必須決定什麼值得投入注意與行動時，判斷便開始發揮作用。我們可能有多種理由採取某項行動，但這些理由未必指向相同的方向。有些來自眼前的需要，有些來自已經作出的承諾，或我們希望避免的後果。判斷不只是從現有選項中挑選一項，而是思考行動的理由、審視其重要性，並確定它們應如何引導決定。判斷使我們所珍視的事物，成為決定如何行動的理由。

選擇目的，本身就是一項判斷，而不只是行動的起點。我們可能因為習慣、他人的期待，或某種渴望的結果而追求一項目標。這些都可以構成理由，卻不一定足以作出最後決定。判斷要求我們追問目的有何依據，以及當它與其他需要考慮的事情放在一起時，原有理由是否仍然充分。這樣的探究，不必然導向一條能夠支配所有決定的普遍原則。它更需要我們理解：為什麼某個目的在此時、此地、這些條件下具有重要性，以及它與其他同樣值得關注的事情有何關係。

當不同而且具有正當理由的考量相互競爭時，權衡便更加困難。保障一方利益的選擇，可能限制另一方的利益；眼前獲得的好處，可能帶來日後的後果；對一個人有益的行動，也可能改變其他人的處境。有些選擇之所以艱難，正因為不只一條道路通向值得守護的事物；增加資訊本身，仍無法決定不同關切應具有怎樣的分量。判斷需要區分哪些事情可以妥協、哪些不應輕易放棄；哪些偏好可以重新考慮、哪些承諾使決定具有意義。這些區分無法消除衝突，卻使我們能夠正視衝突，而不必假裝所有重要的事情都能同時得到滿足。

智慧透過經驗與反思，使判斷獲得深度。它不只是堅守某項原則，也不是隨著每一種情況改變立場，而是理解何時需要堅持、何時可以調整，以及其中的理由。經驗可以揭示過去決定的局限，反思則幫助我們區分：究竟是原先的推理有誤，還是情況出現了我們無法控制的變化。隨著時間累積，智慧使判斷懂得分寸，而不必假定所有價值都能放在同一把尺上衡量。智慧不會提供一套解決各種理由衝突的公式，卻能使判斷更加關注具體情境，同時不失去對其所處生活整體的理解。

人工智能能夠以具有實際影響的方式參與這項工作。它可以協助辨認相關考量、研究可能的後果，並使原本容易被忽略的關係進入視野。它也可以依據學習到的規律、明確設定的目標與運行限制，作出或提出決策建議。然而，人工系統作出的決策，取決於問題如何被界定、有哪些資訊可用，以及哪些考量被納入或排除。智能可以擴展判斷所依據的資訊與理由，而這些依據本身，也必須接受判斷：我們需要能夠檢視並質疑建議如何形成。當推理與決策由人和所創造的系統共同參與時，責任並不因此消失。

作出決定時，我們只能依據當下所能理解的事情，而決定的後果仍會超出這種理解。後續經驗可能證實原先的理由，也可能揭示我們忽略了什麼。好的判斷不要求我們預見一切，卻要求我們對後來獲得的認識有所回應。我們可以修正決定，而不必否定當初支持它的所有理由；也可以承擔責任，而不必宣稱自己曾控制每一種結果。

## 2.4 The Boundaries of Intervention｜創造介入生活的邊界

### 英文版

A creation does more than offer a capability that people may decide to use. Once integrated into daily activity, it can arrange when things happen, what becomes visible, and which actions take place without a person initiating each one. Intelligent devices and systems make this especially clear: they can respond to changing conditions and act within spaces people inhabit. When our creations begin to organize parts of daily life, their place in that life becomes a question of judgment.

Some intervention is welcome precisely because it relieves people of unnecessary work. A system may coordinate routine actions or sustain conditions that people need without continually asking for their attention. Yet the same capacity can become intrusive if it decides matters that people have reason to decide for themselves. Human freedom is not measured by how many tasks we perform ourselves, but by how much room remains for us to direct our lives.

Every creation that becomes part of life establishes conditions for its continued use. It may ask people to learn new procedures, monitor unfamiliar failures, or remain available to resolve problems that the system cannot handle. Some demands are foreseeable; others emerge as dependence grows. Whether the intervention is helpful depends partly on how these demands are recognized, shared, and responsibly managed. A creation should not demand a life organized around the complexity it was meant to relieve.

Over time, what people create can change the practices through which they live. New capabilities may become familiar, expectations may adjust, and ways of acting that once required deliberate choice may settle into habit. As these arrangements become ordinary, people may cease to notice that they were once choices. Familiarity can make coordination easier, but it can also conceal whose preferences an arrangement serves. To understand a creation's place in life, we must consider how it shapes expectations and whether the ways of living that develop around it remain open to reconsideration.

A shared system may affect people who never chose it. For example, an automated rule that makes a common space convenient for some may restrict when others can use it, or collect information about people who were not consulted. Their interests cannot be represented solely by the preferences of the person who installed the system. Those affected need intelligible reasons for the arrangement and a practicable way to question, contest, or change it. The power to shape shared life carries an obligation to those who must live with its effects.

The boundary of intervention is not determined by the number of decisions a system makes. Routine actions may be entrusted to it, while a small adjustment can affect someone who had no voice in the arrangement. Its scope of action therefore needs justification, and the people affected need meaningful ways to challenge or change it. The more independently a creation acts, the more clearly the authority delegated to it must be defined. The ability to act within our lives does not, by itself, confer authority to determine how we must live.

### 繁體中文版

創造物所帶來的，不只是等待人們選擇使用的能力。一旦進入日常活動，它還可能改變事情發生的時機、哪些情況會被看見，以及哪些行動不必由人逐一發起。智能裝置與系統尤其如此：它們能回應環境變化，在人們生活的空間中採取行動。當我們的創造物開始參與安排日常生活，它們應在生活中占據什麼位置，也就成為需要判斷的問題。

有些介入之所以受到歡迎，正是因為它減少了人不必要的工作。系統可以協調日常行動，或維持人們需要的條件，而不必持續要求人投入注意力。然而，如果同樣的能力替人決定了原本值得親自選擇的事情，協助也可能變成干預。人的自由，不取決於有多少事情必須親自完成，而在於我們仍有多大的空間決定自己的生活方向。

每一項進入生活的創造，都會形成使其得以持續使用的條件。它可能要求人學習新的操作程序、監督陌生的故障，或隨時準備處理系統無法自行解決的問題。有些要求可以預先了解，另一些則隨著依賴加深而逐漸出現。這種介入是否真正帶來協助，部分取決於這些要求能否被認識、共同承擔並妥善處理。一項原本為人減輕複雜性的創造，不應反過來要求人的生活圍繞它的複雜性運轉。

隨著時間推移，人們所創造的事物，可能改變日常生活的實踐方式。新的能力逐漸變得熟悉，期待隨之調整，原本需要有意識選擇的行動方式，也可能慢慢成為習慣。當這些安排變得平常，人們可能不再察覺它們原本也是可以選擇的。熟悉的做法能讓協作更容易，卻也可能使人忽略它究竟優先照顧了誰的需要。要理解一項創造在生活中的位置，我們必須考察它如何塑造期待，以及圍繞它形成的生活方式，是否仍然容許重新審視。

共同使用的系統，可能影響從未選擇使用它的人。例如，一項自動化規則讓某些人更方便使用公共空間，卻可能限制其他人的使用時間，或蒐集未經徵詢者的資訊。這些人的利益，不能只由安裝系統者的偏好代為決定。受到影響的人需要理解相關安排的理由，也需要有實際可行的途徑提出疑問、異議或要求改變。塑造共同生活的力量，也伴隨著對所有必須承受其影響者的責任。

介入的邊界，並不取決於系統替人作出多少決定。例行工作可以交給系統，但一項細微調整也可能影響無從參與安排的人。因此，系統的行動範圍必須有正當理由，受影響者也需要有實際途徑提出異議或改變安排。創造物越能獨立行動，人所授予的權限就越需要清楚界定。能夠參與我們的生活，並不意味著它因此獲得決定我們必須如何生活的權限。

## 2.5 The Continuing Measure｜持續的尺度

### 英文版

Completion marks the point at which a creation begins to encounter uses its makers cannot fully anticipate. A system may meet its design requirements, yet enter households, workplaces, and shared spaces whose habits are different from those imagined during development. The completion of a creation begins a new relationship between what was intended and what becomes real. Its first success is therefore a beginning of lived experience, not a final verdict on its worth.

As use becomes habitual, the value of a creation may change. Early convenience can develop into dependence; a feature that once attracted attention may come to matter chiefly because it works quietly and reliably. People also find purposes its makers never expected, while discovering costs they did not notice at first. Time reveals dimensions of value that achievement alone cannot show.

Nor can the worth of creation be understood through the experience of its immediate users alone. Keeping an intelligent device or system working may require materials, energy, maintenance, and labor that remain largely invisible to those who benefit from it. The costs can fall on workers, communities, and environments far from the place of use. The lasting value of a creation depends not only on what it provides, but also on what it takes to sustain it. To recognize that dependence is to include the conditions and lives bearing those costs in our judgment.

Continuing judgment does not require perpetual suspicion of everything we have built. People need to be able to trust what supports their lives, and such trust grows through experience, reliability, and the possibility of correction when things change. We can trust what supports our lives without surrendering the freedom to reconsider it. Its lasting value lies partly in allowing people to continue living, choosing, and adapting without having to organize their lives around its complexity.

A creation may remain useful while its original form no longer serves the lives around it. New knowledge can reveal a safer design, changing needs can call for another approach, and the burden of keeping something running can exceed what it provides. Sometimes care calls for improvement; sometimes for restraint, replacement, or an end to what has been built. To care for what we create is also to know when it must change. Making that decision is part of sustaining value, not a denial that the earlier creation mattered.

A creation can change more than what a person is able to do; it can change what others recognize that person is capable of contributing. An assistive communication device may first be judged by how clearly it gives voice to someone who has difficulty speaking. Yet as that person begins to join conversations and take part in decisions once made on their behalf, others may discover how much their own assumptions had limited the life they imagined possible. The value revealed is not simply the efficiency of communication, but the importance of being heard and having a say in a shared life. Human life gives our creations a measure, yet what we create can also change our understanding of that life. Through creation, human beings can come to understand anew what kind of life is worth pursuing.

### 繁體中文版

創造完成之時，也正是它開始面對創造者無法完全預見的用途之時。一套系統可以符合設計要求，卻進入生活習慣與開發時設想不同的家庭、工作場所與公共空間。一項創造的完成，也開啟了原有意圖與後續現實之間新的關係。最初的成功，因此是實際生活經驗的起點，而非價值判斷的終點。

當使用逐漸成為習慣，創造物的價值也可能改變。起初的便利可能逐漸形成依賴；曾經令人驚喜的功能，日後或許主要因為安靜而可靠地運作才顯得重要。人們也會發現創造者未曾預想的用途，以及最初沒有察覺的代價。時間會揭示創造的價值中，單憑最初成就無法看見的部分。

創造的價值，也不能只從直接使用者的經驗來理解。要讓智能裝置或系統持續運作，可能需要材料、能源、維護與勞動，而受益者往往不易看見這些投入。相關代價可能落在遠離使用現場的工作者、社區與環境身上。一項創造的長久價值，不只取決於它能夠提供什麼，也取決於維持它所需要付出什麼。認識這種依存關係，意味著判斷時也要看見承擔代價的條件與生命。

持續判斷並不意味著人必須永遠懷疑自己建立的一切。人需要能夠信賴支持生活的事物，而信賴透過經驗、可靠性，以及情況改變時仍可修正的可能而逐漸形成。我們可以信賴支持生活的事物，同時保有重新審視它們的自由。它長久的價值，也在於讓人能夠持續生活、選擇與適應，而不必圍繞造物自身的複雜性安排生活。

一項創造可能仍然有用，原有形式卻已不再適合周圍的生活。新的知識可能帶來更安全的設計，改變的需要可能要求另一種方法，而維持運作的負擔也可能超過它所提供的益處。有時，妥善對待一項創造意味著改進；有時則意味著節制、替換，或讓已經建成的事物走向終結。真正珍惜我們的創造，也包括懂得它何時需要改變。作出這種決定，是持續維護價值的一部分，並不是否認早先的創造曾具有意義。

一項創造能改變的，不只是某個人可以完成什麼，也包括他人如何認識這個人能夠作出的貢獻。一種輔助溝通裝置，起初可能主要以它能否清楚傳達言語表達困難者的意思來評價。然而，當使用者逐漸參與交談，並對過去由他人代為決定的事情發表意見，周圍的人也可能發現，原先的想像曾如何限制他們對這個人生活可能性的認識。這時顯現的價值，不只是溝通效率，而是共同生活中能夠被傾聽、參與決定的重要性。人的生活為自身創造提供尺度，而創造也可能改變我們對生活的理解。人能夠藉由創造，重新理解什麼樣的生活值得追求。
