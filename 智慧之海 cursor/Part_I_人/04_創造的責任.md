# 第四章——創造的責任

**核心問題：** 對於我們帶入世界的事物，應當承擔什麼責任？

**獨有思想內容：**

- 創造不只是獲得能力，也意味著承擔其可能產生的後果。
- 責任從設計與選擇階段就已開始，不只在出現問題後才成立。
- 創造涉及多方合作時，責任可以分配，但不能因此消失。
- 責任、權限、控制能力與實際影響範圍需要區分。
- 人可能無法控制所有結果，但仍須建立合理的預防、監督與回應方式。
- 創造責任應同時關注當下使用者、其他受影響者與長期後果。

**本章思想成果：** 建立 Responsibility of Creation｜創造責任的基本原則。

**概念邊界：** 不詳細展開智能系統複雜性管理、設備生命週期或環境影響分析。

### 英文版

## Introduction｜引言

Responsibility does not diminish the freedom to create. It allows the power of creation to become worthy of trust, to endure through commitment, and to make new freedom possible.

### 繁體中文版

責任不削弱創造的自由，而是使創造的力量值得信賴，透過承諾得以延續，並讓新的自由仍然可能。

## Chapter Opening｜章首

### 英文版

A creation may accomplish everything it was intended to do and still leave difficult questions behind. Even success cannot tell us everything we need to know about the choices that brought it about.

### 繁體中文版

即使創造的成果達到了預期，仍可能留下尚未回答的問題。事情做成了，不代表當初的每個決定都經得起重新檢視。

## 4.1 Responsibility Begins with Creation｜責任始於創造

### 英文版

A creation begins to affect the world before its consequences can be fully known. What seemed clear in thought must now meet the conditions of reality, and its results may reach beyond the purpose that gave it a beginning. This is why responsibility cannot be left until something goes wrong. It begins when we give an idea the power to make a difference, knowing that our intentions alone cannot answer for everything that follows.

The first responsibility of creation lies in taking our own reasons seriously. A purpose may be sincerely held, and we may have the ability to pursue it, yet neither sincerity nor ability is enough to settle how we should act. We need to consider what our work asks of the world, what it may change, and what we might be overlooking. This inquiry does not stand apart from creation. It belongs to the care with which creation begins.

Careful judgment does not remove uncertainty. To begin anything new is to act without a complete account of what will happen. But uncertainty is not a reason to surrender judgment, nor does confidence excuse us from it. We can learn what is knowable, notice where the greatest risks lie, and leave room to correct a mistake while correction is still possible. Taking responsibility at the beginning means making such care part of the action, not a promise to think about it afterward.

Our reasons must also be examined beyond the limits of our own intentions. A decision may seem sound to those who make it while placing a burden on lives they have not considered. We cannot anticipate every effect, and responsible creation does not require us to pretend otherwise. It does require a willingness to hear what our first judgment left out, and to take those effects seriously when they come to light. The right to begin does not give us the right to stop listening.

We often recognize freedom in the courage to begin. There is courage, too, in stopping, changing course, or admitting that a decision was mistaken. A person who cannot reconsider may be driven more by pride than by freedom. Responsibility protects our ability to choose again, even when doing so costs us something. A mature freedom can pursue a purpose without becoming captive to the first way it tried to achieve it.

### 繁體中文版

當我們把想法付諸實行，往往還無法預知它將帶來什麼。原先想得清楚的事情，一旦進入現實，便會遇到未曾考慮的情況，也可能產生超出預期的影響。因此，責任不能等到出了問題才開始。從決定付諸行動的那一刻起，我們就需要明白：良好的初衷，不足以承擔此後的一切。

創造之初，我們首先要認真想清楚自己為什麼這樣做。即使目的出於真心，也有能力實現，仍不能據此認定做法就一定妥當。我們必須思考：事情會改變什麼，需要付出什麼，又有哪些影響還沒有看見？這些問題不是完成創造之後才需要補上的考慮，而是從一開始就應有的審慎。

再審慎，也無法預知一切。要開始做前所未有的事，本就得在許多情況尚不明朗時作出判斷。然而，不確定不能成為放棄判斷的藉口，自信也不能使風險消失。我們可以盡力了解情況，看清哪些後果最難承受，並趁錯誤仍可補救時留下調整的餘地。從一開始負起責任，就是把這份審慎落實在行動中，而不是等出了問題才回頭思考。

作出決定時，不能只問自己的用意是否良好。眼前看來合理的做法，可能讓原先未曾顧及的人承受代價。我們無法預見所有影響，也不必假裝無所不知。但當被忽略的事情逐漸顯露，就必須願意傾聽，認真回應。擁有開始行動的自由，不代表從此可以對不同的聲音充耳不聞。

我們常從敢於開始的勇氣看見自由。然而，願意停下來、改變方向，或承認判斷失誤，同樣需要勇氣。如果一個人再也無法重新思考，支配他的或許已經不是自由，而是自尊或固執。責任使我們即使需要付出代價，仍保有重新選擇的能力。成熟的自由能夠堅持值得追求的目的，卻不必被最初採取的方法束縛。

## 4.2 The Power of Responsibility｜責任的力量

### 英文版

Being willing to reconsider a decision is one thing. Changing what happens because of it can be much harder. We may recognize a mistake and still lack the means to repair it. Responsibility gains practical force when a change of judgment can become a change in what we do. Without that step, even an honest admission may leave the consequences untouched.

Good intentions cannot carry out a difficult responsibility on their own. Someone must be able to notice a problem, understand why it matters, and do something about it. Where effort is shared, these abilities may rest with different people. One person may recognize a danger while another holds the authority to respond. Responsibility has real strength only when what has been learned can reach those able to act upon it.

Even a worthy purpose may carry a cost. Sometimes that cost cannot be avoided, and refusing every difficult choice would mean refusing to act at all. But necessity is easy to claim when someone else must bear the loss. Before accepting a sacrifice, we have to ask whether it is proportionate to what we hope to achieve, and whether the burden could be reduced or shared more fairly. Responsibility is present in how we face the cost, not merely in how firmly we pursue the goal.

We cannot be responsible for knowing what no reasonable effort could have revealed. But what we failed to foresee may still require a response once it becomes known. A warning can be given, harm can sometimes be repaired, and a practice can be changed to prevent further loss. These duties are not identical to the duty to have predicted the trouble. Responsibility has reasonable limits, yet it does not necessarily end where foresight ends.

A responsibility may be written down, divided among many people, and still remain undone. This happens when everyone can point to a duty but no one can bring about the necessary response. The weakness is not always a lack of concern. It may lie in the distance between knowing, deciding, and acting. The power of responsibility is found in closing that distance. It turns the willingness to answer for something into the ability to do something about it.

### 繁體中文版

願意重新考慮自己的決定，與真正改變它造成的結果，是兩回事。有時我們已經看見錯誤，卻沒有辦法補救。只有讓新的判斷付諸行動，責任才能真正發揮力量。否則，即使坦然認錯，留下的問題也可能絲毫未變。

光有善意，還不足以承擔艱難的責任。有人要能及時發現問題，有人要明白它為何重要，也要有人能夠採取行動。當許多人共同做事，這些能力未必集中在同一人身上。最先察覺危險的人可能無權處理；有權決定的人卻可能尚不知情。只有讓重要的發現傳達給真正能採取行動的人，責任才不會停留在口頭上。

即使目的值得追求，也可能必須付出代價。有些取捨無法避免；如果遇到困難就不敢行動，許多重要的事情也無從開始。但當損失落在別人身上，「沒有辦法」往往說得太容易。我們仍須問：這樣的代價是否值得？能否減少傷害，或讓負擔分配得更公平？負責任，不只要看我們如何堅持目標，也要看我們如何面對為此付出的代價。

即使已經盡了合理的查證與思考，也有些事情無法預見。對這些事，我們不必承擔事先知曉一切的義務；但影響一旦顯現，就不能因此置之不理。該提醒的可以及時提醒，能補救的設法補救，原有做法也可以改變，以免損失繼續擴大。事後的回應與事前的預見是兩種不同的責任。責任有其界限，卻不會因為當初無法預知，就必然到此為止。

責任即使已經明確記錄，也分配給許多人，仍可能無人真正履行。當人人都能指出某項職責，卻沒有人能促成必要的回應，便會出現這種情況。問題未必出在缺乏責任感，也可能出在認識問題、作出決定與採取行動之間存在太大的距離。責任的力量，就在於縮短這段距離，使願意承擔不只停留在態度，而能成為真正解決問題的能力。

## 4.3 The Foundations of Trust｜信賴的基礎

### 英文版

An act of responsibility may resolve a problem, but trust cannot rest on that act alone. We need to know whether the same care will be present when the next difficulty comes, including when no one is watching. That confidence cannot be demanded, and it is not secured by a promise. It grows when ability is shown in ordinary work, limits are spoken of honestly, and questions receive answers. Trust begins where there are reasons to expect responsibility to be carried through.

Good intentions matter because trust concerns how power will be used. They cannot take the place of competence. Skill matters because promises have to be fulfilled in the real world, but skill without honesty can mislead just as effectively as it can help. People need both a reason to believe that something can be done and a reason to believe they are being told the truth about it. Neither can substitute for the other.

Complete understanding is rarely possible, and trust does not require it. What matters is whether we can distinguish what is established from what is still uncertain, and whether assurances can be checked when the facts change. A clear explanation helps, but words remain only words until experience can test them. The willingness to admit what is not yet known may give us more reason for confidence than a promise that leaves no room for doubt.

Trust is formed through ordinary conduct long before a serious difficulty appears. Promises repeatedly kept, problems acknowledged, and small errors corrected all give people grounds for confidence. When a larger failure comes, the response matters greatly, but it cannot erase what happened. An apology without repair is incomplete; a repair without an honest account may leave doubt behind. Trust is maintained over time by what people do, especially when doing the right thing becomes costly.

Trust becomes fragile when those who rely on it are afraid to ask questions. The silence of a dependent person may look like confidence, even when it is only the absence of a safe way to disagree. A trustworthy relationship makes room for doubt, challenge, and the possibility that confidence will be withdrawn. This is not a weakness in trust. It is what prevents trust from becoming obedience and allows it to remain a judgment freely made.

### 繁體中文版

眼前的問題得到妥善處理，固然讓人放心，卻還不足以建立長久的信賴。我們更在意的是：下次遇到困難，對方是否仍會認真面對？即使沒有人在旁察看，是否依然盡責？信心不能靠要求取得，也不能單憑承諾維持。平日做事的能力、對自身界限的坦誠，以及面對疑問時願意回應的態度，才能逐漸讓信賴有了根據。

我們希望所依靠的人懷有善意，因為能力如何被運用，與能力本身同樣重要。但光有善意，未必能把事情做好；有能力卻不誠實，也可能使人受到誤導。要真正放心，我們既得知道對方做得到，也得相信對方願意說實話。兩者缺一不可。

沒有人能把所有事情了解得毫無遺漏，信賴也不必建立在全知之上。重要的是，哪些已有根據、哪些仍不確定，能不能說清楚；原先作出的保證，遇到不同的事實時，又能不能接受檢驗。話說得再周全，終究還是要看事實。有時，坦然承認尚未弄清楚的地方，比毫無保留的保證更能使人安心。

信賴是在平日一點一滴建立的。答應的事一次次做到，出現問題願意承認，小錯也及時改正，人們才逐漸有理由放心。等到較大的失誤發生，如何回應固然重要，卻不能抹去已造成的傷害。只有道歉而不補救，並未真正承擔；只補救卻不說清楚，也難免留下疑慮。能否守住信賴，要看長久以來的行動，尤其是盡責需要付出代價的時候。

當依賴的一方不敢提出問題，信賴便開始變得脆弱。沉默看似放心，有時只是因為沒有安全表達異議的途徑。值得信賴的關係，應容得下疑問、質疑，甚至信心的撤回。這不是信賴的弱點，而是使信賴不至於變成服從，也讓信賴始終出於自由判斷的必要條件。

## 4.4 Commitment and Order｜承諾與秩序

### 英文版

Trust grows from what has been done, yet much of what we depend on still lies ahead. We need to know that a responsibility will not be forgotten simply because circumstances change or keeping it becomes inconvenient. This is what a commitment adds to reliable conduct: it binds a present undertaking to work that remains to be done. The first willingness to act may fade. A commitment matters because the responsibility need not fade with it.

A sincere promise is only a beginning. Keeping it may require preparation, patience, and the ability to work with circumstances that were not foreseen. Sometimes the original way of fulfilling it becomes impossible or harmful. Changing course can then be the faithful choice, provided the reason is made clear and the burden is not quietly passed elsewhere. Keeping one's word means remaining faithful to what was promised, not blindly repeating the first plan.

An individual promise can give one relationship a future. Shared life asks for something more: expectations that people can understand even when they have never met or made a promise to one another. Rules help provide that continuity. But once a rule asks something of people who did not choose it, its authority needs an explanation. The ability to keep a common order depends not only on knowing what the rules say, but on understanding why their demands deserve respect.

An order may work smoothly for years and still be unjust. The ease it brings to some may rest on burdens that others have little power to refuse. Such burdens cannot be justified simply by pointing to how long the rules have been followed. They deserve to be heard and weighed against the purposes those rules are meant to serve. An order becomes more trustworthy when it can face that examination without treating every challenge as a threat.

Rules are made to help people keep faith with purposes that matter. Over time, following a rule can become easier than remembering why it was established. When the two come apart, obedience may preserve the appearance of order while allowing its purpose to fail. The responsibility to uphold an order then includes the courage to question and, when justified, change it. What deserves to last is not every rule unchanged, but our ability to honor the commitments that made rules necessary.

### 繁體中文版

我們因為過去的行動而逐漸信任彼此，但真正需要依靠的事情，往往還在未來。情況變了，履行變得困難了，原先答應承擔的責任還算不算數？承諾的意義，就在於讓今天的決定延續到明天尚未完成的事情。當初的熱情可能消退，但熱情退去，不該使應盡的責任也隨之消失。

答應下來，只是開始。真正守住承諾，還需要準備、耐心，也需要面對意外變化的能力。有時原先計畫的做法已經不可行，甚至可能造成傷害。這時改變方式，反而更能守住承諾的本意。但理由必須說清楚，不能把代價悄悄轉嫁給別人。守信不是一成不變地照著原來的計畫做，而是不忘自己究竟答應承擔什麼。

人與人之間可以藉由承諾彼此依靠，共同生活卻不能只靠彼此熟識。即使素不相識，我們仍需要知道可以期待什麼，又有哪些責任不容推卸。規範因此成為共同生活的一部分。但當規則也約束那些沒有參與制定的人，就不能只要求他們遵守，還必須說得清楚，為什麼有理由這樣要求。要讓秩序值得維護，人們不只需要知道規則，也需要理解規則為何合理。

秩序即使多年運作順暢，也可能並不公正。有些人得到便利，代價卻由另一些難以拒絕的人承受。規則沿用再久，也不足以證明這樣的負擔合理。那些承受影響的人應當有機會說話，規則所維護的目的也應接受檢視。能夠面對質疑，而不把不同意見一概視為威脅，這樣的秩序才更值得信賴。

規範原本是為了幫助人們維護值得重視的目的。時間久了，遵守規則有時比記得規則為何存在還要容易。當做法與目的逐漸脫節，表面上人人守規矩，真正要維護的事情卻可能已經落空。維護秩序的責任，因此也包括在理由充分時提出質疑、作出改變的勇氣。值得延續的，不是每條規則永遠不變，而是人們仍有能力守住使規則成為必要的承諾。

## 4.5 Responsible Creation｜負責任的創造

### 英文版

Every worthwhile achievement deserves to be taken seriously. Yet success can create a temptation of its own: to treat what has worked as the answer that should continue to prevail. The more effort invested in a creation, the harder it may become to question it. But the freedom that allowed us to begin was never a promise that the first result would be the last word. Responsibility asks us to care for an achievement without making it immune to reconsideration.

To care for an achievement is not necessarily to preserve it unchanged. Some things deserve patient maintenance; others need to be altered when the reasons for keeping them have changed. At times, an ending is the most responsible choice. The difficulty is that neither attachment nor the desire for novelty can decide this for us. We have to look again at what the work still makes possible, and what it may now prevent.

The freedom to create again requires more than permission. A person may be told that change is allowed yet lack the knowledge, opportunity, or means to make a different choice. An achievement becomes less confining when its workings can be understood and its purposes can be questioned. Responsibility for what we create therefore includes leaving the conditions for fresh judgment intact. The ability to begin again is not preserved by words alone.

Some decisions destroy possibilities that no later change of mind can recover. A loss may be too great to repair, a resource too exhausted to restore, or a living relation too damaged to renew. In such cases, freedom is not preserved simply by allowing a future choice. The conditions that would make that choice meaningful may already be gone. Responsibility has to care for what makes future freedom possible, not only for the freedom to make decisions today.

There comes a time when responsibility asks us to loosen our hold on what we have made. This does not mean walking away from obligations that remain, or pretending that everything that follows will be good. We owe what we have begun enough care that it can be understood, questioned, and taken further without depending forever on our direction. Then something remarkable becomes possible: a creation can fulfill its own purpose and still leave room for purposes its beginning could not have imagined. The freedom that brought it into being need not be the last freedom it makes possible.

### 繁體中文版

真正有價值的成果，值得珍惜。然而，成功也可能使人以為，過去行得通的辦法，往後就該一直沿用。投入的心力越多，重新檢視成果也越不容易。但當初讓我們開始創造的自由，並沒有保證最早得到的答案就是最後的答案。珍惜已經完成的事，仍要容許它受到新的檢驗。

珍惜成果，未必就要讓它維持原樣。有些事情值得耐心維護，有些則需要隨著情況改變而調整；有時，讓它結束才是負責任的選擇。難的是分辨何時該堅持、何時該放手。無論捨不得過去，還是一心追求新奇，都不能替我們作判斷。重要的是重新看清：眼前的成果還能帶來什麼，又可能妨礙什麼。

要重新創造，光有改變的許可還不夠。即使有人說「你可以選擇」，若缺乏知識、機會或必要的能力，仍未必走得出另一條路。當我們能了解既有成果如何運作，也能追問它為何如此安排，就比較有機會改變它，而不至於受其束縛。對創造負責，也要讓重新判斷所需要的條件保留下來。自由不能只停留在口頭上的允許。

有些決定會斷送日後再也找不回來的可能。損失可能無法彌補，資源耗盡後未必能再生，受傷害的生命關係也可能無從修復。到了那時，即使仍說人可以自由選擇，使選擇真正有意義的條件，或許早已不在。責任因此不只關乎我們今天能決定什麼，也關乎未來的人是否仍有選擇的餘地。

有時，真正負責任，反而需要鬆開對自己創造成果的掌握。這不是丟下尚未履行的義務，也不是認定往後的改變一定更好。我們仍應盡心照料，使留下的成果能被理解、接受質疑，也能不再依賴我們的安排而繼續發展。如此一來，即使當初的目的已經達成，新的追求仍可能從中生長。使它誕生的那份自由，不必是它所能帶來的最後一份自由。
