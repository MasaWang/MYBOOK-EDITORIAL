# 第三章——超越我們的意圖

**核心問題：** 為什麼我們的創造所產生的後果可能超越原始意圖？

**獨有思想內容：**

- 創造意圖與實際結果並不必然一致。
- 創造物進入世界後，可能被他人重新使用或賦予新用途。
- 不同事物、系統與社會活動的結合可能產生新的效果。
- 創造可能帶來未曾預見的利益，也可能形成風險。
- 一項創造的影響可能超越創造者的直接控制。
- 技術結果應在具體使用和世界關係中理解，而不只依據設計意圖。

**本章思想成果：** 建立 Intention–Consequence Distinction｜意圖與後果的區分。

**概念邊界：** 研究意圖與結果為何分離，而不是全面討論誰應承擔責任。

---

### 英文版

**We create with intentions, but what we create enters a world that does not belong to our intentions.**

The world does not begin where our intentions begin, nor does it end where they end. It has its own histories, relationships, and patterns of change, within which human creation takes place. We may shape what we make, but we do not determine everything it will encounter or become. Creation is therefore an act within the world, not an act over the world as a whole.

What we create becomes part of the world it enters. Its existence introduces new relationships while being shaped by those already present; its consequences unfold through changes that no single creator can wholly direct. Intention remains essential to understanding why something was made, but it cannot explain everything that follows from its existence. To understand our creations more fully, we must also understand the continuing world of which they have become a part.

### 繁體中文版

**我們帶著意圖創造，而我們的創造，終將進入一個不由我們的意圖所支配的世界。**

世界不因我們的意圖而開始，也不隨意圖的終結而停止。世界有自身的歷史、關係與變化，而人的創造就在其中發生。我們可以塑造自己的造物，卻無法決定它將遇見什麼，或最終成為什麼。創造因此是在世界之中進行的行動，而不是對整個世界的支配。

我們的造物成為所進入世界的一部分。它的存在帶來新的關係，也受到既有關係的塑造；它的後果在持續變化中展開，而任何單一創造者都無法完全主導這些變化。意圖仍是理解一項創造為何誕生的重要依據，卻不足以解釋其存在之後發生的一切。若要更完整地理解自己的創造，我們也必須理解造物已經參與其中的持續世界。

## 3.1 The Intention to Create｜創造的意圖

### 英文版

A wish or need may give someone a reason to create, but it does not yet determine what will be made. People must decide what they hope to accomplish and how to begin. These decisions turn an intention toward the work of creation. Intention gives creation direction; action begins to give intention form.

To turn an intention into action, people must work within the conditions of the physical world. Materials have their properties, tools have their limits, and requirements cannot always be met together. Choosing one means of realization can close off another, even when both appear desirable. What we can create depends not only on what we intend, but also on the conditions in which we act.

A purpose gives people a reason to create; design goals specify what they will try to achieve. Such goals may be clear and measurable, making it possible to examine the result. Yet a creation can meet its specifications without fully accomplishing the purpose that motivated it. What can be specified in a design does not always encompass what people set out to achieve.

People create according to their understanding of the world. They anticipate how things will be used, what others know, and which circumstances are likely to occur. Some expectations are explicit, while others remain assumptions shaped by particular experiences and ways of life. An assumption that serves well in one setting may fail to describe another. Every creation draws upon an understanding of the world that is useful but necessarily incomplete.

The intention behind a creation need not belong to one person. Work done together brings different knowledge, practical concerns, and expectations into the same undertaking. People may agree on a purpose while interpreting it differently, and their decisions may reflect both agreement and compromise. A coherent creation need not arise from a single, complete intention.

Intention helps explain why a creation takes its form and what its makers expected it to do. Yet the work of making gives rise to an object or system with properties that can be examined independently of those expectations. Its structure and capacities belong to what now exists, not only to what someone once hoped for. Design gives intention form, but what takes form is more than intention.

### 繁體中文版

人的意圖為創造提供方向，使人們希望實現的事情與實際作出的決定產生聯繫。需要與願望可以使人萌生創造的念頭，卻仍須透過選擇與行動進入創造過程。意圖使人知道自己希望走向何處，行動則開始使這個方向取得具體形式。

要將意圖付諸行動，人必須面對物理世界的條件。材料有自身的性質，工具有其限制，不同要求也未必能同時滿足。即使兩條路徑都值得考慮，選擇其中之一，也可能使另一條無法實現。我們能夠創造什麼，不只取決於意圖，也取決於行動所面對的現實條件。

目的使人有理由開始創造，設計目標則明確界定準備實現什麼。目標可以清晰、可衡量，使人得以檢查成果。然而，造物即使符合既定規格，也可能沒有充分實現最初的目的。設計能明確規定的內容，未必涵蓋人們原本希望達成的一切。

人依據自己對世界的理解進行創造，預想造物將如何被使用、他人知道什麼，以及可能遇到哪些情況。有些期待被明確寫入設計，另一些則隱含在特定經驗與生活方式所形成的預設之中。在某種處境下合理的預設，到了另一種處境卻未必適用。因此，創造總依靠某種具有實際作用、卻無法完整涵蓋世界的理解。

創造背後的意圖未必只屬於某個人。共同工作把不同的知識、實際考量與期待帶進同一個過程。人們可能同意追求共同目的，卻對它有不同理解；最後的決定也可能同時包含共識與妥協。因此，設計即使形成一致的整體，也不代表它出自單一而完整的意圖。

意圖有助於說明造物為何具有現在的形式，以及創造者曾經期待它完成什麼。然而，製作完成的事物或系統具有自身的性質，不能只憑原先的期待來認識。它的結構與能力已成為世界中可以觀察、使用和檢驗的事實。設計使意圖取得形式，而取得形式的事物，已不只是意圖。

## 3.2 A Creation in a Changing World｜變化世界中的創造物

### 英文版

A creation may be completed, but its existence in the world has only begun. It enters places, activities, and relationships that were already changing before it arrived. The world continues through change. Its things, lives, and relationships do not remain the same, yet their changing histories form the world in which we live and create.

People come to understand created things through experience. Knowing their intended function does not tell us everything about how they will work in everyday life. Repeated use reveals strengths and limitations, the attention required, and the conditions under which people can rely on them. We know what a creation was designed to do before we know all that living with it will teach us.

Materials wear, parts fail, and the conditions of continued use require attention. Care, repair, and adjustment can become necessary long after the initial work is complete. This labor is often less visible than making something new, though the creation's usefulness may depend on it. Making something is one achievement; sustaining it through change is another.

Time also changes the world around what has been made. People's needs shift, other technologies appear, and arrangements once taken for granted disappear. Something that once fitted naturally into daily life may become difficult to use or acquire a different role. A creation need not change for its place in the world to change.

The people who make something may not remain part of its history for as long as it endures. Later generations encounter creations whose makers they never knew and whose original circumstances have passed. They inherit completed works and the earlier decisions that shaped them. Those decisions become conditions that later people must interpret and live with.

The history of a creation is not contained in the thing alone. It takes shape through the experience of those who live with it, the work required to sustain it, and the changing circumstances in which it remains present. Even when its material form endures, the relationships through which it matters may be transformed. To understand its history is to understand what has persisted and how its presence has continued.

### 繁體中文版

造物可以完成，而它在世界中的歷程才剛開始。它進入早已存在的場所、活動與關係，而這一切在造物到來之前便持續變化。世界在變化中持續。事物、生命與關係不會永遠保持原樣，而它們不斷變化的歷程，共同構成我們生活與創造的世界。

人們透過經驗逐漸認識造物。知道預定功能，不等於已經知道它在日常生活中如何發揮作用。反覆使用使人了解它的長處與限制、需要投入多少注意力，以及在什麼條件下可以依賴它。我們可以先知道造物被設計來做什麼，卻未必已經知道與它共同生活將使我們認識什麼。

材料會磨損，部件可能失效，持續使用所需要的條件也必須受到照顧。創造完成後，維護、修復與調整仍可能長期不可或缺。這些工作未必像製作新事物那樣顯眼，卻可能決定造物能否繼續發揮作用。創造出事物是一種成就；使它在變化中持續發揮作用，則是另一種成就。

時間也在改變造物周圍的世界。人的需要會轉變，其他技術陸續出現，原本理所當然的安排也可能消失。曾經自然融入生活的事物，日後可能難以使用，也可能承擔不同的作用。即使造物本身維持原狀，它在世界中的位置仍可能改變。

造物可能比創造者存續得更久。後來的人接觸到前人留下的事物，卻未必認識創造者，也未必經歷過當時的條件。他們承繼的不只是完成的成果，還包括那些早已作出、卻仍影響今日生活的選擇。如何理解和延續這些成果，於是成為後來世代必須面對的事情。

造物的歷程並不只存在於事物本身，也形成於人們與它共同生活的經驗、維持它的工作，以及周圍持續變化的條件。即使物質形式得以保存，使它具有作用的關係也可能逐漸改變。因此，理解造物的歷程，既要認識哪些事物延續了下來，也要理解它如何透過不同關係持續存在。

## 3.3 Unexpected Uses and Discoveries｜未曾預見的用途與發現

### 英文版

We tend to understand familiar creations through the purposes we already know. These purposes help us use them, but they can also narrow what we think to examine. When we ask a different question, properties once overlooked may become significant. Discovery sometimes begins not with something new, but with a different way of attending to what is already there.

A new use often begins with a different human need. People may approach a familiar creation with purposes its makers did not share or could not foresee. An existing capability may serve a task for which it was never specifically designed. The capability may remain unchanged while the purpose it serves becomes different.

Things created for separate purposes may share qualities or complement one another. Recognizing such connections allows people to explore ways of working together that no original plan anticipated. Simply combining things, however, does not ensure a useful result; their different properties must contribute to a common activity. What creations can do together may not be apparent when each is considered alone.

People do not always know the full range of what their creations can do. Some properties become apparent only under conditions that have not previously been examined. Observation, experience, and deliberate experiments may reveal capabilities that were present all along. Discovery can change what we know about a creation without changing the creation itself.

Recognizing a possible use is not the same as making it work. A new application may require testing, adjustments, or changes in the tools and knowledge people use. What first appears promising may prove impractical, while another approach may become dependable through further work. Recognizing a new use is a beginning; realizing it requires another kind of understanding.

A discovery made through one person's experience may remain uncertain until others can examine it. Describing the conditions under which a property appears allows people to test whether the same result can be found again. Some findings withstand further examination, while others prove dependent on circumstances first overlooked. What begins as an observation becomes knowledge through the work of understanding its conditions and limits.

### 繁體中文版

我們習慣透過熟悉的用途理解身邊的造物。這些用途有助於使用，卻也可能限制我們願意進一步觀察什麼。當我們提出不同的問題，原先被忽略的性質便可能顯現出重要性。有時，發現並不始於新事物的出現，而是我們開始以不同的方式認識早已存在的事物。

新的用途往往始於不同的需要。人們可能帶著創造者不曾具有、也未曾預見的目的接觸既有造物。原本的能力可以被用來完成另一種工作，即使造物並未為此特別設計。能力可以保持不變，而它所服務的目的卻可能不同。

為不同目的而創造的事物，可能具有相近的特性，也可能彼此互補。人們認識這些關聯之後，便能探索原先未曾設想的協作方式。然而，將事物放在一起並不保證產生有用的成果，還需要讓它們不同的性質共同參與實際活動。單獨觀察各個造物時，我們未必能看見它們共同作用所能實現的事情。

人未必完全了解自己造出的事物具有哪些能力。有些性質只有在先前未曾檢驗的條件下才會顯現。觀察、經驗與實驗，可能揭示早已存在、卻尚未被認識的能力。發現可以改變我們對造物的認識，而不必改變造物本身。

發現新的用途，不等於已經能讓它真正發揮作用。新的使用方式可能需要驗證、調整，也可能要求人們改變工具與知識。某些看似可行的想法最後未必適用，另一些則能透過持續探索變得可靠。發現新的用途只是開始；使它真正實現，還需要另一層理解。

個人的經驗可能帶來發現，卻不一定足以使這項發現獲得可靠的理解。當人們能夠說明某種性質在什麼條件下出現，他人便有機會加以檢驗，確認結果能否再次發生。有些發現經得起反覆檢驗，另一些則受到最初未曾察覺的條件限制。觀察要成為可靠的知識，仍需要認識使它成立的條件與界限。

## 3.4 Consequences Beyond Control｜超越控制的後果

### 英文版

When people create something, they seek to give it dependable form and function. Design establishes how it should operate, which tasks it should perform, and where its limits lie. Such control matters because people need to know what they can rely upon. Yet we may control how a creation works without controlling everything that happens because it works.

A creation can perform as intended and still bring changes its makers never anticipated. A machine that eases work may alter how work is organized; a dependable tool may change the skills people continue to practice. Their success can itself alter the work and relationships around them. A creation need not fail to produce consequences its makers never intended.

As people begin to rely on a creation, they adjust their activities and expectations around it. Others may plan their work on the assumption that it will remain available, and further arrangements can grow from that expectation. The effects of the creation now depend partly on these arrangements, not just on its operation. What begins as something people use can become something their activities depend upon.

Over time, consequences can become less visible even as their influence grows. Habits formed around a creation may become so familiar that people stop noticing how much has changed. Arrangements once made for convenience can grow difficult to alter, and earlier choices may begin to constrain later ones. Some consequences become harder to notice precisely because they have become part of ordinary life.

We can adjust a machine, limit its functions, or stop it altogether. These forms of control are real, yet they may leave untouched the habits and dependencies that have grown around the machine. Changing an object does not necessarily restore the conditions that existed before people came to rely upon it. Control over what we make is not the same as control over what our creations bring about.

Recognizing these limits does not leave us powerless. We can still alter what we have made, while also examining the needs and arrangements that now depend upon it. Sometimes an effective response requires changes in how people work together rather than in the creation alone. When consequences extend beyond the creation itself, responding to them requires more than changing the creation.

### 繁體中文版

人們創造事物，往往希望它具有可以依賴的形式與功能。設計規定造物如何運作、完成哪些工作，以及應有什麼限制。這種控制十分重要，因為人需要知道自己能夠依靠什麼。然而，我們或許能控制造物如何運作，卻未必能控制它運作之後發生的一切。

造物即使完全按照預期運作，也可能帶來創造者未曾想到的改變。減輕勞動的機器可能改變工作的組織方式，可靠的工具也可能改變人們日常運用的技能。這些影響不一定來自缺陷或故障。造物不必發生故障，也可能產生創造者從未預期的後果。

當人們開始依賴造物，便會圍繞它調整活動與期待。其他人可能以它將持續可用為前提安排工作，而更多新的安排又由此形成。這時，造物的影響不只取決於它如何運作，也取決於人們如何依照它的存在組織活動。原本供人使用的事物，可能逐漸成為各種活動賴以進行的條件。

隨著時間推移，某些後果的影響逐漸加深，卻反而不再顯眼。圍繞造物形成的習慣可能變得十分熟悉，使人不再察覺生活曾經發生哪些改變。最初為了便利而建立的安排，也可能越來越難調整，讓過去的選擇限制了後來的行動。某些後果之所以難以察覺，正因為它們已成為日常生活的一部分。

我們可以調整機器、限制功能，甚至使它停止運作。這些控制確實有效，卻未必能消除圍繞機器形成的習慣與依賴。改變造物本身，也不一定能恢復人們依賴它之前的生活條件。控制我們所造之物，並不等於控制這些造物所帶來的一切。

認識控制的界限，不意味著人無能為力。我們仍然可以調整造物，也可以檢查人們如今依賴哪些安排、面對哪些需要。有時，有效的回應必須改變合作方式，而不只是修改造物的功能。當後果超出造物本身，回應這些後果，便不能只著眼於改變造物。

## 3.5 Reconsidering Our Intentions｜重新審視我們的意圖

### 英文版

The completion of a creation does not settle the choices that brought it about. Its later place in people's lives may give those earlier decisions a significance that was not apparent when the work began. What once seemed reason enough to begin may need examination as a reason to continue, change course, or stop. This does not erase the original intention; it makes that intention open to renewed judgment.

Intention tells us what people hoped to achieve and why their choices seemed reasonable at the time. These reasons remain part of a creation's history even when later experience changes how we regard them. Later evidence may call those choices into question without making their historical reasons disappear. Understanding why a choice was made is different from judging whether it was justified.

Experience can reveal more than a difference between intention and result. It may expose needs or conditions that the original purpose left out of view, or show that solving one difficulty creates another. Such discoveries can change how people understand the problem they first tried to address. Experience may change not only the answers we find, but the questions we learn to ask.

What we learn does not always challenge the same part of our thinking. Sometimes the purpose remains sound but the means prove inadequate; sometimes the conditions we assumed turn out to be different. Harder questions arise when experience leads us to doubt the purpose itself. Improving a design, correcting an assumption, and reconsidering a purpose are not the same task.

Knowing what has happened does not settle what ought to be done. Consequences may be carefully understood while competing reasons still point toward different choices. Evidence can clarify what is at stake and whose lives are affected, but it cannot decide which course people should take. Knowledge can inform our choices, but it cannot make those choices for us.

We create with the understanding available to us, knowing that the world will not remain as we first encountered it. What follows may reveal limits in our knowledge, consequences we failed to anticipate, or reasons to question the purposes we once pursued. We cannot return to the beginning and make those first choices with everything we have since learned. But we can allow what we learn to change the choices that are still ours to make.

### 繁體中文版

造物完成，並不表示促成它的選擇從此無須再受檢視。造物後來在人們生活中的作用，可能使當初的決定具有原先未曾察覺的意義。曾經足以支持開始創造的理由，日後也可能需要重新檢驗，才能決定是否繼續、改變方向或停止。這不是抹去最初的意圖，而是讓意圖在新的處境中重新接受判斷。

意圖使我們知道人們當初希望實現什麼，以及為何認為那些選擇合理。即使後來的經驗改變了我們的看法，最初的理由仍屬於造物的歷程。後來出現的證據可能使當初的選擇受到質疑，卻不會抹去這些選擇形成時的理由。理解選擇為何作出，與判斷它是否有充分理由，是兩回事。

經驗揭示的，不只是預期與結果之間的差異。它可能讓最初目的未曾考慮的需要與條件顯現出來，也可能使人發現，解決某個困難卻帶來了另一個困難。這些發現會改變我們對原先問題的理解。經驗可能改變的，不只是我們找到的答案，也包括我們學會提出的問題。

後來的認識，未必總是對原先思考的同一部分提出挑戰。有時目的仍然合理，只是手段不足；有時則是我們對現實條件的預設出了差錯。更困難的情況，是經驗使我們開始懷疑原先追求的目的。改進設計、修正預設與重新思考目的，是三種不同的工作。

知道發生了什麼，並不等於知道接下來應當如何行動。即使已經清楚了解後果，不同的理由仍可能指向不同的選擇。證據可以幫助我們認識其中的利害關係，以及哪些人的生活受到影響，卻不能替人決定應走哪條路。知識能為選擇提供依據，卻不能代替我們作出選擇。

我們依據當時所能掌握的認識開始創造，而世界不會始終保持最初的模樣。此後的發展，可能揭示知識的局限、未曾預見的後果，也可能使我們重新思考當初追求的目的。我們無法回到創造開始之前，帶著後來獲得的全部認識重新作出最初的選擇。但我們能夠讓新的認識，改變那些仍由自己作出的選擇。
